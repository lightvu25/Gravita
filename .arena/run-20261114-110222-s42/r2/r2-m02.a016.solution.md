# Gravita — Kinetic Lab Game Feel Upgrade Blueprint

> **Codebase Calibration Note (Current Truth vs. Design Targets)**
> 1. **Speed & Velocity Tiers**: In `Assets/Scenes/GameScene.unity`, `PlayerMovement.moveSpeed` is `30` units/sec (`cellSize = 1`), meaning Subject G-0 traverses **1 tile every `33.3 ms`** (and **`48 u/s`** during `StepPhaseLaunch` at `1.6x`). In `VelocityTier.cs` (and `docs/gdd.md`), `VelocityTierUtil.FromTiles(int tilesTraveled)` maps `0` → `None`, `1–2` → `Drift`, `3–4` → `Cruise`, and `5+` → `KineticRam` (reaching max tier in **`167 ms`**), whereas the high-level spec describes `1–3 / 4–7 / 8+`. Every timing below is calibrated to the live `33.3 ms/tile` (`30 u/s`) loop and scales automatically if `FromTiles` is tuned later.
> 2. **Economy & Revive Flags**: `Assets/Resources/EconomyBalanceConfig.asset` sets `enableLegacyExtraRewardSystems: 0` (suppressing `CloseCallDetector`'s `bonusCoins: 5`), `enableMidGameplayRevive: 0` (`LevelFlowController.CanRevive` is always `false` and `OnPlayerRevived` never fires), and `grantWalletCoinsFromInLevelPickups: 0` (`EconomyManager.OnCoinsChanged` does not increment per in-level coin pickup). Near-misses must therefore pay off through immediate audiovisual and post-FX surge, coin streak HUD pulses must hook `Collectible.OnAnyCoinCollected`, and respawn polish must hook active restart/respawn paths rather than revive.
> 3. **Camera Pixel-Perfect Lock**: `Assets/Scenes/GameScene.unity` currently enables both `CinemachinePixelPerfect` (`m_Enabled: 1`) on `CinemachineCamera` and URP `PixelPerfectCamera` (`m_Enabled: 1`, `m_AssetsPPU: 32`, `320x180`) on `Main Camera`. Every dynamic tier zoom and lens pulse requires disabling **both** overrides.

---

## Target End-State Experience

When a player clears a chamber after these upgrades:
1. Every `33.3 ms` tile crosses on an ascending harmonic synth tick; crossing 5 tiles kicks the camera out smoothly (`5.50 → 5.72 → 6.02`) and trails crisp afterimages without ever dropping a frame during hit-stop.
2. Swiping a perpendicular corner turn while still sliding lights a subtle directional "Input Coil" chevron on Subject G-0 (never falsely triggering on idle starts, wall bumps, or instant reversals) and whips cleanly through chained `DeflectionWedge` bounces with bisector sparks and ascending pitch.
3. Crashing into a wall punches the camera *along the slide vector*, pins Subject G-0 flush against the collision face during unscaled hit-stop while particles and ribbons keep animating, plays the exact impact tier (never stale `LastImpactTier`), and fires unmistakable `MediumImpact` / `RigidImpact` haptics—while `BrakeTile` and `GravityBlock` give a soft magnetic settle with zero wall slam.
4. Threading past an arming `PulseLaser` or `SentinelDronePatrol` flashes an unscaled URP `Overclock` burst (`Bloom`, `ChromaticAberration`, `Vignette`, `-0.28` lens snap, pooled callout), and hazard deaths snap cleanly to the Result modal in `~0.60 s`.

## 1. Core Game Feel Diagnosis: Why Gravita Currently Feels "Flat"

Gravita's deterministic stop-to-stop physics, velocity tiers, squash/stretch, trails, shake, and hit-stop are cleanly separated—but six concrete implementation leaks cancel out speed and impact weight in live play:

1. **Pixel-Perfect Camera Lock & Unscaled Hit-Stop Conflicts**
   - `CinemachineCameraZoom2D` currently has **zero callers** anywhere in code and uses scaled `Time.deltaTime`. Even when called, `CinemachinePixelPerfect.PostPipelineStageCallback` overwrites `state.Lens.OrthographicSize` to integer `PPU` steps (locking `1920x1080` and `1280x720` to `5.625` across `4.82–6.75` and `4.50–7.50`), and disabling *only* `CinemachinePixelPerfect` leaves `PixelPerfectCamera.m_CinemachineCompatibilityMode == false` so `PixelPerfectCamera.OnBeginCameraRendering` forces `m_Camera.orthographicSize` down to `2.8125` at `1080p`.
   - `PlayerImpactFeedback.PulseHitStop` pauses gameplay via `Time.timeScale = 0f` for `33 ms` (`Cruise`) and `83 ms` (`KineticRam`), yet `PlayerLineTrail.LateUpdate()` returns early on `Time.deltaTime <= 0f`, `ReturnToPool` uses scaled `WaitForSeconds`, `PooledParticlePlayer` tracks `Time.deltaTime`, existing particle prefabs (`WallImpactBurst.prefab`, `WallImpactBreak.prefab`, `Particle Star.prefab`) serialize `useUnscaledTime: 0`, and `PlayerImpactFeedback.OnDisable()` stops `hitStopRoutine` without restoring `Time.timeScale`.
2. **Pivot Offset Mismatch, False Brake/Gravity Slams & Stale/Sub-Tile Impact Reads**
   - `Player/Sprite` in `GameScene.unity` sits at `localPosition (0, -0.5, 0)` with bottom-center `(0.5, 0)` pivot and `0.37 x 0.36` world bounds. `PlayerSquashStretch.Play()` applies only raw `pressDirection * (wallPressPx / 32f)` without compensating for sprite height or pivot, so upward ceiling hits compress *away* from the top wall face.
   - `PlayerImpactFeedback.HandleStopped()` listens to `OnMoveStopped` **without checking `movement.WasLastStopWallImpact`**, causing cushioned stops on `BrakeTile` and `GravityBlock` to trigger wall-impact shake and hit-stop.
   - `PlayerMovement` invokes `OnWallImpact` *before* `StopMoving(wallImpact: true)` writes `LastImpactTier`, so reading `LastImpactTier` inside `OnWallImpact` plays the **previous** dash's tier. Conversely, stops under `1.0 u` (such as starting adjacent to a blunt `DeflectionWedge` on `Layer 0` vs `wallLayers = 64`, halting at `0.049 u`) enter `OnWallImpact` with `CurrentTier == VelocityTier.None`—meaning falling back to `LastImpactTier` would still replay a stale `KineticRam` slam on a sub-tile tap.
3. **Omnidirectional Shake, Particle Axis Mismatch & Empty Per-Prefab Feedback**
   - `CinemachineCameraShake2D.ShakeCamera(float force)` calls `impulseSource.GenerateImpulse(force)` against a fixed `DefaultVelocity = (0, -1, 0)`, kicking the camera downward regardless of travel direction.
   - `WallImpactBurst.prefab` uses a 3D Cone `ShapeModule` (`rotation = 0,0,0`) emitting along local **`+Z`**, not `+X`, whereas `impactAnimationPrefab` expects 2D `+X` Z-rotation. Meanwhile `tier1Shake`, `redirectShake`, `impactAnimationPrefab`, and `blockBreakAnimationPrefab` are zero/null in `GameScene.unity`, `CrackedBlock.OnAnyBroken` triggers zero hit-stop/shake, and `Wedge.prefab`, `Brake.prefab`, `Exit.prefab`, and `Hazard.prefab` have empty `InteractionEffectPlayer`s.
4. **167ms "Runway Blindness", Phase Clipping, `Redirect()` Double-Fire & Unfiltered Input State**
   - At `30 u/s`, Subject G-0 reaches `KineticRam` in `0.167 s` without per-tile audio escalation or afterimages, while `PlayerLineTrail` clamps `instSpeed` to `[4f, 28f]`—clipping both `30 u/s` runs and `48 u/s` `StepPhaseLaunch` phasing.
   - `PlayerMovement.Redirect()` (and `StartPhaseLaunch`'s zero-distance branch) fires **both** `OnRedirected` and `OnMoveStarted`, double-playing `moveStart` + `wedgeRedirect` in `EntityAudio`, forcing frame-count workarounds in `PlayerSquashStretch`, and overcounting `LevelFlowController.movesUsedThisRun`.
   - `PlayerMovement.QueueDirection` writes raw `pendingDirection` on normal idle launches, blocked adjacent-wall swipes, and mid-slide reversals, so visual turn indicators cannot read raw `pendingDirection` safely.
5. **Silent Audio Wiring & Unsubscribed / Unfired World Hooks**
   - `SoundConfig.asset` currently has `clips: []` on all `23` `SoundEntry` fields, and `AudioSystems.prefab` leaves `pooledAudioSourcePrefab`, `fallbackAudioSource`, `musicSource`, and `ambientSource` unassigned (`null`), collapsing `SoundManager.sfxPool` down to a single source shared with `RandomPitchAudio`.
   - `CloseCallDetector.OnCloseCallTriggered` has **0 subscribers**, `Global Volume Profile.asset` is static at runtime, and `EnergyGate.OnLevelClockTick` clears `pendingDeferredClose` without notifying audio or visual listeners.
6. **Flat Haptic Priority Blocking & `1.60 s` Failure Loop**
   - `GameHaptics.EventCount = 7` emits identical `LightImpact` across all wall speeds, while `GlobalMinGapSeconds = 0.03f` silently suppresses higher-priority `KineticRamImpact` when `TierUp` or `BlockSmash` fires on the same tick (`0.000 s`) or one `FixedUpdate` tick prior (`0.020 s` at `30 u/s`).
   - `CutsceneManager.DeathRoutine` uses `deathFallbackDuration = 0.8f` + `endFadeDuration = 0.4f` (~`1.60 s` total delay before showing the Result panel).

## 2. Six High-Impact Game Feel Pillars

All systems below obey Gravita's 4-layer architecture, create **zero** runtime `new GameObject(...)` or `AddComponent<...>()`, preserve deterministic `SimulateStep` grid physics, and guard every DOTween tween/sequence with `.DOKill()` (or `?.Kill()`), `.SetUpdate(true)`, and `.SetLink(gameObject)`.

### Pillar 1: Kinetic Runway Engine ("33ms-per-Tile Escalation & Unscaled Dynamic Lens")

#### Why It Makes Gravita Uniquely Addictive
Because slides finish in fractions of a second (`167 ms` to reach `KineticRam`), static trail color swaps aren't enough for the player's nervous system to *feel* acceleration. Turning every crossed tile into an ascending harmonic synth tick paired with unscaled additive camera zoom-out, crisp `KineticRam` afterimages, and trail/particle playback that keeps flowing smoothly during hit-stop turns long corridors into an accelerating slingshot.

- **`CinemachineCameraZoom2D.cs` (Pixel-Perfect Guard + Dual-Channel Unscaled Composition)**:
  Serialize optional `[SerializeField] private Behaviour cinemachinePixelPerfect;` and `[SerializeField] private Behaviour urpPixelPerfectCamera;` with `[SerializeField] private bool disablePixelPerfectOverrides = true;` and disable both in `Awake()` alongside scene Inspector state so `PixelPerfectCamera.OnBeginCameraRendering` can never clamp `m_Camera.orthographicSize` to `2.8125`. Separate `currentBaseSize` from `pulseOffset` so `Update()` and DOTween compose cleanly:
  ```csharp
  private float currentBaseSize;
  private float pulseOffset;
  private Sequence pulseSequence;

  private void Awake()
  {
      // ... existing singleton setup ...
      if (disablePixelPerfectOverrides)
      {
          if (cinemachinePixelPerfect != null) cinemachinePixelPerfect.enabled = false;
          if (urpPixelPerfectCamera != null) urpPixelPerfectCamera.enabled = false;
      }
      currentBaseSize = normalOrthographicSize > 0f ? normalOrthographicSize : 5.5f;
      if (targetOrthographicSize <= 0f) targetOrthographicSize = currentBaseSize;
  }

  private void Update()
  {
      if (cinemachineCamera == null) return;
      float alpha = 1f - Mathf.Exp(-zoomSpeed * Time.unscaledDeltaTime);
      currentBaseSize = Mathf.Lerp(currentBaseSize, targetOrthographicSize, alpha);
      cinemachineCamera.Lens.OrthographicSize = Mathf.Max(1f, currentBaseSize + pulseOffset);
  }

  public void PulseZoom(float orthoDelta, float inDuration, float outDuration)
  {
      pulseSequence?.Kill();
      pulseSequence = DOTween.Sequence()
          .Append(DOTween.To(() => pulseOffset, x => pulseOffset = x, orthoDelta, inDuration).SetEase(Ease.OutQuad))
          .Append(DOTween.To(() => pulseOffset, x => pulseOffset = x, 0f, outDuration).SetEase(Ease.OutSine))
          .SetUpdate(true)
          .SetLink(gameObject)
          .OnKill(() => pulseOffset = 0f);
  }
  ```

  ```csharp
  {

      private Sequence fadeSequence;

      public void Play(SpriteRenderer source, Color tint, float fadeDuration)
      {

      private void OnDisable()

          fadeSequence = null;
      }
  }
  ```

### Pillar 2: Directional Impact Physics, Wall-Face Compression & Break/Brake Fidelity

#### Why It Makes Gravita Uniquely Addictive
In a stop-to-stop maze, impact *is* the punctuation mark of every swipe. Fixing directional camera kick, keeping Subject G-0 pinned flush against ceilings and walls during hit-stop compression, distinguishing soft `BrakeTile`/`GravityBlock` stops from hard wall slams, aiming 3D vs. 2D particles along their actual emission axes, and ensuring sub-tile stops never replay stale `KineticRam` sounds makes every collision feel physically honest.

     ```csharp
     Vector3 pressed = baseLocalPosition;
     if (pressDirection != Vector2.zero)
     {
         Vector2 dir = pressDirection.normalized;
         float pressWorld = wallPressPx / 32f;
         Vector2 size = spriteRenderer != null && spriteRenderer.sprite != null
             ? (Vector2)spriteRenderer.sprite.bounds.size
             : Vector2.one;

             ? spriteRenderer.sprite.pivot / spriteRenderer.sprite.rect.size

         float dx = dir.x > 0.5f ? size.x * (1f - pivotNorm.x) * (baseScale.x - deformed.x)

                  : dir.y < -0.5f ? -size.y * pivotNorm.y * (baseScale.y - deformed.y) : 0f;

         pressed += new Vector3(dir.x * pressWorld + dx, dir.y * pressWorld + dy, 0f);
     }
     ```

### Pillar 3: Billiard Corner-Whip & Buffered-Turn "Input Coil"

#### Why It Makes Gravita Uniquely Addictive
Gravita's flow relies on `bufferTurnsUntilStop = true` and chained `DeflectionWedge` / `VectorPad` rebounds. Players swipe *before* hitting the wall—showing a crisp visual confirmation *exclusively* when a perpendicular turn is genuinely armed removes perceived input drop, while fixing `Redirect()`'s `OnMoveStarted` double-fire stops move counters and audio from misfiring on corner bounces.

#### Exact Numbers, Curves & Inspector Parameters
| Feature | Parameters & Curves |
|---|---|
| **Input Coil Chevron** | Pre-wired child `SpriteRenderer` on `Player`, offset `0.58 u` toward `BufferedTurnDirection`; scales `0.35 → 1.0` in `0.065 s` (`OutBack`); alpha pulses `0.65 ↔ 1.0` (`10 Hz`); UI click `vol = 0.20, pitch = 1.18` (armed turns only) |
| **Wedge Deflection Chain (`OnRedirected`)** | Bisector spark along `(-prevDir + newDir).normalized`; pitch escalates `1.00f + 0.07f * redirectChain` (capped at `maxConsecutiveRedirects = 8` → `1.56f`); directional camera nudge `0.08` along `newDir` |
| **Corner-Whip Tween** | Rotate `visualTransform` toward travel turn `±10°` and snap back `0°` over `0.10 s` (`OutExpo`, `.DOKill().SetUpdate(true).SetLink(gameObject)`) |
| **Phase Launch (`OnPhasingChanged`)** | Pulse `phaseSpriteTint` alpha `0.45 ↔ 0.85` (`12 Hz`); spawn pooled phase ring every `0.07 s` unscaled; on exit (`false`), call `PulseZoom(-0.12f, 0.04f, 0.12f)` + phase-exit chime |

- **Fix `PlayerMovement.Redirect()` & `StartPhaseLaunch()` Double-Fire (`PlayerMovement.cs`)**:

  ```csharp
  private bool hasArmedBufferedTurn;
  private Vector2 armedTurnDirection;
  private Vector2 lastNotifiedBufferedTurn;

  public Vector2 BufferedTurnDirection => hasArmedBufferedTurn ? armedTurnDirection : Vector2.zero;
  public event Action<Vector2> OnBufferedTurnChanged;

  private void NotifyBufferedTurnIfChanged()
  {
  ```
