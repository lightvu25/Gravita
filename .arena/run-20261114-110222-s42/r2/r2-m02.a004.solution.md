# Gravita (`Puzzle 2D`) — Game Feel Signature Upgrade Blueprint

> **Engine Target:** Unity `6000.4.0f1` URP (2D Top-Down, `32 PPU`, `cellSize = 1`, `PixelPerfectCamera` `320x180`, `PlayerMovement.moveSpeed = 30` u/s = `33.3 ms/tile`).  
> **Architecture Standard:** Event-driven SRP across `Assets/Scripts/Feedback/`, `Assets/Scripts/Audio/`, and `Assets/Scripts/UI/`. Zero procedural `new GameObject()` / runtime `AddComponent<T>()`; all DOTween sequences use `.DOKill()`, `.SetUpdate(true)`, and `.SetLink(gameObject)`. `FixedUpdate` deterministic grid physics and `Collider2D` bounds stay untouched.

---

## Scope & Assumptions

1. **Velocity Tier Threshold Reconciliation:** `Assets/Scripts/Gameplay/VelocityTier.cs` (`VelocityTierUtil.FromTiles`) classifies `1–2` tiles as `Drift`, `3–4` tiles as `Cruise`, and `5+` tiles as `KineticRam` (`MinTilesForTier`), whereas higher-level design notes sometimes describe `1–3 / 4–7 / 8+`. All timing curves below anchor to `VelocityTierUtil.FromTiles` and `moveSpeed = 30` u/s (`Drift = 33–67 ms`, `Cruise = 100–133 ms`, `KineticRam = 167+ ms`), using `VelocityTier`-keyed Inspector fields so rebalancing thresholds never breaks feedback.
2. **Audio, VFX & Prefab Hierarchy Baseline:**
   - `Assets/Data/Sound/SoundConfig.asset` currently ships with empty `clips: []` arrays; `Assets/Prefabs/Manager/AudioSystems.prefab` and `Assets/Scenes/GameScene.unity` leave `pooledAudioSourcePrefab`, `musicSource`, and `ambientSource` unassigned (`{fileID: 0}`); and `InteractionEffectPlayer` rules on `Brake.prefab`, `Exit.prefab`, `Hazard.prefab`, and `Wedge.prefab` are empty.
   - `VectorPad.prefab` and `VectorLauncher.prefab` already separate a child `Visual` transform from the root `Collider2D` (and `FailingTile.prefab` already has child `CollapsedPitHazard`). By contrast, `EnergyGate.prefab` (`closedVisual`/`openVisual` unassigned), `Wedge.prefab`, `CircuitSwitch.prefab`, `PulseLaser.prefab`, `OverloadedFloor.prefab` (`inactiveVisual`/`warningVisual`/`activeVisual` unassigned), `Brake.prefab`, `Brittle.prefab`, `Exit.prefab`, `Hazard.prefab`, `Cracked.prefab`, and `GravityBlock.prefab` currently have `m_Children: []` with `SpriteRenderer` and `Collider2D` together on the root `GameObject`. All script hooks below forbid transforming collider-bearing roots until child `Visual` objects are separated in Phase B.

## 1. Core Game Feel Diagnosis: Why Gravita Currently Feels Flat

Gravita already contains event hooks (`PlayerMovement`, `CloseCallDetector`, `InteractionEffectPlayer`, `GameplayHapticsFeedback`, `CinemachineCameraZoom2D`), yet feels muted in hands-on play for six concrete engineering reasons:

1. **Animation Timescales Ignore `33.3 ms/tile` Speed & Redirect Chaining:** In `GameScene.unity`, `PlayerSquashStretch` sets `stretchDuration = 0.18s` (`72 ms` out, `108 ms` back) and `squashDuration = 0.22s` (`88 ms` compress, `132 ms` recover). At `30 u/s`, a `Drift` slide (`33–67 ms`) hits the wall *before* launch stretch peaks, while a long `KineticRam` (`167+ ms`) snaps back to `baseScale` mid-corridor instead of sustaining aerodynamic tension. `HandleMoveStopped()` also applies identical `impactHeightPx = 16 / movingHeightPx = 28` squash to a 1-tile tap, a `KineticRam` slam, a `BrakeTile` stop, and a `LevelExit` freeze, ignores `OnRedirected`, lacks `.SetUpdate(true)` on `Play()`, and kills only `activeSequence` rather than protecting against external tween contention.
2. **Bottom-Center Sprite Pivot (`localPosition = (0, -0.5, 0)`) Inverts Wall Squashes & Spins:** `Player/Sprite` (`Player Icon.aseprite`, `32x32` @ `32 PPU`) scales from a `(0.5, 0)` bottom-center pivot. During `Vector2.up` (`maxDeformation = 0.3`), compressing `sy` to `0.70` drops the top face by `0.30` u (`9.6 px`) while `wallPressPx = 4` (`0.125` u) shifts upward—leaving Subject G-0 **`0.175` u (`5.6 px`) pulled away from north walls**. Conversely, `Vector2.down` shifts `localPosition.y` down by `-0.125` u, sinking the sprite **`4 px` inside south colliders**. Horizontal runs shrink toward the feet instead of center of mass, and naive Z-rotations orbit around the bottom edge.
3. **Broken Event Ordering, Custom-Break Skip, Hit-Stop Leaks & Trail Reset:**
   - **Stale Impact Audio & Missing Normal:** `PlayerMovement.AdvanceSlideStep` (`PlayerMovement.cs:400, 429`) invokes `OnWallImpact?.Invoke(...)` *before* `StopMoving(wallImpact: true)` (`L769`) writes `LastImpactTier`. `EntityAudio` reads `LastImpactTier`, playing the *previous* dash's tier, while `PlayerImpactFeedback` splits shake/hit-stop inside parameterless `HandleStopped()` (ignoring `WasLastStopWallImpact`) and contact sparks inside `HandleWallImpact(Vector2 normal)`.
   - **Silent `CrackedBlock` Skip & Hit-Stop Collisions:** `Cracked.prefab` already assigns `WallImpactBreak.prefab` on `InteractionEffectPlayer` (`HasCustomBreakEffect == true`), so `PlayerImpactFeedback.HandleBlockBroken` (`L128`) early-returns before any break feedback runs; `CrackedBlock.lastImpactDirection` is `private` (and `PowerUpController.TryBreakAlongDirection` calls `Break()` with `Vector2.zero`); and `PlayerImpactFeedback.OnDisable()` stops `hitStopRoutine` without restoring `preHitStopTimeScale`.
   - **Redirect -> `OnMoveStarted` Event Order:** `PlayerMovement.Redirect` (`L997-998`) emits `OnRedirected` then `OnMoveStarted` without resetting `TilesTraveled`. `EntityAudio` subscribes to both (`L55-57`), double-playing `moveStart` + `wedgeRedirect` on every corner, while naive `OnMoveStarted` listeners wipe ricochet chains and collapse sustained flight stretch.
   - **Premature Trail Color Reset & Speed Clamp:** `StopMoving()` invokes `ResetDashTracking()` (`OnVelocityTierChanged(None)`) *before* `OnMoveStopped`, snapping `PlayerLineTrail` back to cyan `Drift` (`headColor`) instead of holding `Cruise` gold or `KineticRam` magenta during retraction. `PlayerLineTrail.LateUpdate` (`L292`) clamps `smoothedMoveSpeed` at `28f`, below `moveSpeed = 30` and `PhaseLaunch = 48`.
4. **Dead Momentum & Near-Miss Channels + Unwired Hit-Flash Shader:** `PlayerMovement.OnTileCrossed` has **0 subscribers**, leaving velocity escalation silent; `CloseCallDetector.OnCloseCallTriggered` has **0 external subscribers** (`CloseCallDetector` runs only its internal `0.06f` pulse and `StarBurst` VFX). Furthermore, `Player/Sprite` uses `Sprite-Lit-Default` with `Color.white`, so color-only white flashes are multiplicative no-ops even though `Assets/Shaders/Hit/Hit Flash White.mat` (`Shader "Echoes/VFX/Solid White Sprite"`) is already in the project.
5. **Locked `PixelPerfect` Ortho, Scalar Shake & Particle Up-Vector Collinearity:** `GameScene.unity` zeroes `tier1Shake` and `redirectShake`; `CinemachineCameraShake2D` emits scalar jitter; `PlayerImpactFeedback.HandleWallImpact` calls `Quaternion.LookRotation((Vector3)normal)` with implicit `Vector3.up` (collinear on `Vector2.up`/`Vector2.down`, rotating `WallImpactBurst.prefab`'s Cone `+Z` emitter inconsistently while `useUnscaledTime` defaults to `0`); and `CinemachineCameraZoom2D` (`5.5` ortho, `Time.deltaTime` lerp) has **0 callers** while both `CinemachinePixelPerfect` (`CinemachineCamera`) and `PixelPerfectCamera` (`Main Camera`, `320x180`, `32 PPU`) remain enabled and lock `orthographicSize`.
6. **Invisible Input Queueing, Unsplit Prop Visuals & Null Scene Audio Refs:** `bufferTurnsUntilStop = true` gives zero mid-dash queued confirmation or blocked-swipe tactility; `BrittleFloor`, `CircuitSwitch`, `EnergyGate`, `PulseLaser`, `OverloadedFloor`, and `LevelExit` produce flat swaps (and share root transforms with `Collider2D`); and `MusicManager.musicSource` & `SoundManager.pooledAudioSourcePrefab` are null in `GameScene.unity` and `AudioSystems.prefab`.

## 2. Six Gravita-Specific Game Feel Pillars

### Pillar 1: Kinetic Runway Engine (Center-Safe Deformation, Pooled Ghost Echoes & Cooldown-Safe Semitone Rail Ticks)

#### Why It Makes Gravita Uniquely Addictive
At `30 u/s` (`50 Hz` physics = `0.6` tiles/step), straightaways resolve in fractions of a second. Coupling sustained aerodynamic elongation, properly reset pooled sprite ghosts, retract color persistence, and a cooldown-safe semitone rail-tick staircase turns Tiles 1 through 5+ into escalating kinetic anticipation—making `KineticRam` feel *earned* in the player's fingertips before impact lands.

#### Exact C# Integration Hooks
1. **Center-Compensated, Redirect-Safe & Capture-Safe `Assets/Scripts/Feedback/PlayerSquashStretch.cs`:**
   ```csharp
   [SerializeField] private Vector2 defaultPivotToCenter = new Vector2(0f, 0.5f);
   [SerializeField] private float stretchInDuration = 0.040f;
   [SerializeField] private float sustainedGlideTransition = 0.045f;
   [SerializeField] private float redirectFlipDuration = 0.035f;

   private bool justRedirectedInFixedStep;
   private float lastRedirectFixedTime = -1f;
   private bool externalCaptureActive;

   public void BeginExternalCapture()
   {
       externalCaptureActive = true;
       KillActive();
   }

   public void EndExternalCapture()
   {
       externalCaptureActive = false;
       KillActive();
       if (visualTransform != null)
       {
           visualTransform.localScale = baseScale;
           visualTransform.localPosition = baseLocalPosition;
           visualTransform.localRotation = Quaternion.identity;
       }
   }

   public void PlayBlockedBump(Vector2 dir)
   {
       if (externalCaptureActive || visualTransform == null || (movement != null && movement.IsMoving)) return;
       KillActive();
       visualTransform.localScale = baseScale;
       Vector3 bumpOffset = (Vector3)(dir.normalized * (3f / 32f));
       activeSequence = DOTween.Sequence()
           .Append(visualTransform.DOLocalMove(baseLocalPosition + bumpOffset, 0.030f).SetEase(Ease.OutQuad))
           .Append(visualTransform.DOLocalMove(baseLocalPosition, 0.060f).SetEase(Ease.OutSine))
           .SetUpdate(true)
           .SetLink(gameObject);
   }

   private Vector2 ResolvePivotToCenter()
   {
       if (spriteRenderer == null || spriteRenderer.sprite == null)
           return defaultPivotToCenter;

       Sprite sprite = spriteRenderer.sprite;
       Vector2 rectSize = sprite.rect.size;
       if (rectSize.x <= 0.001f || rectSize.y <= 0.001f)
           return defaultPivotToCenter;

       Vector2 normPivot = new Vector2(sprite.pivot.x / rectSize.x, sprite.pivot.y / rectSize.y);
       Vector2 worldSize = sprite.bounds.size;
       return new Vector2((0.5f - normPivot.x) * worldSize.x, (0.5f - normPivot.y) * worldSize.y);
   }

   private Vector3 ComputeCompensatedLocalPosition(Vector3 targetScale, Vector2 pressDir, float pressPx)
   {
       Vector2 pivotToCenter = ResolvePivotToCenter();
       Vector2 ratio = new Vector2(
           baseScale.x > 0.0001f ? targetScale.x / baseScale.x : 1f,
           baseScale.y > 0.0001f ? targetScale.y / baseScale.y : 1f);
       Vector3 centerComp = baseLocalPosition + new Vector3(
           pivotToCenter.x * (1f - ratio.x),
           pivotToCenter.y * (1f - ratio.y),
           0f);

       if (pressDir == Vector2.zero)
           return centerComp;

       bool horizontal = Mathf.Abs(pressDir.x) >= Mathf.Abs(pressDir.y);
       float scaleAlong = horizontal ? ratio.x : ratio.y;
       float cellSize = movement != null ? movement.CellSize : 1f;
       float pinOffset = 0.5f * cellSize * (1f - scaleAlong) + pressPx / 32f;
       return centerComp + (Vector3)(pressDir.normalized * pinOffset);
   }

   private void HandleRedirected(Vector2 newDir)
   {
       if (externalCaptureActive) return;
       justRedirectedInFixedStep = true;
       lastRedirectFixedTime = Time.fixedTime;
       lastDirection = newDir;
       GetSustainedStretchForState(out float along, out float across);
       ApplySustainedStretch(newDir, along, across, redirectFlipDuration);
   }

   private void HandleMoveStarted(Vector2 dir)
   {
       if (externalCaptureActive) return;
       if (justRedirectedInFixedStep && Mathf.Approximately(Time.fixedTime, lastRedirectFixedTime))
       {
           justRedirectedInFixedStep = false;
           return;
       }

       justRedirectedInFixedStep = false;
       lastDirection = dir;
       GetSustainedStretchForState(out float along, out float across);
       ApplySustainedStretch(dir, along, across, stretchInDuration);
   }

   private void HandleMoveStopped()
   {
       justRedirectedInFixedStep = false;
       if (externalCaptureActive) return;
       if (movement != null && movement.InputLocked)
       {
           ResetScale();
           return;
       }
       // Branch on movement.WasLastStopWallImpact: true -> tier-scaled wall squash + unscaled hit-stop hold; false -> 0.06s relax.
   }
   ```
   - `PlayerSquashStretch` preserves existing `HandlePhasingChanged` and `RestoreSpriteColor` (`PlayerCosmeticApplier` sync), binds `movement.OnRedirected` in `OnEnable`/`OnDisable`, and runs every tween on its owned `activeSequence` with `.SetUpdate(true).SetLink(gameObject)` (never calling `visualTransform.DOKill()` from outside).

### Pillar 2: Directional Impact, Shader Hit-Flash & Stop Polarity (Wall-Slam vs. Mag-Brake)

#### Why It Makes Gravita Uniquely Addictive
Gravita halts `30 u/s` motion in two opposite ways: slamming a solid wall/`CrackedBlock`, or gliding onto a `BrakeTile`/`GravityBlock` magnetic floor arrest. Routing wall impact feedback through `OnWallImpact(normal)` after latching `LastImpactTier`/`LastImpactNormal`, stacking hit-stop cleanly across `CrackedBlock` breaks, firing solid-white shader flashes, and keeping magnetic floor stops at **zero shake and zero hit-stop** makes impacts punchy through physical contrast.

           cinemachineCamera.Lens.OrthographicSize,

     // Base offset of sprite visual center from Player root is centerLocal = baseLocalPosition + pivotToCenter (0, 0, 0).
     private void ApplyCenteredTransform(float uniformScale, float angleDeg)
     {
         Quaternion rot = Quaternion.Euler(0f, 0f, angleDeg);
         playerVisualTransform.localRotation = rot;
         playerVisualTransform.localScale = baseScale * uniformScale;

         playerVisualTransform.localPosition = centerLocalPosition - rotatedPivotToCenter;

     {
         squashStretch?.BeginExternalCapture();
         CinemachineCameraZoom2D.Instance?.SetZoomOffset(-0.45f, 5f);
         PlayHarmonicExitChime();

         float currentAngle = 0f;

             .SetUpdate(true)
             .SetLink(gameObject);
     }

## 3. Technical Guardrails & Failure-Mode Prevention

| Risk / Failure Mode | Concrete Guardrail in This Blueprint |
|---|---|
| **Procedural Hierarchy Debt** | Strictly prohibited. Zero `new GameObject()` or runtime `AddComponent<T>()` calls. All arrows, echoes, popups, and volumes are serialized scene/child references or pooled prefabs via `ObjectPoolManager` / `PrefabAnimationSpawner` / `ParticleSpawner`. |

| **Event-Order, Pool-Reuse & Pivot Races** | `LastImpactTier`, `WasLastStopWallImpact`, and `LastImpactNormal` latch *before* `OnWallImpact`; `PlayerImpactFeedback.HandleWallImpact` owns wall shake/hit-stop; `HandleBlockBroken` triggers shake/hit-stop/flash before `HasCustomBreakEffect`; `PlayerMovement` separates `OnPendingTurnBuffered`/`Executed`/`Discarded`/`OnBlockedDirectionInput`; `PooledGhostEcho` & 3D `PooledWorldTextPopup` reset `color`/alpha on spawn; `ExitCaptureFeedback` claims `BeginExternalCapture()` and animates around `(0, 0)` center in lockstep with `CutsceneManager.GoalRoutine`. |
| **PixelPerfect vs. Zoom Fight & Audio Cooldown Drops** | `CinemachineCameraZoom2D` uses `Time.unscaledDeltaTime`, manages `CinemachinePixelPerfect` + `PixelPerfectCamera` cleanly during active zoom deltas, and restores at rest; `SoundManager.PlaySFXWithPitch(..., ignoreCooldown: true)` prevents `20 ms` `50 Hz` `OnTileCrossed` and `HazardTelegraphFeedback` drops; `EntityAudio` suppresses `moveStart` on `Redirect`. |
| **DOTween Leak & Respawn Drift** | Every tween calls `.DOKill()` on its specific target (`activeSequence`, `arrowRoot`, `overdriveVolume`, `musicSource`), binds `.SetUpdate(true)`, and binds `.SetLink(gameObject)`. `OnDisable()` and `LevelFlowController.OnAnyRunStartedOrReset` restore scales, rotations, positions, materials, and pooled objects. |
