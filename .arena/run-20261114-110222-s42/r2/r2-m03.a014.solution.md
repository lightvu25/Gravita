# Gravita Game Feel Signature Upgrade Blueprint

**Target Project**: `Puzzle 2D` / **Gravita** (Unity 6000.4.0f1 URP, 2D Top-Down Kinetic Momentum Puzzle Slider)  
**Architectural Mandate**: Elevate tactile kinetic weight, high-velocity readability, and micro-moment reward loops while preserving deterministic `FixedUpdate` grid physics, zero runtime procedural hierarchy construction, single-responsibility modular feedback scripts, and clean asset isolation.

---

## 1. Systemic Diagnosis: Current Pipeline Bottlenecks & Failure Modes

Auditing `Assets/Scripts/Gameplay/`, `Assets/Scripts/Feedback/`, `Assets/Scripts/Audio/`, `Assets/Scenes/GameScene.unity`, and gameplay prefabs reveals seven structural bottlenecks muting kinetic feel and introducing edge-case artifacts:

1. **Hit-Stop & Cinemachine Time-Scale Desynchronization**:
   - `CinemachineBrain` in `Assets/Scenes/GameScene.unity` runs with `IgnoreTimeScale: 0` and `UpdateMethod: 2` (`SmartUpdate`), while `CinemachineImpulseManager.Instance.IgnoreTimeScale` defaults to `false` (reading `CinemachineCore.CurrentTime`).
   - When `PlayerImpactFeedback.HitStopRoutine` sets `Time.timeScale = 0f`, both `CinemachineBrain` updates and `CinemachineImpulseManager` elapsed time freeze until Hit-Stop expires—deferring wall-slam recoil until after the freeze ends. Worse, because `CinemachineCamera.TrackingTarget` targets `Player` (which simulates in `FixedUpdate`), `SmartUpdate` can inherit `UpdateTracker.UpdateClock.Fixed`, gating `CameraUpdateManager` even if `IgnoreTimeScale` is enabled unless temporarily routed to `LateUpdate`.
   - Meanwhile, `PlayerImpactFeedback.OnDisable()` cancels `hitStopRoutine` without restoring `Time.timeScale` or `CinemachineBrain.UpdateMethod` if the player or scene restarts mid-Hit-Stop, and `CinemachineCameraZoom2D.Update()` currently polls `Time.deltaTime` rather than `Time.unscaledDeltaTime`.

2. **Fixed Sprite Geometry Assumptions & Lost Flight Postures**:
   - `PlayerSquashStretch.Deform` derives position compensation from a hardcoded `1.0` world-unit bottom-pivot assumption (`0.5f * (1f - targetScale.y)`), matching `Player Icon.aseprite` (`32x32 @ 32 PPU`, `pivot: {0.5, 0}` with `Player/Sprite` at `localPosition = (0, -0.5, 0)`), but misaligning unlocked cosmetic skins (`Skin_Amber`, `Skin_Cobalt`, `Skin_Prism` in `Assets/Data/Cosmetics/`) whose sprites are `24x24 @ 100 PPU` (`0.24u` tall, `pivot: {0.5, -0.16666667}`, `bounds.center.y = 0.16`).
   - `PlayerSquashStretch.HandleMoveStarted` unconditionally forces `VelocityTier.Drift`, collapsing sustained `Cruise` or `KineticRam` flight postures back to `Drift` whenever `DeflectionWedge`/`VectorPad` fires `Redirect` (`OnRedirected` followed by `OnMoveStarted`) or `StepPhaseLaunch` resumes sliding after a phase jump. Conversely, `HandlePhasingChanged(true)` on a stationary `VectorLauncher` lacks an immediate flight-stretch transition, and `HandleMoveStopped` runs an anchor squash even when `PlayerMovement.Freeze()` triggers `StopMoving` with `InputLocked == true`.
   - `Player/Sprite` uses a built-in URP sprite material (`GUID 9dfc825aed78fcd4ba02077103263b40`) lacking custom shader properties (`_FlashAmount`, `_PhaseAlpha`, `_KineticRimStrength`), forcing reliance on `SpriteRenderer.color` alpha updates until a kinetic shader is authored.

3. **Uncompensated Negative/Bottom Sprite Pivots on World Tiles & Hazards**:
   - Across `Assets/Prefabs/Gameplay/`, visual sprites pivot at or below their bottom edge rather than geometric center: `Wedge` (`{0.516129, 0}`), `CircuitSwitch_Off` (`{0.5, -0.11538462}`), `PulseLaser_Emitter` (`{0.5, -0.16666667}`), and `VectorPad` / `VectorLauncher` / `OverloadedFloor` / `FailingTile` (`{0.5, -0.071428575}`). Scaling their `Visual` child transforms directly shifts sprites upward or laterally relative to their grid footprints and rotated launch vectors.

4. **Non-Polyphonic Audio Pool State & `RandomPitchAudio` Interference**:
   - `Assets/Prefabs/Manager/AudioSystems.prefab` configures `SoundManager` with `poolContainer: null`, `pooledAudioSourcePrefab: null`, `fallbackAudioSource: null`, and a scene override in `GameScene.unity` setting `sfxPool.Array.data[0]: null`.
   - `SoundManager.InitializePool()` falls back to scanning children of `AudioSystems`, picking up only the root `AudioSource` (`m_PlayOnAwake: 1`, shared with `RandomPitchAudio`). Triggering rapid `OnTileCrossed` cadence ticks or layered impact transients (`Thud + Crack + Spark`) on a single voice clips transients or mutates `.pitch` on actively playing sounds.

5. **Duplicate Shake Ownership & Non-Wall Stop Bleeding**:
   - `Wedge.prefab` (`InteractionEffectPlayer.rules`) assigns `cameraShake` on `Deflect`/`Hit` while `PlayerImpactFeedback.HandleRedirected()` also dispatches `redirectShake`, compounding impulses per deflection.
   - `PlayerImpactFeedback.HandleStopped()` does not filter on `movement.WasLastStopWallImpact`, causing `Brake` and `GravityBlock` halts (`HaltAt -> StopMoving(wallImpact: false)`) and `VectorLauncher` post-phase idle stops to fire wall-tier Hit-Stop and wall camera shake on top of `InteractionEffectPlayer.defaultCameraShake`.
   - Additionally, inside `PlayerMovement.SimulateStep`, `OnWallImpact` emits before `StopMoving(wallImpact: true)` records `LastImpactTier`, requiring explicit alignment between impact VFX, audio, and stop-based recoil.

6. **Shared Volume Profile Mutation & Overlapping Post-Process Clobbering**:
   - `Global Volume` in `GameScene.unity` points directly to `Assets/Volume/Global Volume Profile.asset` (`ChromaticAberration = 0.2`, `Bloom = 9`, `Vignette = 0.4`, all `m_OverrideState: 1`). Writing `sharedProfile` dirties version-controlled assets on disk and persists intensity drifts outside Play Mode.
   - Driving `ChromaticAberration` directly from a single value across `CloseCall` grazes and `KineticRam` wall impacts causes concurrent tweens to overwrite or zero-snap each other mid-decay.

7. **Missing Reversal Clarity, Hazard Anticipation & Narrow Haptic Palette**:
   - Instant opposite-direction reversal drops a held perpendicular `pendingDirection` silently in `PlayerMovement.QueueDirection` without an observational cue (`OnDirectionBufferCleared`).
   - `PulseLaser`, `OverloadedFloor`, and `FailingTile` transition to danger without a crisp pre-frame quiver/brightness telegraph, `CinemachineCamera` (`Damping: {1, 1, 1}`, `Lookahead.Enabled: 0`) lags behind `30 u/s` dashes, and `HapticEvent` (`0..6`, `EventCount = 7`) omits `Graze`, `TierCruise`, `TierRam`, and `BufferClear`.

## 2. Core Engineering Rules & Architectural Guardrails

Every change in this blueprint adheres strictly to these project constraints:

- **Deterministic Physics Isolation**: `PlayerMovement.FixedUpdate`, `SimulateStep`, `MoveSpeed = 30`, `VelocityTier` thresholds (`Drift` 1–2 tiles, `Cruise` 3–4, `KineticRam` 5+), `bufferTurnsUntilStop = 1`, and `maxConsecutiveRedirects = 8` remain untouched. Movement script additions are strictly read-only observational events (`OnDirectionBufferCleared`) without altering simulation state or timing.
- **No Runtime Hierarchy Construction**: Zero calls to `new GameObject(...)` or runtime `AddComponent<...>()`. All VFX prefabs (`VfxSpawner.Spawn`), `SfxPool` voices, and feedback components are pre-authored in Edit Mode on prefabs and scene objects.
- **DOTween Safety Discipline**: Every tween and sequence calls `.DOKill()` before creation, `.SetUpdate(true)` so motion decays cleanly during `Time.timeScale == 0`, and `.SetLink(gameObject)` to prevent orphan callbacks on destroy.
- **Asset & Scene Integrity**: Runtime post-processing clones `VolumeProfile` cleanly and frees it on destroy (`Assets/Volume/Global Volume Profile.asset` never dirtied). Every `ScriptableObject` config exposes `[CreateAssetMenu]` and validates bounds in `OnValidate()`.

## 3. The 5 Pillars of Gravita Game Feel

### Pillar 1: Kinetic Momentum Posture, Bounds-Relative Squash-Stretch & Shader Telegraphy

**Why**: The player needs immediate spatial read of velocity tier (`Drift` -> `Cruise` -> `KineticRam`), turn cancellation, and phase traversal without expanding `BoxCollider2D` footprints.  
**Trade-off**: Pure transform scaling breaks across skins with varying PPU/pivots; deriving offsets live from `SpriteRenderer.sprite.bounds` guarantees flush wall compression for every cosmetic without per-skin config maintenance.

#### Technical Design & C# Hooks
1. **Dynamic Bounds-Relative Anchor Compensation (`PlayerSquashStretch.cs`)**:
   - Remove hardcoded `1.0` world-unit offsets in `Deform(Vector3 targetScale, Vector2 impactDir, bool pressIntoWall)`.
   - Read live `Bounds sb = spriteRenderer.sprite.bounds;` per deformation tick and compute offset `Vector3 offset`:
     - On any axis perpendicular to a wall press (and on all axes during flight stretches or anchor squashes where `pressIntoWall == false`), anchor at the sprite's true geometric center:  
       `offset.x = (1f - targetScale.x) * sb.center.x;`  
       `offset.y = (1f - targetScale.y) * sb.center.y;`
     - On the axis pressing into a wall (`pressIntoWall == true`):  
       - When `impactDir.x > 0.1f`: lock outer wall face at `sb.max.x` -> `offset.x = (1f - targetScale.x) * sb.max.x;`  
       - When `impactDir.x < -0.1f`: lock outer wall face at `sb.min.x` -> `offset.x = (1f - targetScale.x) * sb.min.x;`  
       - Analogously use `sb.max.y` / `sb.min.y` on vertical wall slams.
     - Works identically for `Player Icon` (`1.0u`, `{0.5, 0}`) and `Skin_Amber`/`Skin_Cobalt`/`Skin_Prism` (`0.24u`, `{0.5, -0.16666667}`).
   - Hook `PlayerCosmeticApplier.RefreshEquippedSkin()` completion (or detect `spriteRenderer.sprite` change before applying offsets) to refresh cached `baseSpriteColor` cleanly and call `ResetScale()`.
2. **Sustained Flight Posture Preservation & Guards (`PlayerSquashStretch.cs`)**:
   - In `HandleMoveStarted(Vector2 dir)`: resolve `VelocityTier activeTier = (movement != null && movement.CurrentTier != VelocityTier.None) ? movement.CurrentTier : VelocityTier.Drift;` so `DeflectionWedge` / `VectorPad` (`OnRedirected -> OnMoveStarted`) and `StepPhaseLaunch` post-phase movement keep `Cruise`/`KineticRam` elongation instead of resetting to `Drift`.
   - In `HandlePhasingChanged(bool isPhasing)`: when `isPhasing == true` (including launches directly from a stationary `VectorLauncher`), immediately snap/tween into `KineticRam` flight posture along the launch direction alongside phase translucency.
   - In `HandleMoveStopped()`: start with `if (movement != null && movement.InputLocked) { ResetScale(); return; }` so `PlayerMovement.Freeze()` on death or level clear exits cleanly without spuriously bouncing into an anchor squash.
   - Subscribe to `PlayerMovement.OnDirectionBufferCleared` to play a crisp `0.05s` reversal micro-squash (`(0.92, 1.08)` orthogonal pulse) confirming cancelled buffered turns.
3. **Two-Stage Shader & `MaterialPropertyBlock` Activation**:
   - **Color-Safe Fallback (Phases 1–2)**: Keep `SpriteRenderer.color` alpha fade (`0.45f` using cached `baseSpriteColor` RGB so skin tints remain intact) whenever `!spriteRenderer.sharedMaterial.HasProperty(PhaseAlphaId)`.
   - **Kinetic Shader Activation (Phase 3)**: Author `Assets/Shaders/GravitaPlayerURP2D.shader` (URP 2D Sprite Lit/Unlit compatible shader exposing `_FlashColor`, `_FlashAmount`, `_PhaseAlpha`, `_KineticRimColor`, `_KineticRimStrength`) and assign `Assets/Materials/Player_Kinetic.mat` to `Player/Sprite`. Activate `MaterialPropertyBlock` updates in `PlayerSquashStretch`:
     - `_FlashAmount`: spikes `1.0 -> 0.0` over `0.06s` (unscaled) on `Cruise`/`KineticRam` wall impact and `CrackedBlock` shatter.
     - `_PhaseAlpha`: lerps cleanly to `0.45f` while `movement.IsPhasing`.
     - `_KineticRimStrength`: steps `0.0f` (`None`) -> `0.20f` (`Drift`) -> `0.45f` (`Cruise`) -> `0.85f` (`KineticRam`).
4. **High-Refresh Smoothness**: Evaluate toggling `Rigidbody2D.m_Interpolate = 1` (`Interpolate`) on `Player` in `GameScene.unity` paired with `CinemachinePixelPerfect` so `30 u/s` movement renders smoothly between `FixedUpdate` steps on 120Hz+ panels without changing `SimulateStep`.

### Pillar 2: Time-Scale-Safe Hit-Stop, Unscaled Camera Choreography & Single-Owner Impulse Routing

**Why**: Impact pauses sharpen collision clarity only if camera shake, FOV punch, and flash decay play *through* `Time.timeScale == 0f`. Any freeze of Cinemachine or overlapping duplicate impulses turns crisp punch into mushy frame lag.  
**Trade-off**: Temporary `UpdateMethod` switching cleanly guarantees immediate camera shake response without permanently leaving `LateUpdate` active during normal `FixedUpdate` gameplay tracking.

#### Technical Design & C# Hooks
1. **End-to-End Unscaled Cinemachine Synchronization**:
   - **Scene (`GameScene.unity`)**: Set `CinemachineBrain.IgnoreTimeScale = true`.
   - **Impulse Clock (`CinemachineCameraShake2D.Awake()`)**: Set `CinemachineImpulseManager.Instance.IgnoreTimeScale = true` once at initialization so every `ImpulseEvent.StartTime` and `CurrentTime` resolves monotonically against `Time.realtimeSinceStartup`—never toggled mid-flight.
   - **Brain Update Clock Guard (`PlayerImpactFeedback.HitStopRoutine`)**: Because `SmartUpdate` (`UpdateMethod = 2`) can lock `CameraUpdateManager` to `UpdateTracker.UpdateClock.Fixed` for rigidbody targets (stalling camera updates when `Time.timeScale == 0`), capture `var savedUpdateMethod = cinemachineBrain.UpdateMethod;` when entering Hit-Stop, temporarily switch `cinemachineBrain.UpdateMethod = CinemachineBrain.UpdateMethods.LateUpdate` if not already `LateUpdate`, wait via `yield return new WaitForSecondsRealtime(duration);`, and restore both `Time.timeScale = 1f;` and `cinemachineBrain.UpdateMethod = savedUpdateMethod;` inside a `try { ... } finally { ... }` block.
   - **Lifecycle Guard (`PlayerImpactFeedback.OnDisable()`)**: Track `bool hitStopActive` and restore `Time.timeScale = 1f` plus `cinemachineBrain.UpdateMethod = savedUpdateMethod` if disabled/restarted mid-Hit-Stop, stopping stale freeze coroutines cleanly.
   - *(Note on `PlayerLineTrail.LateUpdate()`)*: Its existing `if (dt <= 0f) return;` intentionally freezes trail ribbon fade during the freeze frame so the streak holds onto the player frame-for-frame until time resumes.
2. **Single-Owner Shake Routing & Non-Wall Stop Separation**:
   - **`Wedge.prefab` (`InteractionEffectPlayer`)**: Zero out `cameraShake = 0f` on **both** `Deflect` and `Hit` rules. `PlayerImpactFeedback` is the sole impulse owner for wall impacts (`HandleStopped`) and wedge redirects (`HandleRedirected`).
   - **Stop Classification (`PlayerImpactFeedback.HandleStopped()`)**: Early-return `if (movement == null || movement.InputLocked || !movement.WasLastStopWallImpact) return;`. Non-wall stops (`Brake` / `GravityBlock` via `HaltAt -> StopMoving(wallImpact: false)`, `VectorLauncher` idle exits, `Freeze()`) never trigger wall Hit-Stop or wall shake, keeping `InteractionEffectPlayer` (`defaultCameraShake = 0.025f` on `Brake.prefab` / `GravityBlock.prefab`) cleanly isolated as the anchor shake owner.
   - **Event Order Awareness**: In `PlayerMovement.SimulateStep`, `OnWallImpact` fires prior to `StopMoving(wallImpact: true)` populating `LastImpactTier`. `PlayerImpactFeedback` keeps Hit-Stop & tier camera shake driven by `HandleStopped()` (where `WasLastStopWallImpact == true` and `LastImpactTier` is finalized), while `HandleWallImpact(Vector2 dir)` spawns impact particle VFX using `movement.CurrentTier` (before `StopMoving` clears it to `None`).
3. **Unscaled Dynamic Zoom & Velocity Look-Ahead**:
   - Update `CinemachineCameraZoom2D.cs` to step damping with `Time.unscaledDeltaTime`. Add `VelocityTier` ortho/FOV offset (`None/Drift: +0.00u`, `Cruise: +0.18u` subtle pull-back, `KineticRam: +0.35u` pull-back) and an unscaled `-0.22u` zoom punch-in (`0.04s` in, `0.12s` `Ease.OutQuad` back) on `KineticRam` wall impact or `CrackedBlock` shatter.
   - Tune `CinemachineCamera` in `GameScene.unity`: tighten `PositionComposer` `Damping` from `{1, 1, 1}` to `{0.45, 0.45, 1}` and enable bounded velocity `Lookahead` (`Enabled = true`, `Time = 0.12`, `Smoothing = 6`) so fast slides reveal upcoming walls and hazards sooner without over-panning.

### Pillar 3: Polyphonic Tiered Audio Engine & Pitch-Escalation Envelope

**Why**: Fast `30 u/s` dashes produce overlapping events—tile cadence ticks, combo redirects, close-call whips, and multi-layered impacts—within 30–100ms. A working, pitch-isolated voice pool is an architectural prerequisite before enabling high-frequency cadence ticks.  
**Trade-off**: Cap dynamic pool growth (`24` voices) and prefer `PlayOneShot` on a dedicated fixed-pitch fallback channel at cap rather than stealing or pitch-warping an active sound tail.

#### Technical Design & Prefab Wiring
1. **Polyphonic Pool Infrastructure (`Assets/Prefabs/Manager/AudioSystems.prefab` & `GameScene.unity`)**:
   - Author `Assets/Prefabs/Audio/SfxVoice.prefab`: single 2D `AudioSource` (`playOnAwake = false`, `loop = false`, `spatialBlend = 0f`).
   - Add two explicit child objects inside `AudioSystems.prefab`:
     - `SfxPool` containing `10` pre-authored `SfxVoice` instances (`playOnAwake = false`).
     - `FallbackSfxVoice` (`AudioSource`, `playOnAwake = false`) separate from the root `AudioSource` used by `RandomPitchAudio`.
   - Wire `SoundManager`: `poolContainer = SfxPool`, `pooledAudioSourcePrefab = SfxVoice.prefab`, `fallbackAudioSource = FallbackSfxVoice`, `initialPoolSize = 10`. Ensure `playOnAwake = false` on unused scene root SFX sources so `RandomPitchAudio` never shares pooled slots.
   - Remove the stale prefab override on `AudioSystems` in `Assets/Scenes/GameScene.unity` (`sfxPool.Array.data[0]: null`) so `InitializePool()` reliably registers all 10 clean `SfxPool` children at startup.
2. **Active-Voice Pitch Safety (`SoundManager.PlayResolvedClip`)**:
   - When selecting a voice from `sfxPool`, strictly pick an `AudioSource` where `!source.isPlaying`.
   - If all pool voices are busy, instantiate from `pooledAudioSourcePrefab` under `poolContainer` only if `sfxPool.Count < 24` (`maxPoolSize = 24`).
   - If at `maxPoolSize`, invoke `fallbackAudioSource.PlayOneShot(clip, volume)` with constant `pitch = 1f`—**never** mutate `.pitch` on a source where `isPlaying == true`.
3. **Kinetic Event Sound Design (`SoundConfig` & `EntityAudio`)**:
   - *Tile Cross Cadence (`OnTileCrossed` — enabled after pool wiring)*: muted mechanical click (`vol 0.08 -> 0.22`), `bypassCooldown: true`, climbing `+0st` to `+7st` across `tilesTraveled 1..12` (suppressed while `movement.IsPhasing`, where a sustained bandpass phase-hum loops instead).
   - *Layered Wall Impact (`EntityAudio`)*: synchronize tier selection with `HandleWallImpact` / `HandleStopped` (reading `movement.CurrentTier` on `OnWallImpact` prior to `StopMoving` clearing it, or reading `LastImpactTier` consistently inside stop handlers) across three distinct tiers: `Drift` crisp clack, `Cruise` dual-layer thud + ceramic snap, `KineticRam` `55Hz` sub-drop + fracture + metallic spark decay.
   - *Combo Redirect Harmonic Chain*: `DeflectionWedge` and `VectorPad` chimes climb `+1` semitone per consecutive redirect (clamped at `+8`, matching `maxConsecutiveRedirects = 8`), resetting on stop or fresh idle launch.
   - *Graze & Buffer Clear*: `CloseCallDetector.OnCloseCallTriggered` triggers sharp filtered air-whip (`vol 0.55`, `±4%` pitch jitter); `PlayerMovement.OnDirectionBufferCleared` fires a soft muted detent click (`vol 0.20`).

### Pillar 4: Pivot-Compensated Tile Micro-Choreography, Hazard Anticipation & Runtime-Isolated Post-Processing

**Why**: Kinetic movement feels empty if walls, pads, switches, and camera lenses stay inert, or if world sprites lift off their grid tiles when squashing. Simultaneously, atmospheric punch cannot come at the cost of dirtying project `.asset` files.  
**Trade-off**: Centralizing bounds-center scale compensation in one lightweight static helper eliminates duplicate math across tiles and hazards while supporting rotated `Visual` children cleanly.

#### Technical Design & C# Hooks
1. **Shared Pivot-Compensated Scale Helper (`Assets/Scripts/Feedback/VisualPivotScaleHelper.cs`)**:
   - Static utility encapsulating `PivotScaleContext` (`Transform target`, `Vector3 baseLocalPos`, `Quaternion baseLocalRot`, `Vector3 baseLocalScale`, `Vector3 spriteLocalCenter`).
   - `Capture(Transform target, SpriteRenderer sr)` reads `sr.sprite.bounds.center` (computed strictly in sprite local coordinates).
   - `ApplyScale(ref PivotScaleContext ctx, Vector3 targetScale)` sets:  
     `ctx.target.localScale = targetScale;`  
     `ctx.target.localPosition = ctx.baseLocalPos + ctx.baseLocalRot * Vector3.Scale(ctx.baseLocalScale - targetScale, ctx.spriteLocalCenter);`
   - `Restore(in PivotScaleContext ctx)` snaps exact baseline `localScale` and `localPosition` on completion or `OnDisable()`.
   - Eliminates vertical/lateral drift across every bottom- or negative-pivoted tile/hazard (`Wedge`, `CircuitSwitch`, `PulseLaser`, `OverloadedFloor`, `FailingTile`, and rotated `VectorPad`/`VectorLauncher`).
2. **Tile Reaction & Hazard Anticipation Scripts**:
   - **`TileReactionFeedback.cs`** (on `Wedge`, `VectorPad`, `VectorLauncher`, `CircuitSwitch`): drives snappy `VisualPivotScaleHelper` recoil (`0.05s` squash `0.85x` along trigger vector, `0.16s` `Ease.OutBack` settle) and invokes `VfxSpawner.Spawn(burstPrefab, ...)` using pre-authored prefabs.
   - **`HazardPhaseFeedback.cs`** (on `PulseLaser`, `OverloadedFloor`, `FailingTile`, `HazardActivation`): cleanly separates *Wind-Up* (`0.20s` pre-activation high-frequency quiver + sprite tint warn via `VisualPivotScaleHelper`), *Engage* (`1.18x` punch + beam/discharge VFX), and *Cool-Down* restore. On `FailingTile`, scales micro-jitter frequency with remaining collapse fraction.
   - **`CrackedBlock` Pre-Cracks & `Exit` Breath**: `Tier 1/2` non-breaking bumps trigger a brief `VisualPivotScaleHelper` shudder + dust burst via `VfxSpawner`; `Tier 3` shatter pairs directional debris VFX with Pillar 1/2 flash + Hit-Stop.
3. **Runtime-Isolated Multi-Channel Post-Processing (`KineticPostProcessFeedback.cs`)**:
   - **Disk-Safe Profile Isolation**: In `Awake()`, retrieve Unity's lazy runtime clone via `runtimeProfile = globalVolume.profile;` (never modify `globalVolume.sharedProfile`), track ownership, and call `Destroy(runtimeProfile)` inside `OnDestroy()` so `Assets/Volume/Global Volume Profile.asset` (`ChromaticAberration = 0.2`, `Bloom = 9`, `Vignette = 0.4`) stays untouched on disk and across scene reloads.
   - Cache `runtimeProfile.TryGet(out chromaticAberration)` and `TryGet(out vignette)`, ensure `.active = true` and `.intensity.overrideState = true`, and record `baseChromatic` (`0.2f`) and `baseVignette` (`0.4f`).
   - **Non-Clobbering Channel Compositing**: Maintain independent tweened float channels (`tierChromaticFloor`, `impactChromatic`, `grazeChromatic`) updated via `DOTween.To` (`.DOKill()`, `.SetUpdate(true)`, `.SetLink(gameObject)`) into `ApplyPostProcess()`:
     - `chromaticAberration.intensity.value = Mathf.Clamp01(baseChromatic + tierChromaticFloor + Mathf.Max(impactChromatic, grazeChromatic));`
     - `vignette.intensity.value = Mathf.Clamp01(baseVignette + tierVignetteOffset + impactVignette);`
   - `PulseGraze()` drives `grazeChromatic` (`0 -> +0.18f` over `0.02s`, `Ease.OutCubic` decay over `0.12s`); `KineticRam` impact drives `impactChromatic` (`0 -> +0.38f` over `0.02s`, `Ease.OutCubic` decay over `0.18s`). Overlapping triggers compose monotonically without snapping each other downward.

### Pillar 5: Haptic Vocabulary, Near-Miss Graze Reward Loop & Input Clarity

**Why**: Rapid tactile feedback closes the loop between narrow survival (`CloseCallDetector`), velocity escalation, and intentional buffered-turn reversals without violating burst cooldown ceilings.

#### Technical Design & C# Hooks
