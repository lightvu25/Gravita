# Gravita (Puzzle 2D) — Game Feel Signature Blueprint

## 0. Assumptions & Design Guardrails
- **Velocity Tier Thresholds**: `Assets/Scripts/Gameplay/VelocityTier.cs` maps discrete crossed-tile counts via `VelocityTierUtil.FromTiles` into `Drift`, `Cruise`, and `KineticRam` (while higher-level design notes use `1–3` / `4–7` / `8+`, live thresholds live solely in `VelocityTierUtil.FromTiles`). All feedback hooks below bind exclusively to `PlayerMovement.CurrentTier`, `OnVelocityTierChanged`, and `OnTileCrossed` — never hardcoded tile counts — so tuning `VelocityTierUtil.FromTiles` automatically scales every audio, camera, and VFX threshold.
- **Audio Assets**: `Assets/Data/Sound/SoundConfig.asset` currently ships with empty `clips: []` entries. All audio code hooks assume clips are populated in `SoundConfig.asset` (`Assets/Audio/`).
- **Determinism & Time-Scale Separation**: `ProjectSettings/TimeManager.asset` runs a discrete `Fixed Timestep = 0.02s` (`moveSpeed = 30` units/sec = `0.6` tiles/tick; `48` units/sec = `0.96` tiles/tick during `1.6x` Phase Launch), and `LevelClock` drives timed hazards deterministically. **Only `PlayerImpactFeedback` modifies `Time.timeScale`**, exposing `public static bool IsAnyHitStopActive { get; private set; }` so unscaled feedback animates cleanly through Hit-Stop (`Time.timeScale == 0f && IsAnyHitStopActive`) while freezing or clearing cleanly in the Pause Menu (`Time.timeScale == 0f && !IsAnyHitStopActive`).
- **Scene-Contract & DOTween Discipline**: Zero `new GameObject(...)` or runtime `AddComponent<...>()` calls anywhere in gameplay or feedback scripts; all pooled visuals spawn/return via `VfxSpawner` / `ObjectPoolManager` and scene refs use `[SerializeField]` Inspector fields. Every tween/sequence calls `.DOKill()` before creation, `.SetUpdate(true)`, `.SetLink(gameObject)` (or `LinkBehaviour.KillOnDisable`), and resets in `OnDisable()`.
- **Accessibility & Speedrun Guards**: Hit-stop, camera trauma/zoom, chromatic aberration, and vignette flashes expose Inspector toggles (`hitStopEnabled`, `photosensitivitySafeMode`, trauma/zoom multipliers) so "Speedrun Minimal" and "Photosensitivity Safe" modes degrade gracefully to crisp non-flashing cues.

## 1. Core Game Feel Diagnosis: Why Gravita Feels "Flat" Today

Inspecting `Assets/Scenes/GameScene.unity`, `Assets/Scenes/Bootstrap.unity`, `Assets/Prefabs/Manager/AudioSystems.prefab`, gameplay prefabs, and live C# scripts exposes five concrete breaks making a `30 u/s` inertia puzzle feel weightless:

1. **Camera Pipeline Suppresses Zoom & Freezes Hit-Stop Shake**:
   - `CinemachineCameraZoom2D` (`normalOrthographicSize = 5.5` in `GameScene.unity`) has zero callers and lerps on scaled `Time.deltaTime`.
   - Worse, `CinemachineCamera` has `CinemachinePixelPerfect` enabled (`m_Enabled: 1`) and `Main Camera` has `PixelPerfectCamera` enabled (`m_Enabled: 1`, `320x180`, `32 PPU`), snapping `OrthographicSize` post-pipeline and overriding subtle fractional zoom steps (`5.50f -> 5.95f` and `PunchZoom`).
   - Meanwhile `Main Camera`'s `CinemachineBrain` has `IgnoreTimeScale = 0` (`UpdateMethod = LateUpdate`), and `CinemachineCameraShake2D` leaves `CinemachineImpulseManager.Instance.IgnoreTimeScale = false`, freezing camera shake propagation and lens interpolation during every `Time.timeScale = 0f` Hit-Stop.
2. **Broken Audio Pool & BGM Pitch Contamination**:
   - In both `Bootstrap.unity` and `AudioSystems.prefab`, `SoundManager.pooledAudioSourcePrefab` and `fallbackAudioSource` are unset (`null`), so `SoundManager.InitializePool()` spawns zero voices regardless of `initialPoolSize`.
   - Worse, in `Bootstrap.unity`, `SoundManager.InitializePool()` sweeps `GetComponentsInChildren<AudioSource>(true)` on root `AudioSystems`, absorbing `MusicManager.musicSource` into `sfxPool[0]` under `sfxMixerGroup` and mutating active BGM `pitch` (`up to 2.52x`) via `PlayOneShot`.
3. **Mid-Slide Sensory Vacuum (`0.6` Tiles per `0.02s` Tick)**:
   - `PlayerMovement.OnTileCrossed` has zero subscribers, leaving corridors silent and visually flat outside `PlayerLineTrail` (which clamps `smoothedMoveSpeed` to `28f` at `PlayerLineTrail.cs:292`, below `30f` normal and `48f` Phase Launch speeds, and aborts `LateUpdate` when `Time.deltaTime <= 0f`).
4. **Squash Timing, Stale Tiers, Dead Hops & Silent Tile Prefabs**:
   - `PlayerSquashStretch` caps `maxDeformation = 0.3` (clipping the authored `28x16` impact silhouette), eases into squash over `0.088s` (outlasting `0.033s–0.083s` Hit-Stops so time resumes before peak compression lands), bounces mid-slide on `OnVelocityTierChanged`, and lacks general `(targetAnchorY - pivotNormY)` compensation across ceiling/horizontal impacts.
   - `GameScene.unity` zeroes `tier1Shake = 0` and `redirectShake = 0`. `EntityAudio` and `GameplayHapticsFeedback` read `LastImpactTier` inside `OnWallImpact` — which fires *before* `StopMoving()` latches `LastImpactTier` — playing the *previous* dash's tier. `Cracked.prefab` (`WallImpactBreak.prefab` cone emits `+Z` with `alignToInteractionDirection = false`), `Brake.prefab`, `Wedge.prefab`, `Exit.prefab`, `Hazard.prefab`, and `StarCollectFeedback` are silent or misaligned.
5. **Unwired Signature Systems & Runtime `GameObject` Leaks**:
   - `CloseCallDetector.OnCloseCallTriggered` has zero subscribers and reuses `Particle Star.prefab`. `Assets/Volume/Global Volume Profile.asset` is static. `PlayerMovement.HasPendingDirection` and `IsAwaitingFreshInput` have no cues. `PowerUpController` builds `SpawnRadialPulseVFX`, `SpawnDirectionalScanOrBurstVFX`, and `SpawnEchoVisual` (`echoVisualPrefab == null` in `GameScene.unity`) via procedural `new GameObject` + `AddComponent<SpriteRenderer>()`.

## 2. Five Gravita-Specific Game Feel Pillars

### Pillar 1: Kinetic Runway Escalation (Mid-Slide Momentum)

#### Why It Makes Gravita Uniquely Addictive
In stop-to-stop inertia puzzles, planning 5–8 tile slides feels rewarding only when crossing distance visibly and audibly accelerates. Converting each grid cell into an ascending harmonic tick, holding an aerodynamic stretch until wall stop, subtly pulling out camera ortho per tier, and shedding HDR magenta/violet afterimages at `KineticRam` / `Phasing` creates an unmistakable **"ignition rush"**.

#### Exact Specs, Curves & Inspector Parameters
- **Per-Tile Kinetic Tick (`OnTileCrossed`)**:
  - Suppressed whenever `movement.IsPhasing` or during a zero-distance `VectorLauncher` jump (`tiles > lastNotifiedTiles + 1`).
  - Semitone curve: `pitch = 0.88f * Mathf.Pow(1.059463f, Mathf.Min(tiles - 1, 12))` (`0.88x -> 1.76x`, +1 octave), volume `Mathf.Lerp(0.35f, 0.85f, Mathf.Clamp01(tiles / 8f))`.
  - **`0.02s` Fixed-Timestep Safe Cadence**: At `moveSpeed = 30` and `Fixed Timestep = 0.02s` (`0.6` tiles/tick), integer crossings land `0.04s, 0.04s, 0.02s` apart, and render-frame catch-up can place consecutive `FixedUpdate` steps `< 0.015s` apart in unscaled time. Calls `SoundManager.PlaySFX(..., bypassCooldown: true)` and coalesces only same-physics-tick duplicates via `Mathf.Abs(Time.fixedUnscaledTime - lastTickFixedTime) < 0.015f` while guarding `tiles == lastNotifiedTiles + 1`.
- **Camera Runway Pull-Out & Time-Scale Unlock (`Main Camera` + `CinemachineCameraZoom2D` + `CinemachineCameraShake2D`)**:
  - Disable `CinemachinePixelPerfect` (`CinemachineCamera`) and `PixelPerfectCamera` (`Main Camera`) in `GameScene.unity` (textures stay crisp via `FilterMode.Point` and `32 PPU` asset settings), and enable `CinemachineBrain.IgnoreTimeScale = true` on `Main Camera`.
  - `Drift` (`baseOrtho = 5.50f`) -> `Cruise` (`5.68f`, `zoomSpeed = 6f`) -> `KineticRam` (`5.88f`, `zoomSpeed = 7f`) -> `Phasing` (`5.95f`, `zoomSpeed = 8f`).
  - In `CinemachineCameraShake2D.OnEnable()`, set `CinemachineImpulseManager.Instance.IgnoreTimeScale = true` once (never toggled mid-impulse because `CinemachineImpulseManager.CurrentTime` switches between `Time.realtimeSinceStartup` and `CinemachineCore.CurrentTime`). Guard `ShakeCamera(float force)` with `if (Time.timeScale <= 0f && !PlayerImpactFeedback.IsAnyHitStopActive) return;`, and in `Update()` clear paused residue (`if (Time.timeScale <= 0f && !PlayerImpactFeedback.IsAnyHitStopActive) CinemachineImpulseManager.Instance.Clear();`).
- **Sustained Aerodynamic Stretch (`PlayerSquashStretch`)**:
  - Holds deformed scale **until `OnMoveStopped`**: `Drift` (`+8% / -4%`, `0.05s`), `Cruise` (`+14% / -7%`, `0.06s`), `KineticRam` (`+20% / -10%`, `0.06s`), `Ease.OutQuad`, `.SetUpdate(true)`, center-anchored (`targetAnchorY = 0.5f`).
- **Kinetic Ram & Phase Afterimages (`KineticAfterimage.prefab` + `PooledAfterimage.cs`)**:
  - Active only while `CurrentTier == VelocityTier.KineticRam || IsPhasing`. Spawn interval `0.032s` unscaled (~1 ghost/tile at `30 u/s`, pool prewarm `10`), ghost duration `0.16s` unscaled, `_FlashColor` HDR fade (`KineticRam` `#FF52C7` `a = 0.62`, `Phasing` `#B86BFF` `a = 0.70`, `Ease.OutQuad`), scale `1.0 -> 0.86` (`Ease.InQuad`).
- **URP Volume Escalation (`VolumePulseFeedback` on `Global Volume`)**:
  - `KineticRam`: `ChromaticAberration.intensity` `0.20f -> 0.40f` (`0.08s`), `Bloom.intensity` `9.0f -> 11.2f` (`0.08s`), returning smoothly over `0.14s` on stop.
- **`PlayerLineTrail` Fix**:
  - Raise speed clamp `Mathf.Clamp(instSpeed, 4f, 28f)` (`PlayerLineTrail.cs:292`) to `Mathf.Clamp(instSpeed, 4f, 60f)` (`30` slide & `48` Phase Launch) and read `float dt = PlayerImpactFeedback.IsAnyHitStopActive ? Time.unscaledDeltaTime : Time.deltaTime;` so trail retraction stays smooth during Hit-Stop and frozen while paused.

Calls `transform.DOKill(); DOTween.Kill(this);`, runs `.SetTarget(this).SetUpdate(true).SetLink(gameObject, LinkBehaviour.KillOnDisable)` scale + `_FlashColor.a -> 0f` fade, and returns via `ObjectPoolManager.ReturnObjectToPool(gameObject)`. `OnDisable()` kills tweens and clears `SetPropertyBlock(null)`.
- **New `Assets/Scripts/Feedback/KineticRunwayFeedback.cs`** (on `Player`):
  - Serialized: `[SerializeField] private PlayerMovement movement;`, `[SerializeField] private SpriteRenderer playerSpriteRenderer;`, `[SerializeField] private Transform playerVisualTransform;`, `[SerializeField] private GameObject afterimagePrefab;`, `[SerializeField] private VolumePulseFeedback volumePulse;`, `[SerializeField] private SoundConfig soundConfig;`.

### Pillar 2: Unscaled Elastic Impact & Magnetic Anchor Choreography

#### Why It Makes Gravita Uniquely Addictive
With `bufferTurnsUntilStop = true`, every stop marks a deliberate tactical beat. Landing full compression *inside* Hit-Stop, distinguishing solid walls vs breakable `CrackedBlock` slams vs magnetic `GravityBlock`/`BrakeTile` locks, and keeping hazard deaths / level exits crisp makes every tile surface immediately readable through touch alone.

#### Exact Specs, Curves & Inspector Parameters
- **Snappy Unscaled 2-Stage Impact Squash (`PlayerSquashStretch`)**:
  - Increase `maxDeformation = 0.45f` in `GameScene.unity` (unlocks authored `28x16` deformation).
  - Stage 1 (`0.030s`, `Ease.OutExpo`, `.SetUpdate(true)`): instant peak squash inside Hit-Stop (`Tier 1`: `60%` deform, `wallPressPx = 2.5f`; `Tier 2`: `85%`, `4.0f`; `Tier 3`: `100%`, `5.0f`).
  - Stage 2 (`0.145s`, `Ease.OutBack(1.35f)`, `.SetUpdate(true)`): elastic spring back to `baseScale` / `baseLocalPosition`.
  - **General Anchor-Preserving Pivot Y Compensation**: Compute `spriteWorldHeight = (sprite.rect.height / sprite.pixelsPerUnit) * baseScale.y` and `pivotNormY = sprite.pivot.y / sprite.rect.height` dynamically per equipped sprite (`sy = deformedScale.y / baseScale.y`):
    - Upward impact (`pressDirection.y > 0.5f`): `targetAnchorY = 1f` (pins top to ceiling).
    - Downward impact (`pressDirection.y < -0.5f`): `targetAnchorY = 0f` (pins bottom to floor).
    - Horizontal impacts, mid-slide stretches, and center-anchored anchor locks: `targetAnchorY = 0.5f`.
    - Apply: `localPos.y = baseLocalPosition.y + (targetAnchorY - pivotNormY) * spriteWorldHeight * (1f - sy) + pressDirection.y * (wallPressPx / 32f)`.
- **Tiered Trauma & Order-Independent `CrackedBlock` / `Breaker` Coalescing (`PlayerImpactFeedback`)**:
  - `Tier 1 (Drift)` Wall Stop: `tier1Shake = 0.045f`, `tier1HitStop = 0f`.
  - `Tier 2 (Cruise)` Wall Stop: `tier2Shake = 0.12f`, `tier2HitStop = 0.033f`, `PunchZoom(-0.10f, 0.14f)`.
  - `Tier 3 (KineticRam)` Solid Wall Stop: `tier3Shake = 0.24f`, `tier3HitStop = 0.067f`, `PunchZoom(-0.18f, 0.16f)`.
  - `CrackedBlock` Break: `blockBreakShake = 0.28f`, `blockBreakHitStop = 0.055f`, `PunchZoom(-0.26f, 0.18f)`, `HapticEvent.KineticImpact`. Breaking up to 2 walls in a single `Breaker` pulse spawns per-block directional debris on each block while coalescing screen shake, Hit-Stop, zoom punch, and haptics to **one unified impulse per frame** (never `0.56f` stacking).
- **Magnetic Anchor Lock (`GravityBlock` / `BrakeTile` Only — Excluded on Death, Exit & Teleport)**:
  - **Implosion Pulse**: `0.04s` shrink to `(0.84, 0.84, 1)` (`Ease.OutQuad`, `targetAnchorY = 0.5f`, `.SetUpdate(true)`), `0.12s` spring to `(1.06, 1.06, 1)` -> `(1, 1, 1)` (`Ease.OutBack`), `anchorShake = 0.05f`, zero Hit-Stop (`0f`), `SoundConfig.gravityAnchor`.
  - Never fires on `PlayerMovement.Freeze()` (hazard death / `LevelExit`) or `TeleportTo()`.

### Pillar 3: Harmonic Bank-Shot & Corridor Arpeggio

#### Why It Makes Gravita Uniquely Addictive
Chaining `DeflectionWedge` bank-shots through coin lines into a `CrackedBlock` or `VectorLauncher` is Gravita's flow peak. Deterministic major-pentatonic pitch chains that reset only when Subject G-0 stops turn long routes into tight, legible musical phrases, while Z-tilt whips and clean Phase Launch telemetry make rebounds razor-sharp.

#### Exact Specs, Curves & Inspector Parameters
- **Wedge / `VectorPad` Harmonic Escalation (`OnRedirected`)**:
  - Major pentatonic steps: `int[] { 0, 2, 4, 7, 9, 12, 14, 16 }` (`redirectChainCount` increments per redirect, resets to `0` on `OnMoveStopped`).
  - Pitch: `Mathf.Pow(1.059463f, Pentatonic[Mathf.Min(chain, 7)])` (`1.00x -> 2.52x`); volume `Mathf.Min(1f, 0.72f + chain * 0.04f)`; `bypassCooldown = true`.
- **Corridor Coin Arpeggio (`Collectible.OnAnyCoinCollected`)**:
  - `coinChainCount` resets on `OnMoveStopped`, climbs the same pentatonic scale capped at `+12` semitones (`2.00x`) with `bypassCooldown = true`.
- **Snap-Bank Tilt & Shake**:
  - `PlayerImpactFeedback.HandleRedirected`: `ApplyUnstackedCameraShake(redirectShake + 0.015f * Mathf.Min(redirectChainCount, 4))` (`0.07f -> 0.13f`).
  - `PlayerSquashStretch.SnapRedirect(Vector2 oldDir, Vector2 newDir)`: `0.09s` unscaled Z-tilt whip (`±9°` signed via `oldDir.x * newDir.y - oldDir.y * newDir.x`, recovering to `0°` `Ease.OutBack`) + sustained `+14% / -7%` stretch along `newDir` with `targetAnchorY = 0.5f`.
- **Phase Launch (`VectorLauncher` Flight Only — Decoupled From `TeleportTo` / `Freeze`)**:
  - Launch (`OnPhasingChanged(true)`): `PunchZoom(-0.22f, 0.12f)`, `VolumePulseFeedback.PulsePhaseLaunch()` (`ChromaticAberration` `0.20f -> 0.52f` over `0.10s`), `SoundConfig.phaseLaunchStart`.
  - Flight Landing (`PlayerMovement.OnPhaseLaunchLanded`): `ApplyUnstackedCameraShake(0.16f)` + `SoundConfig.phaseLaunchLand` (never triggered if `TeleportTo` or `Freeze` interrupts mid-air).

#### Exact C# Integration Hooks
- **`PlayerMovement.cs` (Clean Phase Launch Ordering & Dedicated Landing Event)**:
  - Expose `public event Action OnPhaseLaunchLanded;` and `public bool IsZeroDistancePhaseLaunch { get; private set; }`.
  - In `StartPhaseLaunch(Vector2 landingPosition, Vector2 postLaunchDirection, VelocityTier minTier)`:
    - Compute `bool willPhase = (landingPosition - origin).sqrMagnitude > 0.0001f;`.
