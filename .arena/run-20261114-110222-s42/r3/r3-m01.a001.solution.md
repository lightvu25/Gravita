# Gravita (Puzzle 2D) — Game Feel Signature Blueprint

## 0. Assumptions & Design Guardrails
- **Velocity Tier Thresholds**: `Assets/Scripts/Gameplay/VelocityTier.cs` maps discrete crossed-tile counts via `VelocityTierUtil.FromTiles` into `Drift` (`1–2` tiles), `Cruise` (`3–4` tiles), and `KineticRam` (`5+` tiles; higher-level design notes use `1–3` / `4–7` / `8+`, while live runtime thresholds live solely in `VelocityTierUtil.FromTiles`). All feedback hooks below bind exclusively to `PlayerMovement.CurrentTier`, `OnVelocityTierChanged`, and `OnTileCrossed` — never hardcoded tier tile counts — so tuning `VelocityTierUtil.FromTiles` automatically scales every audio, camera, and VFX threshold.
- **Audio Assets**: `Assets/Data/Sound/SoundConfig.asset` currently ships with empty `clips: []` entries. All audio code hooks assume clips are populated in `SoundConfig.asset` (`Assets/Audio/`).
- **Determinism & Time-Scale Coordination**: `ProjectSettings/TimeManager.asset` runs a discrete `Fixed Timestep = 0.02s` (`moveSpeed = 30` units/sec in `GameScene.unity` = `0.6` tiles/tick; `48` units/sec = `0.96` tiles/tick during `1.6x` Phase Launch), and `LevelClock` drives timed hazards deterministically. `UIManager` owns modal pause `Time.timeScale` (`Pause`, `Result`, `Revive`, `Multiplayer`, and `BlindBox` serialize `freezeTime: 1`), `CutsceneManager` / `GameManager` restore `Time.timeScale = 1f` on cutscene/scene teardown, and `PlayerImpactFeedback` owns transient combat Hit-Stop (`public static bool IsAnyHitStopActive { get; private set; }`), **strictly subordinate to `UIManager.IsTimeFrozenByModal`** so modal popups cancel Hit-Stop cleanly without ever unfreezing behind an open menu.
- **Scene-Contract & DOTween Discipline**: Zero `new GameObject(...)` or runtime `AddComponent<...>()` calls anywhere in gameplay or feedback scripts; all pooled visuals spawn and return via `VfxSpawner` / `ObjectPoolManager` (`ReturnObjectToPool`), and scene references use `[SerializeField]` Inspector fields. Every tween/sequence kills prior tweens before creation, sets `.SetUpdate(true)` and `.SetLink(gameObject)` (or `LinkBehaviour.KillOnDisable`), and resets state in `OnDisable()`.
- **Accessibility & Speedrun Guards**: Hit-stop, camera trauma/zoom, chromatic aberration, and vignette flashes expose Inspector toggles (`hitStopEnabled`, `photosensitivitySafeMode`, trauma/zoom multipliers) so "Speedrun Minimal" and "Photosensitivity Safe" modes degrade gracefully to crisp non-flashing cues.

## 1. Core Game Feel Diagnosis: Why Gravita Feels "Flat" Today

Inspecting `Assets/Scenes/GameScene.unity`, `Assets/Scenes/Bootstrap.unity`, `Assets/Prefabs/Manager/AudioSystems.prefab`, gameplay prefabs, and live C# scripts exposes five concrete breaks making a `30 u/s` inertia puzzle feel weightless:

1. **Camera Pipeline Suppresses Zoom & Freezes Hit-Stop Shake**:
   - `CinemachineCameraZoom2D` (`normalOrthographicSize = 5.5` in `GameScene.unity`) has zero callers and lerps on scaled `Time.deltaTime`.
   - `CinemachineCamera` has `CinemachinePixelPerfect` enabled (`m_Enabled: 1`) and `Main Camera` has `PixelPerfectCamera` enabled (`m_Enabled: 1`, `320x180`, `32 PPU`), snapping `OrthographicSize` post-pipeline and overriding subtle fractional zoom steps (`5.50f -> 5.95f` and `PunchZoom`).
   - `Main Camera`'s `CinemachineBrain` has `IgnoreTimeScale = 0` (`UpdateMethod = LateUpdate`), and `CinemachineCameraShake2D` leaves `CinemachineImpulseManager.Instance.IgnoreTimeScale = false`, freezing camera shake propagation and lens interpolation during every `Time.timeScale = 0f` Hit-Stop.
2. **Broken Audio Pool & BGM Pitch Contamination**:
   - In both `Bootstrap.unity` and `AudioSystems.prefab`, `SoundManager.pooledAudioSourcePrefab` and `fallbackAudioSource` are unset (`null`), so `SoundManager.InitializePool()` spawns zero voices regardless of `initialPoolSize` (and `Bootstrap.unity` leaves `SoundManager.soundConfig` unassigned).
   - Worse, in `Bootstrap.unity`, `SoundManager.InitializePool()` sweeps `GetComponentsInChildren<AudioSource>(true)` on root `AudioSystems`, absorbing `MusicManager.musicSource` into `sfxPool[0]` under `sfxMixerGroup` and mutating active BGM `pitch` (`up to 2.52x`) via `PlayOneShot`.
3. **Mid-Slide Sensory Vacuum (`0.6` Tiles per `0.02s` Tick)**:
   - `PlayerMovement.OnTileCrossed` has zero subscribers, leaving corridors silent and visually flat outside `PlayerLineTrail` (which clamps `smoothedMoveSpeed` to `28f` at `PlayerLineTrail.cs:292`, below `30f` normal and `48f` Phase Launch speeds, and aborts `LateUpdate` when `Time.deltaTime <= 0f`).
4. **Squash Timing, Stale Tiers, Unbranched Stops & Silent Tile Prefabs**:
   - `GameScene.unity` caps `PlayerSquashStretch.maxDeformation = 0.3` (clipping the authored `28x16` impact silhouette), eases into squash over `0.088s` (outlasting `0.033s–0.083s` Hit-Stops so time resumes before peak compression lands), bounces mid-slide on `OnVelocityTierChanged`, and lacks general `(targetAnchorY - pivotNormY)` compensation across ceiling/horizontal impacts for `Sprite` (`localPosition.y = -0.5` bottom-center pivot).
   - `GameScene.unity` zeroes `tier1Shake = 0` and `redirectShake = 0`. `PlayerImpactFeedback.HandleStopped()` triggers wall-slam shake/Hit-Stop unconditionally without checking `WasLastStopWallImpact`. `EntityAudio` reads `LastImpactTier` inside `OnWallImpact` — which fires *before* `StopMoving()` latches `LastImpactTier` — playing the *previous* dash's tier. `Cracked.prefab` (`WallImpactBreak.prefab` cone emits `+Z` with `alignToInteractionDirection = false`), `Brake.prefab`, `Wedge.prefab`, `Exit.prefab`, `Hazard.prefab`, and `StarCollectFeedback` are silent or misaligned.
5. **Unwired Signature Systems & Runtime `GameObject` Leaks**:
   - `CloseCallDetector.OnCloseCallTriggered` has zero subscribers and reuses `Particle Star.prefab`. `Assets/Volume/Global Volume Profile.asset` is static. `PlayerMovement.HasPendingDirection` and `IsAwaitingFreshInput` have no cues. `PowerUpController` builds `SpawnRadialPulseVFX`, `SpawnDirectionalScanOrBurstVFX`, and `SpawnEchoVisual` (`echoVisualPrefab == null` in `GameScene.unity`) via procedural `new GameObject` + `AddComponent<SpriteRenderer>()` + `Texture2D`/`Sprite.Create`.

## 2. Five Gravita-Specific Game Feel Pillars

### Pillar 1: Kinetic Runway Escalation (Mid-Slide Momentum)

#### Why It Makes Gravita Uniquely Addictive
In stop-to-stop inertia puzzles, planning long slides feels rewarding only when crossing distance visibly and audibly accelerates. Converting each grid cell into an ascending harmonic tick, holding an aerodynamic stretch until wall stop, smoothly widening the camera ortho lens per tier, and shedding HDR magenta/violet afterimages at `KineticRam` / `Phasing` creates an unmistakable **"ignition rush"**.

#### Exact Specs, Curves & Inspector Parameters
- **Per-Tile Kinetic Tick (`OnTileCrossed`)**:
  - Semitone curve: `pitch = 0.88f * Mathf.Pow(1.059463f, Mathf.Min(tiles - 1, 12))` (`0.88x -> 1.76x`, +1 octave), volume `Mathf.Lerp(0.35f, 0.85f, Mathf.Clamp01(tiles / 8f))`.
  - **Launch-Safe Cadence**: Always updates `int prev = lastNotifiedTiles; lastNotifiedTiles = tiles;` *before* checking suppression (`movement.IsPhasing || movement.IsLauncherRedirectActive || tiles > prev + 1 || tiles <= prev`), so `EnforceMinimumTier` jumps (`0 -> 5` or `1 -> 5`) and `VectorLauncher` phasing flights never poison `lastNotifiedTiles` or mute subsequent ground tiles (`6, 7, 8...`). Calls `SoundManager.PlaySFX(..., bypassCooldown: true)` and coalesces same-physics-tick duplicates via `Mathf.Abs(Time.fixedUnscaledTime - lastTickFixedTime) < 0.015f`.
- **Camera Runway Pull-Out & Centralized Time-Scale Unlock (`Main Camera` + `CinemachineCameraZoom2D` + `CinemachineCameraShake2D`)**:
  - Disable `CinemachinePixelPerfect` (`CinemachineCamera`) and `PixelPerfectCamera` (`Main Camera`) in `GameScene.unity` (textures stay crisp via `FilterMode.Point` and `32 PPU` asset settings), and set `CinemachineBrain.IgnoreTimeScale = true` on `Main Camera`.
  - Runway targets relative to `normalOrthographicSize = 5.50f`: `Drift/None` (`5.50f`, `stopReturnZoomSpeed = 6f`) -> `Cruise` (`+0.18f -> 5.68f`, `zoomSpeed = 6f`) -> `KineticRam` (`+0.38f -> 5.88f`, `zoomSpeed = 7f`) -> `Phasing` (`+0.45f -> 5.95f`, `zoomSpeed = 8f`).
  - **Centralized Per-Frame Impulse Coalescing (`CinemachineCameraShake2D`)**: `OnEnable()` sets `CinemachineImpulseManager.Instance.IgnoreTimeScale = true` once; `Update()` clears impulses while modal-paused (`UIManager.IsTimeFrozenByModal || (Time.timeScale <= 0f && !PlayerImpactFeedback.IsAnyHitStopActive)`); `ShakeCamera(float force)` enforces **one peak impulse per `Time.frameCount`** across every caller (`PlayerImpactFeedback`, `InteractionEffectPlayer`, `CloseCallDetector`, `StarCollectFeedback`), calling `CinemachineImpulseManager.Instance.Clear()` only when a larger force supersedes an earlier same-frame impulse.
- **Sustained Aerodynamic Stretch (`PlayerSquashStretch`)**:
  - Holds deformed scale **until `OnMoveStopped`**: `Drift` (`+8% / -4%`, `0.05s`), `Cruise` (`+14% / -7%`, `0.06s`), `KineticRam` (`+20% / -10%`, `0.06s`), `Ease.OutQuad`, `.SetUpdate(true)`, center-anchored (`targetAnchorY = 0.5f`), using isolated `scaleTweenId` so mid-slide tier upgrades never interrupt an active Z-tilt recovery.
- **Kinetic Ram & Phase Afterimages (`KineticAfterimage.prefab` + `PooledAfterimage.cs`)**:
  - Active only while `CurrentTier == VelocityTier.KineticRam || IsPhasing`. Spawn interval `0.032s` unscaled (~1 ghost/tile at `30 u/s`, pool prewarm `10`), ghost duration `0.16s` unscaled, `_FlashColor` HDR fade (`KineticRam` `#FF52C7` `a = 0.62`, `Phasing` `#B86BFF` `a = 0.70`, `Ease.OutQuad`), scale `1.0 -> 0.86` (`Ease.InQuad`).
- **URP Volume Escalation (`VolumePulseFeedback` on `Global Volume`)**:
  - `KineticRam`: `ChromaticAberration.intensity` `0.20f -> 0.40f` (`0.08s`), `Bloom.intensity` `9.0f -> 11.2f` (`0.08s`), returning smoothly over `0.14s` on stop.
- **`PlayerLineTrail` Fix**:
  - Raise speed clamp `Mathf.Clamp(instSpeed, 4f, 28f)` (`PlayerLineTrail.cs:292`) to `Mathf.Clamp(instSpeed, 4f, 60f)` (`30` slide & `48` Phase Launch). In `LateUpdate()`, abort while modal-paused (`if (UIManager.Instance != null && UIManager.Instance.IsTimeFrozenByModal) return;`) and compute `float dt = PlayerImpactFeedback.IsAnyHitStopActive ? Time.unscaledDeltaTime : Time.deltaTime; if (dt <= 0f) return;` so trail retraction stays smooth during Hit-Stop and frozen under modal popups.

### Pillar 2: Unscaled Elastic Impact & Magnetic Anchor Choreography

#### Why It Makes Gravita Uniquely Addictive
With `bufferTurnsUntilStop = true`, every stop marks a deliberate tactical beat. Landing full compression *inside* Hit-Stop, distinguishing solid walls vs breakable `CrackedBlock` slams vs magnetic `GravityBlock`/`BrakeTile` locks, keeping hazard deaths / level exits / teleports completely clean of false wall-slam impulses, and honoring modal UI freeze ownership makes every tile surface immediately readable through touch alone.

#### Exact Specs, Curves & Inspector Parameters
- **Snappy Unscaled 2-Stage Impact Squash (`PlayerSquashStretch`)**:
  - Increase `maxDeformation = 0.45f` in `GameScene.unity` (unlocks authored `28x16` deformation).
  - Stage 1 (`0.030s`, `Ease.OutExpo`, `.SetUpdate(true)`): instant peak squash inside Hit-Stop (`Drift`: `60%` deform, `wallPressPx = 2.5f`; `Cruise`: `85%`, `4.0f`; `KineticRam`: `100%`, `5.0f`).
  - Stage 2 (`0.145s`, `Ease.OutBack(1.35f)`, `.SetUpdate(true)`): elastic spring back to `baseScale` / `baseLocalPosition`.
  - **General Anchor-Preserving Pivot Y Compensation**: Compute `spriteWorldHeight = (sprite.rect.height / sprite.pixelsPerUnit) * baseScale.y` and `pivotNormY = sprite.pivot.y / sprite.rect.height` dynamically per equipped sprite (`sy = deformedScale.y / baseScale.y`):
    - Upward impact (`pressDirection.y > 0.5f`): `targetAnchorY = 1f` (pins top to ceiling).
    - Downward impact (`pressDirection.y < -0.5f`): `targetAnchorY = 0f` (pins bottom to floor).
    - Horizontal impacts, mid-slide stretches, and center-anchored anchor locks: `targetAnchorY = 0.5f`.
    - Apply: `localPos.y = baseLocalPosition.y + (targetAnchorY - pivotNormY) * spriteWorldHeight * (1f - sy) + pressDirection.y * (wallPressPx / 32f)`.
- **Tiered Trauma, Modal-Safe Hit-Stop & `Breaker` Coalescing (`PlayerImpactFeedback`)**:
  - `Tier 1 (Drift)` Wall Stop: `tier1Shake = 0.045f`, `tier1HitStop = 0f`.
  - `Tier 2 (Cruise)` Wall Stop: `tier2Shake = 0.12f`, `tier2HitStop = 0.033f`, `PunchZoom(-0.10f, 0.14f)`.
  - `Tier 3 (KineticRam)` Solid Wall Stop: `tier3Shake = 0.24f`, `tier3HitStop = 0.067f`, `PunchZoom(-0.18f, 0.16f)`.
  - `CrackedBlock` Break: `blockBreakShake = 0.28f`, `blockBreakHitStop = 0.055f`, `PunchZoom(-0.26f, 0.18f)`, `HapticEvent.KineticImpact`. Breaking up to 2 walls in a single `Breaker` pulse spawns per-block directional debris on each block while coalescing Hit-Stop, zoom punch, haptics, and camera shake (via `CinemachineCameraShake2D`) to **one unified impulse per frame** (never `0.56f` stacking).
- **Magnetic Anchor Lock (`GravityBlock` / `BrakeTile` Only — Excluded on Death, Exit & Teleport)**:
  - **Implosion Pulse**: `0.04s` shrink to `(0.84, 0.84, 1)` (`Ease.OutQuad`, `targetAnchorY = 0.5f`, `.SetUpdate(true)`), `0.12s` spring to `(1.06, 1.06, 1)` -> `(1, 1, 1)` (`Ease.OutBack`), `anchorShake = 0.05f`, zero Hit-Stop (`0f`), `SoundConfig.gravityAnchor`.
  - Never fires on `PlayerMovement.Freeze()` (hazard death / `LevelExit`) or `TeleportTo()`.
