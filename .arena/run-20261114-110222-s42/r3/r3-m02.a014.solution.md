# Gravita Game Feel Signature Upgrade Blueprint

**Target Project**: `Puzzle 2D` / **Gravita** (Unity 6000.4.0f1 URP, 2D Top-Down Kinetic Momentum Puzzle Slider)  
**Architectural Mandate**: Deliver unmistakable kinetic weight, high-velocity readability, and crisp micro-moment reward loops while preserving deterministic `FixedUpdate` grid physics, zero runtime procedural hierarchy construction, single-responsibility scripts under `Assets/Scripts/Feedback/` and `Assets/Scripts/Audio/`, and clean prefab/asset integrity.

---

## 1. Core Game Feel Diagnosis: Why Gravita Currently Feels Flat

Auditing `Assets/Scripts/Gameplay/`, `Assets/Scripts/Feedback/`, `Assets/Scripts/Audio/`, `Assets/Scenes/GameScene.unity`, `Assets/Data/Sound/SoundConfig.asset`, and gameplay/particle prefabs exposes eight concrete structural bottlenecks suppressing tactile punch:

1. **Pixel-Perfect Camera Lock Quantizes Away All Dynamic Zoom**:
   - `CinemachineCameraZoom2D` exists on `CinemachineCamera` (`normalOrthographicSize: 5.5`) and is **never called anywhere in code**.
   - Even if invoked, `CinemachineCamera` runs `CinemachinePixelPerfect` (`m_Enabled: 1`) alongside `Main Camera`'s `PixelPerfectCamera` (`m_AssetsPPU: 32`, `320x180`, `m_UpscaleRT: 0`). `PixelPerfectCameraInternal.CorrectCinemachineOrthoSize` forces integer zoom ratios (`zoom * orthoSize / targetOrthoSize` rounded to an int), clamping `5.50u`, `5.68u`, `5.85u`, and `5.28u` identically to `5.625f` at `1920x1080`, `1280x720`, and `2560x1440`. Worse, disabling `CinemachinePixelPerfect` alone causes `PixelPerfectCamera.OnBeginCameraRendering` (`!m_CinemachineCompatibilityMode`) to overwrite `m_Camera.orthographicSize` down to `2.8125f` every frame.
   - Meanwhile, `CinemachinePositionComposer` uses `Damping: {1, 1, 1}` with `Lookahead.Enabled: 0`, lagging behind `30 u/s` slides.

2. **Hit-Stop Freezes Cinemachine Shake & Impact Particles**:
   - `CinemachineBrain` in `GameScene.unity` runs with `IgnoreTimeScale: 0` and `UpdateMethod: 2` (`SmartUpdate`), and `CinemachineImpulseManager.Instance.IgnoreTimeScale` defaults to `false`. Setting `Time.timeScale = 0f` in `PlayerImpactFeedback.HitStopRoutine` stalls both `CinemachineImpulseManager` and `CinemachineBrain` (which inherits `Fixed` tracking clock under `SmartUpdate` for the Rigidbody2D `Player` target), deferring wall-slam recoil until after the freeze ends.
   - Simultaneously, `WallImpactBurst.prefab`, `WallImpactBreak.prefab`, and `Particle Star.prefab` all serialize `useUnscaledTime: 0`, while `PooledParticlePlayer.cs` (`Time.deltaTime`) and `ReturnToPool.cs` (`WaitForSeconds`) run on scaled time—freezing wall sparks and shatter debris at frame 0 throughout Hit-Stop.
   - `PlayerImpactFeedback.OnDisable()` also abandons active `hitStopRoutine` without restoring `Time.timeScale` or `CinemachineBrain.UpdateMethod` on quick restarts, and `CinemachineCameraZoom2D.Update()` steps with `Time.deltaTime`.

3. **Silent Audio Catalog, Collapsed Voice Pool & Double `moveStart` on Redirects**:
   - All 23 `SoundEntry` items in `Assets/Data/Sound/SoundConfig.asset` currently serialize `clips: []`, and `SoundConfig.cs` lacks entries for `tileCrossTick`, `phaseHum`, `graze`, and `bufferClear`—leaving gameplay audio completely mute or unpitched.
   - `Assets/Prefabs/Manager/AudioSystems.prefab` configures `poolContainer: null`, `pooledAudioSourcePrefab: null`, `fallbackAudioSource: null`, and `sfxPool: - {fileID: 0}` (mirrored by a stale `sfxPool.Array.data[0]: null` override in `GameScene.unity`). `SoundManager.InitializePool()` falls back to scanning children, finding only the root `AudioSource` (`m_PlayOnAwake: 1`, shared with `RandomPitchAudio`). `SoundManager.PlaySFX(SoundEntry)` also hard-gates via `Mathf.Max(0.01f, entry.minInterval)` and `PlayResolvedClip` writes `source.pitch = pitch` even when `source.isPlaying == true`, preventing `30 u/s` (`0.033s/tile`) cadence ticks or polyphonic layers without pitch-warping active tails.
   - `PlayerMovement.Redirect` and `StepPhaseLaunch` both emit `OnMoveStarted` right after `OnRedirected` / `SetPhasing(false)`, causing `EntityAudio.HandlePlayerMoveStarted` to play `moveStart` simultaneously on top of `wedgeRedirect` or phase exit.

4. **Posture Reset on Redirects, `24x24 @ 100 PPU` Cosmetic Mismatch & Unlit Shader Absence**:
   - `PlayerSquashStretch.HandleMoveStarted` unconditionally calls `Stretch(direction)` with fixed `stretchAmount = 0.12f`—overwriting active `Cruise` or `KineticRam` elongation on every `Wedge`/`VectorPad` redirect and `StepPhaseLaunch` exit. `HandleTierChanged` pulses briefly and snaps back to `baseScale` rather than holding a sustained speed posture, `HandlePhasingChanged(true)` from a stationary `VectorLauncher` applies tint without immediate launch stretch, and `HandleMoveStopped` runs an anchor squash even when `PlayerMovement.Freeze()` locks input on win/fail.
   - `Player Icon.aseprite` imports as `32x32 @ 32 PPU` (`1.0u`, pivot `{0.5, 0}` on `Player/Sprite` at `localPosition = (0, -0.5, 0)`), whereas `Skin_Amber`, `Skin_Cobalt`, and `Skin_Prism` (`Player_Amber/Cobalt/Prism.aseprite`) import as `24x24 @ 100 PPU` (`0.24u` square, pivot `{0.5, -0.16666667}`), leaving unlocked skins miniature and recessed `0.33u–0.67u` inside the `0.9 x 0.9` collider. Furthermore, `PlayerCosmeticApplier.RefreshEquippedSkin()` provides no completion event, and `Player/Sprite` uses built-in `Sprite-Unlit-Default` (`GUID 9dfc825aed78fcd4ba02077103263b40`) without flash/rim/phase shader parameters.

5. **Root-Renderer World Prefabs & Interaction Tint/Effect Gaps**:
   - Only `VectorPad.prefab` and `VectorLauncher.prefab` separate visuals onto a `Visual` child. `Wedge.prefab`, `CircuitSwitch.prefab`, `PulseLaser.prefab`, `OverloadedFloor.prefab`, `Cracked.prefab`, and `FailingTile.prefab` mount `SpriteRenderer` directly on the prefab root alongside `BoxCollider2D` (`MazeTile.CellCenter`), preventing safe root scaling/offsetting without breaking trigger hitboxes or cell-center resolution (`GatherCellInteractionsAt`), while `HazardActivation` and `FailingTile` directly overwrite `fallbackSpriteRenderer.color`.
   - `CrackedBlock.TryCrush` returns `false` silently on `Tier 1–2` impacts without emitting `InteractionKind.Crack`; `Brake.prefab`, `Exit.prefab`, `Hazard.prefab`, and `Wedge.prefab` have empty `InteractionEffectPlayer.effectPrefabs: []`.

6. **Inconsistent Shake Ownership & Unfiltered Non-Wall Stops**:
   - `GameScene.unity` disables `PlayerImpactFeedback` `tier1Shake: 0` and `redirectShake: 0`; `Wedge.prefab` and `Brake.prefab` both set `defaultCameraShake: 0`; meanwhile `VectorPad.prefab` (`0.04`), `VectorLauncher.prefab` (`0.1`), `GravityBlock.prefab` (`0.04`), and `PulseLaser`/`OverloadedFloor` (`0.12`) set `InteractionEffectPlayer` shakes.
   - Enabling `redirectShake` in `PlayerImpactFeedback` without clearing `VectorPad.prefab`/`VectorLauncher.prefab` would double-shake redirects and launches, while `PlayerImpactFeedback.HandleStopped()` fails to filter `!movement.WasLastStopWallImpact`, firing wall Hit-Stop and wall shake on `Brake`, `GravityBlock`, and post-phase stops.

7. **Shared Volume Profile Risk & Overlapping Post-Process Tweens**:
   - `Global Volume` in `GameScene.unity` references `Assets/Volume/Global Volume Profile.asset` (`ChromaticAberration = 0.2`, `Bloom = 9`, `Vignette = 0.4`). Mutating `sharedProfile` dirties source-controlled assets on disk, and naive single-tween aberration drives overwrite or zero-snap overlapping `CloseCall` and `KineticRam` pulses.

8. **Silent Near-Miss / Reversal Moments & Narrow Haptic Taxonomy**:
   - `CloseCallDetector.OnCloseCallTriggered` has zero camera, audio, post-process, or haptic subscribers.
   - Instant reversal in `PlayerMovement.QueueDirection` drops a held perpendicular `pendingDirection` silently with no observational cue.
   - `GameHaptics` (`EventCount = 7`) maps `WallImpact` to `LightImpact` across all tiers, omits `Graze`, `TierRam`, `WallImpactHeavy`, and `BufferClear`, and drops `WallImpact` unconditionally if `Graze` fires within `GlobalMinGapSeconds = 0.03f` (`0.9u` at `30 u/s`).

## 2. Architectural Guardrails & Explicit Design Trade-Offs

- **Deterministic Physics Preservation**: `PlayerMovement.FixedUpdate`, `SimulateStep`, `moveSpeed = 30`, `VelocityTier` thresholds (`Drift` 1–2 tiles, `Cruise` 3–4, `KineticRam` 5+), `bufferTurnsUntilStop = 1`, and `maxConsecutiveRedirects = 8` remain unaltered. Additions to `PlayerMovement.cs` and `CrackedBlock.cs` are strictly observational events (`OnDirectionBufferCleared`, `InteractionKind.Crack`).
- **Zero Runtime Hierarchy Construction**: Zero `new GameObject(...)` or runtime `AddComponent<...>()` calls. All `Visual` children, `SfxPool` children, and VFX prefabs are authored in Edit Mode on prefabs/scene objects and spawned via `VfxSpawner.Spawn` / `ObjectPoolManager`.
- **DOTween Safety**: Every tween/sequence invokes `.DOKill()` before creation, `.SetUpdate(true)` to run smoothly through `Time.timeScale == 0`, and `.SetLink(gameObject)` to prevent orphan callbacks.
- **Explicit Trade-Offs Chosen**:
  1. **Analog Camera Zoom vs. Integer Pixel-Ratio Lock**: Disabling both `CinemachinePixelPerfect` and `PixelPerfectCamera` in `GameScene.unity` gives up strict integer `1/n` texel-ratio snapping (`unitsPerPixel` matrix snapping) because URP `PixelPerfectCamera` makes sub-step zoom easing mathematically impossible (`5.625f` clamp). Smooth kinetic camera breathing (`+0.18u`/`+0.35u`) and impact punch-ins (`-0.22u`) are far higher value for a `30 u/s` momentum slider.
  2. **Edit-Time `Visual` Child Extraction vs. Shader Vertex Squash**: Extracting a `Visual` child on the 6 root-renderer prefabs (`Wedge`, `CircuitSwitch`, `PulseLaser`, `OverloadedFloor`, `Cracked`, `FailingTile`) modifies 6 prefab files once in Edit Mode rather than building a vertex-deformation shader for static tiles, keeping `BoxCollider2D` and `MazeTile.CellCenter` completely immutable and letting `VisualPivotScaleHelper` work uniformly across all 8 tile/hazard prefabs.
  3. **Selective Haptics (`WallImpactHeavy` + `TierRam`) vs. Noisy Per-Tier Buzz**: Keeping `HapticEvent.WallImpact` mapped to `LightImpact` for `Drift`/`Cruise` and omitting a `TierCruise` mid-slide buzz preserves existing `HapticsTests` regression guarantees and prevents 3-tile/4-tile buzz collisions inside `GlobalMinGapSeconds = 0.03f`, reserving heavy/threshold haptics strictly for `KineticRam` (`5+` tiles).

## 3. The 5 Pillars of Gravita Game Feel

### Pillar 1: Sustained Momentum Posture, Cosmetic-Accurate Wall Flush & Unlit Kinetic Shader

**Why It Makes Gravita Addictive**: Subject G-0 should visually feel like a compressed projectile gaining kinetic energy down every corridor—elongating progressively across `Drift -> Cruise -> KineticRam`, snapping cleanly through chained `Wedge`/`VectorPad` corners without losing posture, and slamming flush against walls across every cosmetic skin.

#### Exact Specs & C# Integration
1. **Sustained Flight Posture & Redirect Preservation (`Assets/Scripts/Feedback/PlayerSquashStretch.cs`)**:
   - Replace transient `Play(...)` bounce-back during active flight with `HoldDeform(Vector3 targetScale, float duration, Ease ease)` (which eases to `targetScale` and **holds** the kinetic stretch while sliding) and `SquashAndRecover(...)` on stops/impulses.
   - Compute tier scale from live velocity tier helper `GetFlightScale(VelocityTier tier, Vector2 dir)`:
     - `None`: `baseScale` `(1, 1, 1)`
     - `Drift` (1–2 tiles): `(1.12, 0.88)` along travel axis (`0.08s`, `Ease.OutQuad`)
     - `Cruise` (3–4 tiles): `(1.24, 0.78)` (`0.10s`, `Ease.OutQuad`)
     - `KineticRam` / `IsPhasing` (5+ tiles): `(1.38, 0.68)` (`0.10s`, `Ease.OutCubic`)
   - In `HandleMoveStarted(Vector2 dir)`: read `VelocityTier activeTier = movement != null && movement.CurrentTier != VelocityTier.None ? movement.CurrentTier : VelocityTier.Drift;` so `DeflectionWedge`/`VectorPad` (`OnRedirected -> OnMoveStarted`) and `StepPhaseLaunch` post-phase exits keep `Cruise`/`KineticRam` posture seamlessly instead of resetting to `0.12f` stretch.
   - In `HandleTierChanged(VelocityTier tier)`: when `tier >= VelocityTier.Drift && movement.IsMoving`, update `HoldDeform` to the new tier scale; when `tier == VelocityTier.None` while stopped, allow stop recovery to settle cleanly.
   - In `HandlePhasingChanged(bool phasing)`: if `phasing == true`, immediately trigger `HoldDeform` at `VelocityTier.KineticRam` scale along `movement.CurrentDirection` (fixing stationary `VectorLauncher` entries).
   - In `HandleMoveStopped()`: early-return `if (movement != null && movement.InputLocked) { ResetScale(); return; }` so `PlayerMovement.Freeze()` on death/exit does not fire an anchor squash.
   - Subscribe to `PlayerMovement.OnDirectionBufferCleared`: trigger a crisp `0.05s` orthogonal snap pulse `(0.92, 1.08)` (`Ease.OutQuad` -> settle into current flight posture) confirming cancelled buffered turns.

2. **Cosmetic PPU Standardization, `OnSkinApplied` Hook & Wall-Flush Anchoring**:
   - **Import Fix (`Player_Amber.aseprite.meta`, `Player_Cobalt.aseprite.meta`, `Player_Prism.aseprite.meta`)**: Change `spritePixelsToUnits` from `100` to `32` (matching `Player Icon.aseprite.meta` and the `32 PPU` project spec). At `32 PPU`, `24x24` becomes `0.75u x 0.75u` centered cleanly inside the `1.0u` cell on `Player/Sprite` (`localPosition = (0, -0.5, 0)`), eliminating miniature `0.24u` scaling.
   - **Event Hook (`Assets/Scripts/Feedback/PlayerCosmeticApplier.cs`)**: Add `public event Action OnSkinApplied;` invoked at the end of `RefreshEquippedSkin()`. Subscribe `PlayerSquashStretch` to `cosmeticApplier.OnSkinApplied` to update `ResetScale()` and reapply phase alpha when skins change.
   - **Bounds + Cell-Face Wall Contact (`PlayerSquashStretch.cs`)**:
     - During flight deformations (`pressIntoWall == false`), anchor at live `Bounds sb = spriteRenderer.sprite.bounds;` center so any sprite pivot scales around its visual midpoint:  
       `offset.x = (1f - targetScale.x) * sb.center.x;`  
       `offset.y = (1f - targetScale.y) * sb.center.y;`
     - During wall slams (`pressIntoWall == true`), compute the deformed sprite edge in Player root space (`baseLocalPosition + offset + Vector3.Scale(targetScale, impactDir > 0 ? sb.max : sb.min)`) and press it flush to the wall boundary (`±movement.CellSize * 0.5f = ±0.50f`, clamped to `maxWallPressShift = 0.28f` so even `0.75u` `24x24` cosmetic skins kiss the wall contact plane cleanly without floating gaps or overlapping the wall tile).
     - Optional smoothness upgrade: evaluate `Rigidbody2D.m_Interpolate = 1` (`Interpolate`) on `Player` in `GameScene.unity` so `30 u/s` motion stays silky at 120Hz+ without altering `FixedUpdate` steps.

3. **Two-Stage Phase Alpha & Unlit URP 2D Kinetic Shader**:
   - **Phase 1–2 Color-Safe Fallback**: In `PlayerSquashStretch`, never overwrite `spriteRenderer.color` RGB with a hardcoded `phaseSpriteTint` or stale `baseSpriteColor` (which breaks `PlayerCosmeticApplier`'s `targetRenderer.color = skin.PlayerTint`). Instead, mutate **alpha only**: `Color c = spriteRenderer.color; c.a = isPhasing ? phaseAlpha : 1f; spriteRenderer.color = c;`. Re-apply `phaseAlpha` inside `HandleSkinApplied` if `isPhasing == true`.
   - **Phase 3 Shader (`Assets/Shaders/GravitaPlayerURP2D.shader` + `Assets/Materials/Player_Kinetic.mat`)**:
     - Author an Unlit URP 2D Sprite shader matching `Player/Sprite`'s existing `Sprite-Unlit-Default` (`Tags { "RenderPipeline"="UniversalPipeline" "Queue"="Transparent" "RenderType"="Transparent" "CanUseSpriteAtlas"="True" }` with `Pass { Tags { "LightMode"="Universal2D" } }`).
     - Multiply `SAMPLE_TEXTURE2D(_MainTex, sampler_MainTex, uv) * input.color` so `PlayerCosmeticApplier.targetRenderer.color = skin.PlayerTint` (`a = 1f` permanently in Phase 3) tints identically to `Sprite-Unlit-Default`.
     - Expose per-instance `MaterialPropertyBlock` uniforms driven by `PlayerSquashStretch`:
       - `_FlashColor` / `_FlashAmount`: spikes `1.0 -> 0.0` over `0.06s` (unscaled) on `Cruise`/`KineticRam` wall slam and `CrackedBlock` shatter.
       - `_PhaseAlpha`: lerps to `0.45f` while `movement.IsPhasing` (keeping `SpriteRenderer.color.a == 1f`).
       - `_KineticRimColor` / `_KineticRimStrength`: samples 4-neighbor texture alpha offsets (`_MainTex_TexelSize.xy`) to add crisp pixel-edge rim glow (`0.00` Idle -> `0.20` Drift -> `0.45` Cruise -> `0.85` KineticRam).

### Pillar 2: Pause-Safe Hit-Stop, Unscaled Impact VFX, Analog Camera Zoom & Single-Owner Shake

**Why It Makes Gravita Addictive**: When a `KineticRam` dash slams a wall or shatters a `CrackedBlock`, time must snap frozen while the camera kicks, sparks explode through the freeze frame, and the lens punches inward—turning every hard stop into physical impact.

#### Exact Specs & C# Integration
1. **Unscaled Cinemachine Sync & Pause-Safe Hit-Stop (`PlayerImpactFeedback.cs`, `CinemachineCameraShake2D.cs`, `UIManager.cs`)**:
   - **Scene (`GameScene.unity`)**: Set `CinemachineBrain.IgnoreTimeScale = true`.
   - **Impulse Clock (`CinemachineCameraShake2D.Awake()`)**: Set `CinemachineImpulseManager.Instance.IgnoreTimeScale = true` once on initialization.
   - **Pause Guard (`Assets/Scripts/UI/Core/UIManager.cs`)**: Expose `public bool IsTimeFrozenByUI` returning `true` whenever any active entry in `popupStack` has `GetFreezeTime(type) == true`.
   - **Hit-Stop Routine (`PlayerImpactFeedback.cs`)**:
     - Guard re-entry with `bool hitStopActive` and capture `preHitStopTimeScale = Time.timeScale;` **only** when `!hitStopActive` (skip if `Time.timeScale < 0.5f` or `UIManager.Instance != null && UIManager.Instance.IsTimeFrozenByUI`).
     - Temporarily capture `savedUpdateMethod = cinemachineBrain.UpdateMethod;` and set `cinemachineBrain.UpdateMethod = CinemachineBrain.UpdateMethods.LateUpdate;` so camera updates never stall under `SmartUpdate` `Fixed` clock while `Time.timeScale == 0f`.
     - Track `abortedByPause` via `UIManager.Instance.OnPopupStackChanged`: if a freeze-time popup (`Pause`, `Result`, etc.) opens during the `WaitForSecondsRealtime(duration)` window, mark `abortedByPause = true` and break.
     - Inside `finally` (and `OnDisable()` if `hitStopActive`), **always** restore `cinemachineBrain.UpdateMethod = savedUpdateMethod;`, and restore `Time.timeScale = preHitStopTimeScale;` **only** if `!abortedByPause && (UIManager.Instance == null || !UIManager.Instance.IsTimeFrozenByUI)`.

2. **Unscaled Impact Particle Prefabs (`PooledParticlePlayer.cs`, `ReturnToPool.cs`, Particle Prefabs)**:
   - Set `ParticleSystem.main.useUnscaledTime = true` (`useUnscaledTime: 1`) on `WallImpactBurst.prefab`, `WallImpactBreak.prefab`, and `Particle Star.prefab` (plus child emitters).
   - Update `PooledParticlePlayer.cs` (`MonitorParticlesRoutine`): add `[SerializeField] private bool useUnscaledTime;` (auto-enabled if any child `ParticleSystem.main.useUnscaledTime` is true) and step `elapsed += useUnscaledTime ? Time.unscaledDeltaTime : Time.deltaTime;`.
   - Update `ReturnToPool.cs`: add `[SerializeField] private bool useUnscaledTime;` and yield `WaitForSecondsRealtime(delay)` when enabled.
   - *(Note: `PlayerLineTrail.LateUpdate()` intentionally retains `Time.deltaTime` so the speed ribbon holds frozen onto the player during Hit-Stop frames and retracts smoothly when time resumes.)*

3. **Single-Owner Shake Routing & Non-Wall Stop Separation**:
   - **Prefab Corrections**: Zero `defaultCameraShake = 0f` on `VectorPad.prefab` (currently `0.04`) and `VectorLauncher.prefab` (currently `0.1`); `Wedge.prefab` is already `0`. Set `Brake.prefab` `defaultCameraShake = 0.025f` (currently `0`) and keep `GravityBlock.prefab` at `0.04f` so `InteractionEffectPlayer` owns floor-anchor micro-shakes cleanly.
   - **`PlayerImpactFeedback.cs` Filtering**:
     - `HandleStopped()`: early-return `if (movement == null || movement.InputLocked || !movement.WasLastStopWallImpact) return;` — `Brake`, `GravityBlock`, post-phase stops, and `Freeze()` never fire wall Hit-Stop or wall shake.
     - `HandleRedirected(Vector2 _)`: early-return `if (movement != null && movement.IsPhasing) return;` so `StartPhaseLaunch` (which emits `OnRedirected`) does not fire `redirectShake` (`0.04f`); instead subscribe to `OnPhasingChanged(bool phasing)` and fire `phaseLaunchShake = 0.10f` when `phasing == true` (covering all `VectorLauncher` launches cleanly from one owner).
     - `HandleWallImpact(Vector2 normal)`: keep wall spark/dust spawning driven by `movement.CurrentTier` (before `StopMoving` clears it), while `HandleStopped` reads finalized `movement.LastImpactTier` for Hit-Stop and shake.

4. **Analog Dynamic Camera Zoom & Velocity Look-Ahead (`GameScene.unity`, `CinemachineCameraZoom2D.cs`)**:
   - In `Assets/Scenes/GameScene.unity`: disable **both** `CinemachinePixelPerfect` (`m_Enabled: 0` on `CinemachineCamera`) and `PixelPerfectCamera` (`m_Enabled: 0` on `Main Camera`), keep `normalOrthographicSize = 5.5f`, tighten `CinemachinePositionComposer.Damping` from `{1, 1, 1}` to `{0.45, 0.45, 1}`, and enable `Lookahead` (`Enabled: 1`, `Time: 0.12`, `Smoothing: 6`).
   - Refactor `CinemachineCameraZoom2D.cs` to separate `tierZoomOffset` (`None/Drift: +0.00u`, `Cruise: +0.18u`, `KineticRam: +0.35u`, smoothed in `Update()` using `Time.unscaledDeltaTime * zoomSpeed`) from `punchOffset` (tweened via `DOTween.To` with `.DOKill()`, `.SetUpdate(true)`, `.SetLink(gameObject)`: `-0.10u` on `Cruise` wall stop over `0.03s` in / `0.10s` `Ease.OutQuad` back; `-0.22u` on `KineticRam` wall stop or `CrackedBlock` shatter over `0.04s` in / `0.12s` `Ease.OutQuad` back; `+0.12u` brief breath on `CloseCall` graze? No—keep `CloseCall` punch `-0.08u` sharp micro-flinch over `0.02s` / `0.10s`), setting `cinemachineCamera.Lens.OrthographicSize = smoothedBaseSize + punchOffset;`.

### Pillar 3: Polyphonic Audio Pool, Populated Sound Catalog & Pitch-Escalation Envelope

**Why It Makes Gravita Addictive**: At `30 u/s`, Subject G-0 crosses a tile every `33.3ms`. Rising rhythmic ticks, harmonic wedge chains, and three distinct impact weight classes turn blind maze slides into readable musical phrases.

#### Exact Specs & Prefab/Script Wiring
1. **Polyphonic Pool & Loop Channel (`AudioSystems.prefab`, `GameScene.unity`, `SoundManager.cs`)**:
   - Author `Assets/Prefabs/Audio/SfxVoice.prefab`: single 2D `AudioSource` (`playOnAwake = false`, `loop = false`, `spatialBlend = 0f`).
