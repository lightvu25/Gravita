ATTACK 7 [MINOR] Raising `Bloom.clamp` (`10 -> 14`) misunderstands URP Bloom clamping and has no effect on `intensity = 13.5` bursts
Where: `r2-m02.a016.solution.md` lines 293 (`"raise clamp: 10.0 -> 14.0 | 13.5"`), 299 (`"bump Bloom.clamp in Global Volume Profile.asset from 10 to 14 so peak bursts aren't clipped."`), 339, and 440.
Problem: In URP (`Bloom.shader:105-106`, `Bloom.cs:191-195`), `Bloom.clamp` (`ClampMax`) runs in the Prefilter pass **before** thresholding (`threshold = 0.9`) and blur (`color = min(ClampMax, color);`), clamping input HDR source pixel radiance (`"If pixels in your Scene are more intense than this, URP renders them at their current intensity, but uses this intensity value for the purposes of Bloom calculations"`). Conversely, `Bloom.intensity` (`13.5`) is applied **unclamped** during final composition in `UberPost.shader:205-206` (`bloom *= BloomIntensity; color += bloom * BloomTint;`). Raising `clamp` from `10` to `14` misconstrues URP's input-radiance cap as an output `intensity` ceiling and does nothing to stop `intensity = 13.5` bursts from clipping or overexposing.
