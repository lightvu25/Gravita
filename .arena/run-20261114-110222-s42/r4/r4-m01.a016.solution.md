# Gravita — Kinetic Lab Game Feel Upgrade Blueprint

> **Codebase Calibration Note (Current Truth vs. Design Targets)**
> 1. **Speed & Velocity Tiers**: In `Assets/Scenes/GameScene.unity`, `PlayerMovement.moveSpeed` is `30` units/sec (`cellSize = 1`), meaning Subject G-0 traverses **1 tile every `33.3 ms`** (and **`48 u/s`** during `StepPhaseLaunch` at `1.6x`). In `VelocityTier.cs` (and `docs/gdd.md`), `VelocityTierUtil.FromTiles(int tilesTraveled)` maps `0` -> `None`, `1–2` -> `Drift`, `3–4` -> `Cruise`, and `5+` -> `KineticRam` (reaching max tier in **`167 ms`**), whereas the high-level spec describes `1–3 / 4–7 / 8+`. Every timing below is calibrated to the live `33.3 ms/tile` (`30 u/s`) loop and scales automatically if `FromTiles` is tuned later.
> 2. **Economy & Revive Flags**: `Assets/Resources/EconomyBalanceConfig.asset` sets `enableLegacyExtraRewardSystems: 0` (suppressing `CloseCallDetector`'s `bonusCoins: 5`), `enableMidGameplayRevive: 0` (`LevelFlowController.CanRevive` is always `false` and `OnPlayerRevived` never fires), and `grantWalletCoinsFromInLevelPickups: 0` (`EconomyManager.OnCoinsChanged` does not increment per in-level coin pickup). Near-misses must therefore pay off through immediate audiovisual and post-FX surge, coin streak HUD pulses must hook `Collectible.OnAnyCoinCollected`, and respawn polish must hook active restart/respawn paths rather than revive.
> 3. **Camera Pixel-Perfect Lock**: `Assets/Scenes/GameScene.unity` currently enables both `CinemachinePixelPerfect` (`m_Enabled: 1`) on `CinemachineCamera` and URP `PixelPerfectCamera` (`m_Enabled: 1`, `m_AssetsPPU: 32`, `320x180`, `CropFrame: 0`) on `Main Camera`. Every dynamic tier zoom and lens pulse requires disabling **both** overrides.

## Target End-State Experience

When a player clears a chamber after these upgrades:
1. Every `33.3 ms` tile crosses on an ascending harmonic synth tick (`minInterval = 0.01f` so zero ticks drop), while Magnet coin sweeps cascade into an ascending semitone arpeggio without dropping same-frame pickups; crossing 5 tiles kicks the camera out smoothly (`5.50 -> 5.72 -> 6.02`) and trails crisp afterimages while `PulseZoom`, directional impulses, ribbons, particles, and 2D impact anims all keep animating smoothly through `Time.timeScale = 0` hit-stop (and yield safely if Pause/Result/Revive opens mid-impact).
2. Swiping a perpendicular corner turn while still sliding lights a subtle directional "Input Coil" chevron on Subject G-0 (never falsely triggering on idle starts, blocked taps, or instant reversals) and whips cleanly through chained `DeflectionWedge` / `VectorPad` bounces with bisector sparks and ascending pitch—without inflating move counts, firing wedge audio/shake on `VectorLauncher` phase launches, or re-firing move-start sounds on redirects or phase arrivals.
3. Crashing into a wall punches the camera *along the slide vector* (coalesced per frame so multi-block `Breaker` sweeps never double-stack), snaps Subject G-0 into `30x14 px` compression within `25 ms` flush against the wall face during unscaled hit-stop, plays the exact impact tier (never stale `LastImpactTier`), and fires unmistakable `MediumImpact` / `RigidImpact` haptics—while `BrakeTile` and `GravityBlock` give a soft magnetic settle (and deaths/exits/teleports/phase-stops stay cleanly quiet).
4. Threading past an arming `PulseLaser` or `SentinelDronePatrol` flashes an unscaled URP `Overclock` burst (`Bloom.intensity`, `ChromaticAberration`, `Vignette`, `-0.28` lens snap, world-space 3D `TextMeshPro` toast, directional shake), lab hazards/switches run silently on spawn/reset while telegraphing live ticks cleanly without double-triggering `FailingTile`, and hazard deaths snap to the Result modal in `~0.60 s`.

## 1. Core Game Feel Diagnosis: Why Gravita Currently Feels "Flat"

Gravita's deterministic stop-to-stop physics, velocity tiers, squash/stretch, trails, shake, and hit-stop are cleanly separated—but six concrete implementation leaks cancel out speed and impact weight in live play:

1. **Pixel-Perfect Camera Lock, Cinemachine Update Freezing & Hit-Stop Ownership Conflicts**
   - `CinemachineCameraZoom2D` currently has **zero callers** anywhere in code and uses scaled `Time.deltaTime`. Even when called, `CinemachinePixelPerfect.PostPipelineStageCallback` overwrites `state.Lens.OrthographicSize` to integer `PPU` steps (locking `1920x1080` and `1280x720` to `5.625` across `4.82–6.75` and `4.50–7.50`), and disabling *only* `CinemachinePixelPerfect` leaves `PixelPerfectCamera.m_CinemachineCompatibilityMode == false` so `PixelPerfectCamera.OnBeginCameraRendering` forces `m_Camera.orthographicSize` down to `2.8125` at `1080p`.
   - `Main Camera`'s `CinemachineBrain` serializes `UpdateMethod: 2` (`SmartUpdate`) and `IgnoreTimeScale: 0` while `CinemachineCamera` targets `Player` (moving in `FixedUpdate()`), locking `UpdateTracker` to `UpdateClock.Fixed`. During `Time.timeScale = 0f` hit-stop, Unity skips `FixedUpdate`, so `CinemachineCamera.State` and `CinemachineImpulseListener` freeze for the entire hit-stop window. Conversely, `PlayerImpactFeedback.PulseHitStop` (`line 147`) rejects calls whenever `Time.timeScale < 0.5f` (blocking chained hit-stops), while `HitStopRoutine` blindly restores `Time.timeScale = preHitStopTimeScale` instead of respecting `UIManager`'s `Pause`/`Result`/`Revive` modal `freezeTime = 1` ownership.
   - Simultaneously, `PlayerLineTrail.LateUpdate()` exits on `Time.deltaTime <= 0f`, `ReturnToPool` uses scaled `WaitForSeconds`, `PooledParticlePlayer` and `PooledAnimationPlayer` (`line 292`) track scaled `Time.deltaTime`, existing particle prefabs (`WallImpactBurst.prefab`, `WallImpactBreak.prefab`, `Particle Star.prefab`) serialize `useUnscaledTime: 0`, and `PoppingVFX.prefab` runs its `Animator` in `Normal` (`0`) update mode.
2. **Pivot Offset Mismatch, Clipped/Slow Squash, Misclassified Non-Wall Stops & Stale/Sub-Tile Impact Reads**
   - `Player/Sprite` in `GameScene.unity` sits at `localPosition (0, -0.5, 0)` with bottom-center `(0.5, 0)` pivot (`Player Icon.aseprite.meta:132`). `PlayerSquashStretch.Play()` applies only raw `pressDirection * (wallPressPx / 32f)` without bounds/pivot compensation (so upward hits compress *away* from the top wall and `pressDirection = Vector2.zero` shifts the center downward around the bottom edge), `GameScene.unity` clips deformation at `maxDeformation = 0.3` (`26x19.6 px`), and the `0.4 * duration` (`0.072 s`–`0.088 s`) squash-in ramp is slower than `0.033 s` (`Cruise`) and `0.040 s` (`CrackedBlock`) hit-stops.
   - `PlayerImpactFeedback.HandleStopped()` listens to `OnMoveStopped` **without checking `movement.WasLastStopWallImpact`**, triggering wall shake/hit-stop on `BrakeTile`/`GravityBlock`. Yet `!WasLastStopWallImpact` also fires on `Freeze()` (death/exit), `TeleportTo()`, and zero-direction `StepPhaseLaunch` stops—so brake feedback must filter strictly on `BrakeTile`/`GravityBlock` interactions rather than `!WasLastStopWallImpact`.
   - `PlayerMovement` invokes `OnWallImpact` *before* `StopMoving(wallImpact: true)` writes `LastImpactTier`, so reading `LastImpactTier` inside `OnWallImpact` plays the **previous** dash's tier. Conversely, `< 1.0 u` stops (e.g., adjacent blunt `DeflectionWedge` stops at `0.049 u`) enter `OnWallImpact` with `CurrentTier == VelocityTier.None`—falling back to `LastImpactTier` would replay a stale `KineticRam` slam on a sub-tile tap.
3. **Omnidirectional/Un-Coalesced Shake, Particle Axis Mismatch & Prefab Config Leaks**
   - `CinemachineCameraShake2D.ShakeCamera(float force)` calls `impulseSource.GenerateImpulse(force)` against a fixed `DefaultVelocity = (0, -1, 0)` with zero same-frame coalescing—and `InteractionEffectPlayer`, `CloseCallDetector`, `StarCollectFeedback`, and `BreachSequencer` all invoke that downward path.
   - `WallImpactBurst.prefab`, `WallImpactBreak.prefab`, and `Particle Star.prefab` use a 3D Cone `ShapeModule` emitting along local **`+Z`**, whereas `InteractionEffectPlayer.ResolveRotation` applies 2D Z-axis `Atan2` intended for `+X` 2D Animator sprites (`PoppingVFX.prefab`, `impactAnimationPrefab`). Meanwhile `tier1Shake`, `redirectShake`, `impactAnimationPrefab`, and `blockBreakAnimationPrefab` are zero/null in `GameScene.unity`; `CrackedBlock.Break()` takes 0 arguments (losing `Breaker` direction) and fires twice on 2-block `Breaker` sweeps (`0.22 + 0.22`); `Cracked.prefab` sets `alignToInteractionDirection: 0`; `VectorPad.prefab` sets `defaultCameraShake: 0.04`; and `Wedge.prefab`, `Brake.prefab`, `Exit.prefab`, and `Hazard.prefab` have empty `InteractionEffectPlayer`s.
4. **167ms "Runway Blindness", Phase Clipping, 3-Site `OnMoveStarted` / `StartPhaseLaunch` `OnRedirected` Leaks & Unfiltered Input**
   - At `30 u/s`, Subject G-0 reaches `KineticRam` in `0.167 s` without per-tile audio escalation or afterimages, while `PlayerLineTrail` clamps `instSpeed` to `[4f, 28f]`—clipping both `30 u/s` runs and `48 u/s` `StepPhaseLaunch` phasing.
   - `PlayerMovement` fires redundant `OnMoveStarted` at three non-idle sites (`StepPhaseLaunch` arrival `line 463`, `StartPhaseLaunch` zero-distance `line 516`, and `Redirect()` `line 998`), inflating `movesUsedThisRun`, double-playing `moveStart`, and wiping `PlayerSquashStretch` elongation mid-dash—while `StartPhaseLaunch` (`lines 515, 523`) also fires `OnRedirected`, which would misroute `VectorLauncher` launches into wedge SFX, `redirectShake`, and Corner-Whip.
   - `PlayerMovement.QueueDirection` writes raw `pendingDirection` on idle launches, blocked adjacent-wall swipes, and reversals, so visual turn indicators cannot read raw `pendingDirection` safely.
5. **Unwired `Bootstrap` Audio, Pool Hijack Risk, Same-Frame Magnet Coin Drop & World Broadcast Leaks**
   - `SoundConfig.asset` has `clips: []` on all `23` entries, and while `AudioSystems.prefab` wires `soundConfig`, the startup `AudioSystems` in `Assets/Scenes/Bootstrap.unity:1366-1426` (`DontDestroyOnLoad`!) has `soundConfig: null`, `poolContainer: null`, `pooledAudioSourcePrefab: null`, and `fallbackAudioSource: null` (with **no** `PooledAudioSource.prefab` in `Assets/`). Worse, `SoundManager.InitializePool()` sweeps all child `AudioSource`s when `poolContainer == null` and overwrites `outputAudioMixerGroup` to `sfxMixerGroup`.
   - `SoundManager.PlaySFX` throttles via `Mathf.Max(0.01f, entry.minInterval)` against `Time.unscaledTime`—so `PowerUpController.UpdateMagnet`'s same-frame coin loop (`dt = 0.000 s`) drops every coin after the first. Meanwhile `FailingTile` subclasses `MazeTile` and emits *both* `MazeTile.OnAnyPlayerInteracted` and `FailingTile.OnStateChanged`, `CloseCallDetector.OnCloseCallTriggered` has 0 subscribers, and adding unguarded static broadcasts on `HazardActivation`/`FailingTile` would burst-fire on spawn and reset.
6. **Flat Haptic Priority Blocking & `1.60 s` Failure Loop**
   - `GameHaptics.EventCount = 7` emits identical `LightImpact` across all wall speeds, while `GlobalMinGapSeconds = 0.03f` silently suppresses higher-priority `KineticRamImpact` when `TierUp` or `BlockSmash` fires on the same tick (`0.000 s`) or one `FixedUpdate` tick prior (`0.020 s` at `30 u/s`).
   - `CutsceneManager.DeathRoutine` uses `deathFallbackDuration = 0.8f` + `endFadeDuration = 0.4f` (~`1.60 s` total delay before showing the Result panel).

### Pillar 1: Kinetic Runway Engine ("33ms-per-Tile Escalation & Hit-Stop-Safe Unscaled Lens")

#### Why It Makes Gravita Uniquely Addictive
Because slides finish in fractions of a second (`167 ms` to reach `KineticRam`), static trail color swaps aren't enough for the player's nervous system to *feel* acceleration. Turning every crossed tile into an ascending harmonic synth tick paired with unscaled additive camera zoom-out, crisp `KineticRam` afterimages, and trail/particle/2D-anim/camera playback that stays 100% live through chained `Time.timeScale = 0` hit-stops—while cleanly yielding `Time.timeScale` back to `UIManager` if a modal opens—turns long corridors into an accelerating slingshot.

    ```csharp
    private bool hasActiveHitStop;
    private float hitStopEndRealtime;
    private float preHitStopTimeScale = 1f;
    private CinemachineBrain.UpdateMethods preHitStopBrainUpdateMethod;
    private bool preHitStopBrainIgnoreTimeScale;

    private void PulseHitStop(float duration)
    {
        if (!hitStopEnabled || duration <= 0f) return;
        if (UIManager.Instance != null && UIManager.Instance.HasTimeFreezingPopupOpen) return;
        if (!hasActiveHitStop && Time.timeScale < 0.5f) return;

        float candidateEnd = Time.realtimeSinceStartup + duration;
        hitStopEndRealtime = hasActiveHitStop ? Mathf.Max(hitStopEndRealtime, candidateEnd) : candidateEnd;

        if (!hasActiveHitStop)
        {
            hitStopRoutine = StartCoroutine(HitStopRoutine());
        }
    }

    private IEnumerator HitStopRoutine()
    {
        hasActiveHitStop = true;
        IsHitStopActive = true;
        preHitStopTimeScale = Time.timeScale;
        if (cinemachineBrain != null)
        {
            preHitStopBrainUpdateMethod = cinemachineBrain.UpdateMethod;
            preHitStopBrainIgnoreTimeScale = cinemachineBrain.IgnoreTimeScale;
            cinemachineBrain.UpdateMethod = CinemachineBrain.UpdateMethods.LateUpdate;
            cinemachineBrain.IgnoreTimeScale = true;
        }
        Time.timeScale = 0f;

        while (Time.realtimeSinceStartup < hitStopEndRealtime)
        {
            if (UIManager.Instance != null && UIManager.Instance.HasTimeFreezingPopupOpen)
                break;
            yield return null;
        }

        RestoreHitStopState();
    }

    private void RestoreHitStopState()
    {
        if (!hasActiveHitStop) return;
        IsHitStopActive = false;
        hasActiveHitStop = false;
        hitStopRoutine = null;

        if (cinemachineBrain != null)
        {
            cinemachineBrain.UpdateMethod = preHitStopBrainUpdateMethod;
            cinemachineBrain.IgnoreTimeScale = preHitStopBrainIgnoreTimeScale;
        }

        if (UIManager.Instance != null)
            UIManager.Instance.RefreshTimeScale();
        else
            Time.timeScale = preHitStopTimeScale;
    }
    ```

  ```csharp
  [DefaultExecutionOrder(-10)]
  public class CinemachineCameraZoom2D : MonoBehaviour
  {
      private float currentBaseSize;
      private float pulseOffset;
      private Sequence pulseSequence;

      private void Awake()
      {
          // ... existing singleton setup ...
          if (disablePixelPerfectOverrides)
          {
              if (cinemachinePixelPerfect != null) cinemachinePixelPerfect.enabled = false;
              if (urpPixelPerfectCamera != null) urpPixelPerfectCamera.enabled = false;
          }
          currentBaseSize = normalOrthographicSize > 0f ? normalOrthographicSize : 5.5f;
          if (targetOrthographicSize <= 0f) targetOrthographicSize = currentBaseSize;
      }

      private void LateUpdate()
      {
          if (cinemachineCamera == null) return;
          float dt = PlayerImpactFeedback.IsHitStopActive ? Time.unscaledDeltaTime : Time.deltaTime;
          if (dt <= 0f) return;
          float alpha = 1f - Mathf.Exp(-zoomSpeed * dt);
          currentBaseSize = Mathf.Lerp(currentBaseSize, targetOrthographicSize, alpha);
          cinemachineCamera.Lens.OrthographicSize = Mathf.Max(1f, currentBaseSize + pulseOffset);
      }

      public void PulseZoom(float orthoDelta, float inDuration, float outDuration)
      {
          pulseSequence?.Kill();
          pulseSequence = DOTween.Sequence()
              .Append(DOTween.To(() => pulseOffset, x => pulseOffset = x, orthoDelta, inDuration).SetEase(Ease.OutQuad))
              .Append(DOTween.To(() => pulseOffset, x => pulseOffset = x, 0f, outDuration).SetEase(Ease.OutSine))
              .SetUpdate(true)
              .SetLink(gameObject)
              .OnKill(() => pulseOffset = 0f);
      }
  }
  ```

### Pillar 2: Directional Impact Physics, Wall-Face Compression & Break/Brake Fidelity

#### Why It Makes Gravita Uniquely Addictive
In a stop-to-stop maze, impact *is* the punctuation mark of every swipe. Fixing directional camera kick with per-frame coalescing, snapping Subject G-0 into `30x14 px` wall-face compression within `25 ms` (peaking *inside* `33 ms`–`70 ms` hit-stops) with exact bottom-center `(0.5, 0)` pivot compensation, isolating `BrakeTile`/`GravityBlock` settles from `Freeze()`/`TeleportTo()`/phase-stops, routing 3D `+Z` particles vs. 2D `+X` anims onto their real emission axes, and ensuring sub-tile stops never replay stale `KineticRam` sounds makes every collision feel physically honest.

#### Exact Numbers, Curves & Inspector Parameters
| Parameter | Current (`GameScene.unity`) | Recommended Target | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `PlayerImpactFeedback.tier1Shake` | `0` | `0.045` | Drift taps feel crisp instead of silent |
| `PlayerImpactFeedback.tier2Shake` / `tier3Shake` | `0.1` / `0.2` | `0.13` / `0.28` | Clear contrast between Cruise and KineticRam |
| `PlayerImpactFeedback.redirectShake` | `0` | `0.08` | Gives wedge/pad deflects a directional punch |
| `PlayerImpactFeedback.tier1HitStop` / `tier2` / `tier3` | `0` / `0.033` / `0.083` | `0` / `0.033` / `0.070` | Preserves instant short taps while keeping `KineticRam` snappy |

### Pillar 6: Priority-Aware Tiered Haptics & Snappy Failure-to-Retry Loop

#### Why It Makes Gravita Uniquely Addictive
In precision kinetic puzzlers, players embrace high difficulty when every collision carries distinct tactile weight in the hands and restarting after failure returns them to motion immediately. Right now `GameplayHapticsFeedback` emits identical `LightImpact` for every wall speed, `GameHaptics.EventCount = 7` caps the service, `GlobalMinGapSeconds = 0.03f` can starve `KineticRamImpact` behind subtle pre-events, and `CutsceneManager` adds `~1.60 s` of delay before opening the Result panel.
