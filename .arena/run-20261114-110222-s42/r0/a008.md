# Gravita (Puzzle 2D) — Kinetic Game Feel Upgrade Blueprint

## Assumptions & Codebase Baseline
- **Velocity Tier Thresholds**: `Assets/Scripts/Gameplay/VelocityTier.cs` (`VelocityTierUtil.FromTiles`) currently maps `1–2` tiles to `Drift`, `3–4` to `Cruise`, and `5+` to `KineticRam` (whereas GDD/design notes also reference `1–3 / 4–7 / 8+`). Every feedback hook below subscribes to `PlayerMovement.CurrentTier`, `PlayerMovement.OnVelocityTierChanged`, `PlayerMovement.LastImpactTier`, and `PlayerMovement.OnTileCrossed` so threshold re-balancing requires zero changes to feedback scripts.
- **Physics & Camera Baseline (`Assets/Scenes/GameScene.unity`)**: `PlayerMovement` runs at `moveSpeed = 30` units/sec (`33.33 ms/tile`), `Phase Launch` (`1.6x`) runs at `48` units/sec (`20.83 ms/tile`), `bufferTurnsUntilStop = 1`, `32 PPU` pixel art (`1 px = 0.03125` world units), and `CinemachineCamera.Lens.OrthographicSize = 5.5` with `CinemachinePositionComposer` `Damping = (1, 1, 1)` and `Lookahead.Enabled = 0`.
- **Architecture & Scene Rules**: All additions obey SRP Layer-4 event observation (`Assets/Scripts/Feedback/` & `Assets/Scripts/Audio/`), never alter `PlayerMovement.FixedUpdate` / `SimulateStep` cell physics, never use procedural `new GameObject()` or runtime `AddComponent<T>()` creation, use pooled prefabs (`VfxSpawner.Spawn` / `ObjectPoolManager.SpawnObject`) + `[SerializeField]` Inspector wiring, and enforce `.DOKill()`, `.SetUpdate(true)`, `.SetLink(gameObject)` on every DOTween animation.

## 1. Core Game Feel Diagnosis: Why Gravita Currently Feels Flat

Despite having basic camera shake, squash/stretch, and a trail renderer, Gravita loses its kinetic punch across five concrete, codebase-level bottlenecks:

1. **Hit-Stop Freezes the Visual Impact It Is Supposed to Accentuate (`33–83 ms` Deadzone)**
   At `moveSpeed = 30 u/s`, a `1–5` tile dash finishes in `33–167 ms`. `PlayerImpactFeedback.PulseHitStop` sets `Time.timeScale = 0f` for `tier2HitStop = 0.033s` and `tier3HitStop = 0.083s`, which inadvertently freezes:
   - `Assets/Prefabs/Particles/WallImpactBurst.prefab` and `WallImpactBreak.prefab` — both have `useUnscaledTime: 0` on their root `ParticleSystem`, pausing impact sparks motionless at frame 1 until Hit-Stop ends.
   - `Assets/Scripts/Feedback/PlayerLineTrail.cs` — `LateUpdate()` returns early on `float dt = Time.deltaTime; if (dt <= 0f) return;` (line 253), freezing the trail ribbon behind the impacted sprite, while line 292 clamps `smoothedMoveSpeed` to `[4f, 28f]` below `moveSpeed = 30` (`48` during Phase).
   - `Assets/Scripts/Feedback/CinemachineCameraZoom2D.cs` — uses `Time.deltaTime` instead of `Time.unscaledDeltaTime`.

2. **Flat Low-End Impact & Bottom-Center Pivot / Mid-Flight Reset Bugs in `PlayerSquashStretch`**
   - In `GameScene.unity`, `PlayerImpactFeedback` has `tier1Shake = 0` and `redirectShake = 0`, making `Drift` steps and `DeflectionWedge` rebounds completely weightless.
   - `PlayerSquashStretch` already calls `.SetUpdate(true)` and `.SetLink(gameObject)`, **but**:
     - `GameScene.unity` overrides `maxDeformation = 0.3`, clipping the authored `impactHeightPx = 16 / movingHeightPx = 28` pancake (`-42.9%`) down to `-30%`.
     - The `Sprite` child sits at `localPosition = (0, -0.5, 0)` (bottom-center `(0.5, 0)` pivot). On `Vector2.up` ceiling impacts, shrinking `localScale.y` pulls the top edge down toward the bottom of the tile (`-0.30` units) while `wallPressPx = 4` (`+0.125` units) shifts upward—leaving a visible `~5–6 px` air gap between Subject G-0 and the ceiling wall.
     - `HandleMoveStarted` and `HandleTierChanged` fire transient `Play(...)` pulses (`stretchDuration = 0.18s`) that return to idle `baseScale` mid-slide instead of sustaining an aerodynamic velocity posture until `OnMoveStopped`.

3. **Dead Corners & Stacked Launch Audio (`Redirect` vs. `OnMoveStarted`)**
   - `PlayerMovement.Redirect()` (`Assets/Scripts/Gameplay/PlayerMovement.cs:997-998`) invokes **both** `OnRedirected` and `OnMoveStarted`, causing `EntityAudio` to layer `moveStart` on top of `wedgeRedirect` instead of letting chained wedges escalate cleanly.
   - `InteractionEffectPlayer` arrays on `Wedge.prefab`, `Brake.prefab`, `Exit.prefab`, and `Hazard.prefab` are completely empty (`effectPrefabs: []`, `defaultCameraShake: 0`), leaving corner rebounds and anchor stops visually silent.

4. **Dormant Signature Systems (`CinemachineCameraZoom2D`, `CloseCallDetector`, Shaders, Global Volume)**
   - `CinemachineCameraZoom2D` is attached to `CinemachineCamera` (`OrthographicSize = 5.5`) and **never called anywhere in code**; `CinemachinePositionComposer` uses uniform `Damping = (1, 1, 1)` with `Lookahead.Enabled = 0`, trailing stiffly behind `30 u/s` bursts.
   - `CloseCallDetector.OnCloseCallTriggered` (`Action<Vector3>`) has **0 subscribers**, `closeCallVfxPrefab` reuses generic `Particle Star.prefab`, and `bonusCoins` never executes because `EconomyBalanceConfig.Active.EnableLegacyExtraRewardSystems` is `false`.
   - `Assets/Shaders/Hit/HitFlashSprite.shader` (`Hit Flash White.mat`) and `Assets/Volume/Global Volume Profile.asset` (`Bloom`, `ChromaticAberration`, `Vignette`) stay static regardless of kinetic energy.

5. **Silent Audio Catalog & Incomplete Haptic Coverage**
   - `Assets/Data/Sound/SoundConfig.asset` currently has **`clips: []` across all 19 slots**, even though ready-to-use sci-fi packs (`Assets/Audio/Sounds/kenney_sci-fi-sounds/`, `Assets/Audio/Sounds/sci-fi-sfx/`) already exist in the project.
   - `EntityAudio` does not listen to `OnTileCrossed`, `OnVelocityTierChanged`, `OnPhasingChanged`, `CloseCallDetector.OnCloseCallTriggered`, `MazeTile.OnAnyPlayerInteracted` (`Switch` / `Brake`), `HazardActivation.OnPhaseChanged`, or `FailingTile.OnStateChanged` (and `EnergyGate` lacks a static `OnAnyGateOpenStateChanged` broadcast like `CrackedBlock.OnAnyBroken`).
   - `GameplayHapticsFeedback` never triggers `HapticEvent.Death` (already mapped to `Failure` in `MostHapticsBackend.cs`), treats `Drift` and `KineticRam` wall slams identically (`LightImpact`), and ignores `CloseCall`.

## 2. Six High-Impact Game Feel Pillars for Gravita

### Pillar 1: Unscaled Kinetic Impact Rig, Pivot-Locked Squash & Two-Frame Breach Snap
* **Psychology & Rhythmic Feel**: Stop-to-stop inertia lives on crisp launch-slide-slam punctuation. Synchronizing sparks, sprite compression, directional recoil, and a localized material flash during the `33–83 ms` hit-stop turns walls from weightless blockers into solid lab bulkheads—making `KineticRam` impacts and `CrackedBlock` shatters feel explosively physical without delaying buffered swipes.
* **Exact Specs & Inspector Parameters**:
  * **Particle Unscaled Sync**: Set `useUnscaledTime = true` on `WallImpactBurst.prefab` and `WallImpactBreak.prefab` so impact bursts explode across frames `0–3` *during* the `Time.timeScale = 0` freeze.
  * **`PlayerImpactFeedback` Scene Tuning**:
    * `tier1Shake = 0.045f` (`Drift` tap, up from `0`), `tier2Shake = 0.12f` (`Cruise`), `tier3Shake = 0.24f` (`KineticRam`), `redirectShake = 0.035f` (up from `0`), `brakeAnchorShake = 0.025f` (when `!WasLastStopWallImpact`).
    * Directional camera impulse: replace omnidirectional shake with `Vector2 impulseDir = impactNormal` weighted by `(1f, 0.25f)` orthogonal jitter so hitting a ceiling wall punches the camera downward/rebound along physical momentum. Honor GDD §12.3 `traumaScale` multiplier (`0f..1f`).
  * **Sustained Flight Silhouette & Pivot Fix (`PlayerSquashStretch`)**:
    * Raise `maxDeformation` in `GameScene.unity` from `0.3` to `0.48` so `impactHeightPx = 16 / movingHeightPx = 28` (`-0.429`) executes fully.
    * **Pivot Compensation (`localPosition = (0, -0.5, 0)`)**: compute `spriteLocalHeight = spriteRenderer.sprite.bounds.size.y` and `pivotNormY = spriteRenderer.sprite.pivot.y / Mathf.Max(1f, spriteRenderer.sprite.rect.height)`. When squashing against `Vector2.up`, shift `pressed.y += (baseScale.y - deformed.y) * spriteLocalHeight * (1f - pivotNormY)` (and when `Vector2.down`, shift `pressed.y -= (baseScale.y - deformed.y) * spriteLocalHeight * pivotNormY`) so the deformed silhouette stays locked flush against the impacted wall instead of pulling away into mid-air.
    * **Hold Flight Form Until Stop**: `HandleMoveStarted` deforms into `movingWidthPx / movingHeightPx` over `0.055s` (`Ease.OutQuad`, unscaled) and **holds** that stretched needle posture (`KineticRam` scales stretch by `+0.08` along axis, `-0.05` across) until `OnMoveStopped` triggers the `28x16` pancake slam (`0.045s` compress + `0.15s` `Ease.OutBack` rebound to `baseScale`).
  * **Two-Frame Breach Flash & Ring (`HitFlashSprite.shader` + `KineticRamRing.prefab`)**:
    * On `Cruise` wall hit: flash `SpriteRenderer` with `Hit Flash White.mat` (`_FlashColor = #B8F3FFFF`) for `0.033s` unscaled.
    * On `KineticRam` wall hit or `CrackedBlock.OnAnyBroken`: flash `#FF70D4FF` (`0.030s`) -> `#FFFFFFFF` (`0.030s`) then restore base material (never clashing with `PlayerCosmeticApplier` tint). Spawn pooled `KineticRamRing.prefab` (`Glow VFX.mat`, `Scale 0.35 -> 2.6` over `0.18s` unscaled, alpha `0.9 -> 0`, color `#FF52C8`). *(A11y Photosensitivity Guard: localized sprite + expanding ring only—zero full-screen white strobe.)*
* **Concrete C# Integration Hooks**:
  * `CinemachineCameraShake2D`: add `public void ShakeDirectional(Vector2 direction, float force)` calling `impulseSource?.GenerateImpulse((Vector3)(direction.normalized * force))`.
  * `PlayerImpactFeedback`: split `HandleStopped()` on `movement.WasLastStopWallImpact`, pass `-movement.LastMoveDirection` to `ShakeDirectional`, trigger `Coroutine`/`DOTween` unscaled material flash via `[SerializeField] private Material hitFlashMaterial` + `[SerializeField] private SpriteRenderer playerSpriteRenderer`, and spawn `[SerializeField] private GameObject kineticRamRingPrefab` via `VfxSpawner.Spawn`.
  * `PlayerSquashStretch`: update `HandleMoveStarted`, `HandleTierChanged`, and `HandleMoveStopped` to use separate `HoldFlightDeform(direction, along, across, duration)` vs `ImpactSquashAndRecover(...)` sequences (always `.DOKill()`, `.SetUpdate(true)`, `.SetLink(gameObject)`).
* **Inspector / Prefab Wiring**:
  * Toggle `Main -> Use Unscaled Time = true` on `WallImpactBurst.prefab` and `WallImpactBreak.prefab`.
  * Duplicate `WallImpactBurst.prefab` into `Assets/Prefabs/VFX/KineticRamRing.prefab` (single ring emitter with `Glow VFX.mat`, `useUnscaledTime = true`).
  * On `Player` in `GameScene.unity`: set `PlayerSquashStretch.maxDeformation = 0.48`; assign `hitFlashMaterial` (`Assets/Shaders/Hit/Hit Flash White.mat`), `playerSpriteRenderer` (`Sprite`), and `kineticRamRingPrefab` on `PlayerImpactFeedback`.

### Pillar 5: Predictive Buffered-Turn Lock-In & Magnetic Anchor Snap

* **Exact Specs & Inspector Parameters**:
