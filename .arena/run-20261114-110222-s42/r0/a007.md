# Gravita — Game Feel Signature Design Blueprint

**Project:** Gravita (`Puzzle 2D`, Unity 6000.4.0f1 URP)  
**Design Goal:** Transform Gravita's deterministic stop-to-stop ice-slide movement (`moveSpeed = 30`, `cellSize = 1`) from functional puzzle traversal into an addictive, tactile, high-velocity **"kinetic pinball meets precision lab instrument"** feel—using the project's existing 4-Layer SRP event architecture, URP 2D Volume/Shader stack, DOTween, Cinemachine, `ObjectPoolManager`/`VfxSpawner`, and `Assets/Audio/Sounds/` libraries without ever touching `FixedUpdate` physics determinism.

---

## 1. Core Game Feel Diagnosis: Why Gravita Currently Feels "Flat"

Despite already having dedicated feedback scripts (`PlayerImpactFeedback`, `PlayerSquashStretch`, `PlayerLineTrail`, `CinemachineCameraZoom2D`, `CloseCallDetector`, `InteractionEffectPlayer`, `GameplayHapticsFeedback`, `EntityAudio`), real-world feel falls flat because **timing physics, time-scale, audio data, and prefab wiring undercut each other at runtime**:

1. **The `33.3 ms` Micro-Burst Problem (`moveSpeed = 30` tiles/sec):**
   - At `cellSize = 1` and `moveSpeed = 30`, a 2-tile `Drift` finishes in **66.7 ms** (4 frames at 60 fps), a 4-tile `Cruise` finishes in **133.3 ms**, and an 8-tile `KineticRam` finishes in **266.7 ms** (`48` tiles/sec during `VectorLauncher` `1.6x` `Phasing`).
   - Because individual slides are razor-short ballistic bursts, slow ease curves or single start/stop triggers barely register. Gravita's feel must live inside **33.3 ms per-tile rhythmic cadence**, **pre-stop input anticipation**, **directional impact contrast**, and **unscaled post-stop resonance**.

2. **`Time.timeScale = 0` Hit-Stop Freezes Your Own Impact Feedback:**
   - `PlayerImpactFeedback` sets `Time.timeScale = 0f` on stop (`tier2HitStop = 0.033s`, `tier3HitStop = 0.083s`).
   - However, `PlayerSquashStretch`'s `DOTween.Sequence()` omits `.SetUpdate(true)`, `PlayerLineTrail` retracts using `Time.deltaTime`, and `CinemachineCameraZoom2D` lerps using `Time.deltaTime`.
   - **2nd-order effect:** At the exact millisecond of a heavy `Cruise` or `KineticRam` collision, squash deformation, trail snap-retraction, and camera zoom **freeze completely**, turning a punchy freeze-frame into a dead stutter.

3. **Zero Velocity Contrast Between Stops, Anchors & Redirects:**
   - In `GameScene.unity`, `PlayerImpactFeedback` sets `tier1Shake = 0` and `redirectShake = 0`.
   - `PlayerImpactFeedback.HandleStopped()` triggers wall shake/hit-stop without checking `PlayerMovement.WasLastStopWallImpact`, so stopping cleanly on a `BrakeTile` or `GravityBlock` (`HaltAt`, `WasLastStopWallImpact = false`) gets treated like a wall crash or feels uncalibrated.
   - `PlayerSquashStretch` uses `wallPressPx = 4` on a child `Sprite` whose transform sits at `m_LocalPosition.y = -0.5` (bottom-center `(0.5, 0)` sprite pivot). Without compensating press direction against the sprite's pivot, horizontal/ceiling squash shrinks *away* from the wall instead of pinning flush against `LastImpactPoint`.
   - `PlayerLineTrail` clamps `smoothedMoveSpeed` to `Mathf.Clamp(instSpeed, 4f, 28f)` (`PlayerLineTrail.cs:292`)—below actual `moveSpeed = 30` and far below `Phasing = 48`—and displays static tier colors with no tier-crossing burst.

4. **Broken, Double-Firing & Empty-Data Bridges Already in the Repo:**
   - **Audio data is empty:** In `Assets/Data/Sound/SoundConfig.asset` (referenced by `Assets/Prefabs/Manager/AudioSystems.prefab`), **all 23 `SoundEntry.clips` arrays are `[]`**, and `AudioSystems.prefab` has unassigned `MusicManager.musicSource` / `ambientSource` and `SoundManager.pooledAudioSourcePrefab` / `fallbackAudioSource`. Yet complete sci-fi/UI SFX packs already sit unused in `Assets/Audio/Sounds/` (`kenney_sci-fi-sounds`, `sci-fi-sfx`, `Shapeforms Audio Free Sound Effects`, `kenney_ui-audio`, `JDSherbert - Ultimate UI SFX Pack (FREE)`).
   - **Redirect double-trigger:** `PlayerMovement.Redirect()` invokes BOTH `OnRedirected` AND `OnMoveStarted` (`PlayerMovement.cs:997-998`), causing `EntityAudio` to overlap `wedgeRedirect` + `moveStart` on the same frame and resetting corner flow.
   - **Orphaned systems with 0 consumers:**
     - `CinemachineCameraZoom2D` (`normalOrthographicSize = 5.5`) has **0 callers** anywhere in `Assets/Scripts/`.
     - `CloseCallDetector.OnCloseCallTriggered` (`closeCallWindowSeconds = 0.15`, `enemyNearMissRadius = 1.08`, `bonusCoins = 5`) has **0 subscribers**.
     - `GameplayHapticsFeedback` implements only `WallImpact` and `GravityAnchor`, leaving `PadRedirect`, `LaunchStart`, `SwitchToggle`, `Death`, and `UISelect` in `HapticEvent` unused.
     - `InteractionEffectPlayer` on `Wedge.prefab`, `Brake.prefab`, `Exit.prefab`, and `Hazard.prefab` has `effectPrefabs: []`, `defaultCameraShake: 0`, `rules: []`; `Cracked.prefab` and `Brittle.prefab` have `rules: []` (missing distinct `Hit` vs `Break` / `Crack` vs `Break` feedback); `PulseLaser.prefab` leaves `inactiveVisual`, `warningVisual`, and `activeVisual` null.
     - `Global Volume Profile.asset` mounts `Bloom`, `Vignette`, `ChromaticAberration`, and `ColorAdjustments`, yet nothing modulates them per speed tier or near-miss.
     - `PowerUpController` (`lines 928, 1452, 1480`) still creates procedural `new GameObject(...)` + `AddComponent<SpriteRenderer>()`, violating pooling & architectural rules.

## 2. Foundation Baseline: Fix Before Layering (Minimum Viable Feel)

Before adding new systems, repair the broken signal pipeline so **Domain Events -> Presentation Observers -> Time/Audio/VFX/Haptics** work reliably without fighting `Time.timeScale = 0`.

```text
PlayerMovement (Domain, FixedUpdate @ 30–48 tiles/s)
  ├── OnMoveStarted(dir)          ──▶ Launch puff, trail start, EntityAudio.moveStart
  ├── OnPendingDirectionChanged   ──▶ [NEW] Pillar 2: Cocked-Spring chevron + tick
  ├── OnTileCrossed(tiles)        ──▶ Pillar 1: Per-tile metronome + Kinetic afterimage
  ├── OnVelocityTierChanged(tier) ──▶ Pillar 1: Ortho zoom + Volume ramp + Shock-stretch
  ├── OnPhasingChanged(phasing)   ──▶ Pillar 1: Cyan HDR flash + Phasing trail/zoom
  ├── OnRedirected(dir)           ──▶ Pillar 3: Escalating chain pitch (NO OnMoveStarted!)
  ├── OnWallImpact(point)         ──▶ Pillar 2: Wall press + flash + WallImpactBurst
  └── OnMoveStopped               ──▶ Check WasLastStopWallImpact: Wall Slam vs Anchor Clamp

Shared Coordination Layer (Presentation Only)
  ├── TimeScaleCoordinator        ──▶ Arbitrates HitStop (0f), CloseCall (0.35f), Pause (0f)
  ├── CinemachineCameraZoom2D     ──▶ Unscaled ortho breathing (4.95..6.05 around 5.5)
  ├── SoundManager + Pitch API    ──▶ PlaySFX(entry, pitchOverride) preserving minInterval
  └── PlayerVolumeDriver          ──▶ Runtime Clone of Global Volume Profile overrides
```

### Required Plumbing Fixes (Zero Gameplay Logic Change)
1. **Create `TimeScaleCoordinator` (MonoBehaviour singleton on Feedback Systems):**
   - Replace direct `Time.timeScale = 0f` writes in `PlayerImpactFeedback` with priority requests so Hit-Stop, Close-Call Time Dilation (`0.35x`), and Pause (`0f`) never overwrite each other or strand `Time.timeScale < 1f` across scene restarts/resets:
     - Priority order: `Paused (0f)` > `HitStop (0f, unscaled timer)` > `TimeDilation (min active scale, unscaled timer)` > `Normal (1f)`.
     - Reset all transient requests in `LevelFlowController` restart/load handlers and `OnDisable()`.
2. **Make `PlayerSquashStretch` & `PlayerLineTrail` & `CinemachineCameraZoom2D` Unscaled-Safe:**
   - `PlayerSquashStretch.cs`: Add `.DOKill()`, `.SetUpdate(true)`, `.SetLink(gameObject)` on `activeSequence` and reset tweens. Apply `wallPressPx` along `LastImpactDirection` on the child visual while offsetting toward the collided face so bottom-center `(0.5, 0)` sprite pivots never pull away from walls.
   - `PlayerLineTrail.cs`: Switch retraction step to `float dt = Time.unscaledDeltaTime;`, raise speed clamp from `Mathf.Clamp(instSpeed, 4f, 28f)` to `Mathf.Clamp(instSpeed, 4f, 52f)` (covering `30` normal and `48` `Phasing`), and snap-retract cleanly on `WasLastStopWallImpact == true` vs soft-settle (`0.65x` retract multiplier) on anchor stops.
   - `CinemachineCameraZoom2D.cs`: Change `Time.deltaTime * zoomSpeed` to `Time.unscaledDeltaTime * zoomSpeed` and expose `PulseOrthographicSize(float punchSize, float holdUnscaledSec, float returnTarget, float returnSpeed)` using a single `.DOKill().SetUpdate(true).SetLink(gameObject)` DOTween sequence or coroutine over `Time.unscaledDeltaTime`.
3. **Eliminate the `Redirect()` Double-SFX Bug & Distinguish `WasLastStopWallImpact`:**
   - In `PlayerMovement.Redirect()`: keep `OnRedirected?.Invoke(moveDirection);` and remove redundant `OnMoveStarted?.Invoke(moveDirection);` (or expose an `OnSlideResumed` only for fresh `Idle -> Moving` starts—audit `InGameHUD`/`LevelFlowController` subscribers; `EntityAudio` only wants `moveStart` on `Idle -> Moving`, while `Trail`/step counters use `OnMoveStarted` or `OnRedirected`). Cleanest minimal fix if external UI relies on `OnMoveStarted` for move counting: keep `OnMoveStarted` only on `StartMoving(...)` and have `Redirect()` fire ONLY `OnRedirected` (redirects are free directional banks within one move; verify move counter only increments on `Idle -> Moving`, preserving exact puzzle par rules!).
   - In `PlayerImpactFeedback.HandleStopped()`: separate `if (movement.WasLastStopWallImpact)` (wall shake + hit-stop) from `!WasLastStopWallImpact` (skip wall shake; delegate floor anchor clamp to `GravityAnchor` feedback).
4. **Extend `SoundManager` for Musical Pitch Escalation:**
   - Add overload `public void PlaySFX(SoundEntry entry, float pitchOverride)` that shares `CanPlay(entry)` cooldown/interval logic and bypasses random variance when `pitchOverride > 0f`.
5. **Unblock `AudioSystems.prefab` & `Assets/Data/Sound/SoundConfig.asset`:**
   - Wire `MusicManager.musicSource` & `ambientSource`, plus `SoundManager.pooledAudioSourcePrefab` & `fallbackAudioSource` on `AudioSystems.prefab`.
   - Populate all 23 empty `SoundEntry.clips` slots in `SoundConfig.asset` from existing `Assets/Audio/Sounds/` packs (`kenney_sci-fi-sounds`, `sci-fi-sfx`, `Shapeforms Audio Free Sound Effects`, `kenney_ui-audio`, `JDSherbert - Ultimate UI SFX Pack (FREE)`).

## 3. Five Signature Game Feel Pillars for Gravita

### Pillar 1 — Kinetic Runway Cadence: "Speed You Can Hear, See & Breathe"

#### Why It Makes Gravita Uniquely Addictive
In stop-to-stop ice puzzles, speed is normally invisible until you hit a wall. Because Gravita gates mechanics like `CrackedBlock` (`requiredTier = KineticRam`) and `OverloadedFloor` (`minimumSafeTier = Cruise`) on discrete tile velocity tiers, players *need* visceral, second-nature awareness of every threshold crossed during a `0.15s–0.30s` slide. Turning each crossed tile into an ascending kinetic pulse—backed by camera breathing, post-processing wind-tunnel optics, and velocity afterimages—turns long straightaways into **high-speed runway launches**.

#### Exact Parameters, Curves & Tuning Values
| Event / State | Visual & Camera Response | Post-Processing (`Global Volume`) | Audio Response | Timing / Curve |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`OnTileCrossed(n)`** | Micro-trail pulse (`+8%` head width for `35 ms`) | — | `moveTileTick` vol `0.18 -> 0.36`; pitch `0.90 + Mathf.Min(n, 10) * 0.045f` (`0.90 -> 1.35`) | Every `33.3 ms` (`minInterval = 0.025s`) |
| **`Drift` (Tiles 1–2)** | `CinemachineCameraZoom2D` target = `5.50` (`zoomSpeed = 6`) | Baseline (`Bloom 9.0`, `CA 0.20`, `Vignette 0.40`) | `moveStart` transient click + air whoosh | Immediate |
| **`Cruise` (Tiles 3–4)** | Ortho widen `5.50 -> 5.68` (`zoomSpeed = 6`); child stretch spike `+0.08` for `65 ms`; `cruiseWidthScale = 1.2` | `Bloom -> 10.2`, `CA -> 0.30` | `tierCruiseUp` harmonic blip (pitch `1.05`, vol `0.45`) | `70 ms` `Ease.OutQuad` |
| **`KineticRam` (Tiles 5+)** | Ortho widen `-> 5.88`; impulse thump `0.045`; spawn pooled `KineticAfterimage.prefab` every `1.0` tile (`33 ms`); `kineticRamWidthScale = 1.45` | `Bloom -> 11.8`, `CA -> 0.44`, `Vignette -> 0.48` | `tierRamLock` resonant turbine lock (pitch `1.0`, vol `0.65`) | Afterimage fade `110 ms` unscaled (`alpha 0.42 -> 0`) |
| **`Phasing` (`VectorLauncher`, `48 t/s`)** | Ortho widen `-> 6.05`; HDR flash (`Hit Flash White.mat`, `_FlashColor = (0.6, 1.8, 2.2, 1)`) for `45 ms`; afterimage every `0.5` tiles | `Bloom -> 13.0`, `CA -> 0.58`, `Vignette -> 0.52` | `HapticEvent.LaunchStart` + phase warp burst | Hold flash `45 ms`, trail cyan glow |
| **`OnMoveStopped`** | Ortho snap back `-> 5.50` (`zoomSpeed = 10` on wall slam, `6` on anchor) | Ramp back to baseline in `140 ms` unscaled | Cut runway tick immediately | `140–180 ms` `Ease.OutCubic` |

*(Note: Keep `VelocityTierUtil.FromTiles` thresholds `1–2 Drift`, `3–4 Cruise`, `5+ KineticRam` aligned cleanly with visual/audio feedback—or if design balances `4–7 / 8+` later, all presentation subscribers react strictly to `OnVelocityTierChanged`, never hardcoding tile thresholds!)*

#### C# Integration Hooks (Layer 1 — Presentation Only)
- **`KineticVelocityFeedback : MonoBehaviour`** (attach alongside `PlayerSquashStretch` on `Player`):
  - Subscribes to `PlayerMovement.OnTileCrossed`, `OnVelocityTierChanged`, `OnPhasingChanged`, `OnMoveStopped`.
  - Spawns `KineticAfterimage.prefab` via `ObjectPoolManager.Spawn` (or `VfxSpawner.Spawn`) matching the active `Player` `SpriteRenderer.sprite` and `flipX`, triggering `PooledAfterimage.PlayFade(color, duration: 0.11f)` (`DOFade(0f, 0.11f).DOKill().SetUpdate(true).SetLink(gameObject)`).
  - Drives `CinemachineCameraZoom2D.Instance.SetTargetOrthographicSize(...)` per tier.
- **`PlayerVolumeDriver : MonoBehaviour`** (on Feedback Systems or Global Volume object):
  - At `Awake()`, instantiates a runtime copy of `Global Volume Profile.asset` (`volume.profile = Instantiate(baseProfile)`) so live tweens **never dirty the `.asset` file on disk**.
  - Tweens `Bloom.intensity`, `ChromaticAberration.intensity`, and `Vignette.intensity` with `DOTween.To(...).SetUpdate(true).SetLink(gameObject)`.

#### Unity Inspector / Prefab Wiring
- **New Pooled Prefab:** `Assets/Prefabs/VFX/KineticAfterimage.prefab` — root `SpriteRenderer` (assigned `Assets/Shaders/Glow/Mind World/Glow Player.mat`, sorting order `Player - 1`) + `PooledAfterimage` script calling `ObjectPoolManager.Despawn` on tween complete.
- **Assign `[SerializeField]`:** Reference `KineticAfterimage.prefab` on `KineticVelocityFeedback` in `GameScene.unity`; assign `Volume` component on `PlayerVolumeDriver`. Zero `new GameObject` or runtime `AddComponent`.

### Pillar 2 — Stop-to-Stop Polarity: "Cocked Spring" Turn Buffer & Anchor vs. Slam Contrast

#### Why It Makes Gravita Uniquely Addictive
Gravita uses `bufferTurnsUntilStop = true` and features floor anchors (`BrakeTile`, `GravityBlock`) that halt you dead mid-room with `IsAwaitingFreshInput = true`. Right now, players experience buffered turns blindly—if a swipe registers 2 frames before stopping, nothing confirms it until motion snaps—and stopping on a `BrakeTile` lacks the distinct "magnetic lock" sensation versus slamming into concrete. Giving queued inputs a visual/audio **"cocked spring"** and splitting **Wall Slam** from **Magnetic Anchor** sharpens puzzle control into tactile precision.

#### Exact Parameters, Curves & Tuning Values
- **"Cocked Spring" Turn Buffer (`PlayerMovement.HasPendingDirection`):**
  - **Trigger:** When `pendingDirection` becomes non-zero mid-slide (or changes queued direction).
  - **Visual:** Activate pre-authored child `QueuedTurnChevron` at `localPosition = pendingDir * 0.62f`, rotated toward `pendingDir`. Tween scale `(0.55 -> 1.12 -> 0.96)` over `65 ms` (`Ease.OutBack`, `.DOKill().SetUpdate(true).SetLink(gameObject)`). Tint HDR cyan `(0.4, 1.5, 2.0, 1)`.
  - **Release:** The exact frame the player stops and consumes `pendingDirection` into a new slide, flare chevron white (`_FlashColor`) for `35 ms`, spawn `Particle Smoke.prefab` (`0.45x` scale, oriented along `-newMoveDir`) as a push-off jet, then hide chevron.
  - **Audio:** Crisp high-frequency latch click (`turnBuffered`, pitch `1.18`, vol `0.30`, `minInterval = 0.04s`).
- **Hard Wall Slam (`WasLastStopWallImpact == true`):**
  - **Hit-Stop & Trauma (via `TimeScaleCoordinator`):**
    - `Drift`: Shake `0.045` (fix current `0`), Hit-Stop `0 ms`, Haptic none/light (`WallImpact` gated to `>= Cruise` or subtle tick).
    - `Cruise`: Shake `0.115`, Hit-Stop `33 ms` (`0.033s`), `HapticEvent.WallImpact`.
    - `KineticRam`: Shake `0.22`, Hit-Stop `83 ms` (`0.083s`), `HapticEvent.WallImpact`.
  - **Directional Wall-Pin & Flash:** Apply `wallPressPx = 4` (`0.125` units at 32 PPU) *toward* `LastImpactDirection` (compensating for the `(0.5, 0)` sprite pivot so top/bottom/side edges press flush into the wall tile!), then ease back `(1 -> -0.06 * dir -> 0)` over `95 ms` (`Ease.OutBack`). Swap overlay/tint to `Hit Flash White.mat` (`_FlashColor = (1, 1, 1, 1)`) for `38 ms` unscaled on `Cruise` / `KineticRam`.
  - **VFX:** `VfxSpawner.Spawn(WallImpactBurst.prefab, LastImpactPoint)` aligned to `-moveDir`.
- **Magnetic Anchor Clamp (`GravityBlock` / `BrakeTile`, `WasLastStopWallImpact == false`):**
  - **Hit-Stop & Shake:** `0 ms` hit-stop (instant control lock without freeze), `cameraShake = 0.04` micro-thud, `HapticEvent.GravityAnchor`.
  - **Vertical Clamp Squash:** Flatten both axes slightly down onto the tile `(1.18, 0.82)` -> `(1.0, 1.0)` over `110 ms` (`Ease.OutQuad`, `.SetUpdate(true)`), **zero** directional wall rebound.
  - **Tethered Hold State (`IsAwaitingFreshInput`):** Show pre-authored child `AnchorTetherRing` around player pulsing `alpha 0.30 <-> 0.70` at `2.2 Hz` until player lifts/swipes fresh input—giving unmistakable readability why held keys stay clamped on `GravityBlock`.
  - **Audio:** Low resonant magnetic lock (`anchorClamp`, pitch `0.85`, vol `0.70`).

#### C# Integration Hooks
- **`PlayerMovement.cs`:** Expose `public event Action<Vector2> OnPendingDirectionChanged;` (invoked only when `pendingDirection` mutates, including reset to `Vector2.zero`) and `public event Action<bool> OnAwaitingFreshInputChanged;` alongside existing `HasPendingDirection` and `IsAwaitingFreshInput`.
- **`QueuedTurnFeedback : MonoBehaviour`** (child/component on `Player`): Subscribes to `OnPendingDirectionChanged`, `OnAwaitingFreshInputChanged`, `OnMoveStarted`.
- **`PlayerImpactFeedback.cs`:** Branch `HandleStopped()` on `movement.WasLastStopWallImpact` and route `tier1Shake = 0.045f` + `HitStop` through `TimeScaleCoordinator`.

#### Unity Inspector / Prefab Wiring
- Add two pre-authored inactive child objects under `Player`'s visual root in `GameScene.unity`: `TurnBufferChevron` (`SpriteRenderer` using `Glow VFX.mat`) and `AnchorTetherRing` (`SpriteRenderer`), assigned via `[SerializeField]` on `QueuedTurnFeedback`.
- Populate `InteractionEffectPlayer` on `Assets/Prefabs/Gameplay/Brake.prefab` and `GravityBlock.prefab`: add pooled clamp shock-ring prefab (`AnchorClampRing.prefab`), rule `kind = InteractionKind.Brake`, `defaultCameraShake = 0.04`.

### Pillar 3 — Billiard Bank-Shot Combo & Musical Corridor Arpeggios

#### Why It Makes Gravita Uniquely Addictive
Gravita's puzzle flow shines when a single swipe banks across `DeflectionWedge`s (`OnRedirected`, preserving `TilesTraveled`!) through a corridor of `Coin`s and shatters a `CrackedBlock` on tile 8. Turning chained ricochets and coin pickups into a **dynamic musical instrument + escalating spark trail** creates intense "one-more-try" flow state—players actively crave cleaner routes just to hear and feel the chain complete.

#### Exact Parameters, Curves & Tuning Values
- **Chained Redirect Escalation (`OnRedirected(Vector2 newDir)` up to `maxConsecutiveRedirects = 8`):**
  - Maintain `redirectChainCount` in presentation (increments on `OnRedirected`, resets on `OnMoveStopped` or level reset).
  - **Audio Pitch Ladder (`wedgeRedirect`):** Play via `SoundManager.PlaySFX(soundConfig.wedgeRedirect, pitch)` with exact ascending steps:  
    `[1.000f, 1.059f, 1.122f, 1.189f, 1.260f, 1.335f, 1.414f, 1.498f]` (chromatic-to-perfect-fifth climb, clamped at step 8).
  - **Corner Snap Feedback:**
    - `PlayerImpactFeedback.redirectShake = 0.055f + Mathf.Min(redirectChainCount, 6) * 0.01f` (fix current `0`).
    - Trigger `HapticEvent.PadRedirect` inside `GameplayHapticsFeedback` (subscribing to `PlayerMovement.OnRedirected`).
    - Flash trail corner vertex for `40 ms` (temporary width spike `1.45x` on `PlayerLineTrail`) and emit directional corner sparks via `InteractionEffectPlayer` (`alignToInteractionDirection = true`).
    - Apply quick `15° -> 0°` torsional snap or directional stretch re-alignment (`PlayerSquashStretch.Deform(stretch: 0.14f, duration: 0.09f)`, `.SetUpdate(true)`).
- **Corridor Coin Arpeggio (`Collectible.OnAnyCoinCollected`):**
  - Maintain `coinChainCount` (increments per coin within `0.45s` unscaled window or within same continuous slide; resets on `OnMoveStopped`).
  - **Pentatonic Pitch Ladder (`coinCollect`, `minInterval = 0.02s`):**  
    `[1.000f, 1.122f, 1.260f, 1.498f, 1.682f, 2.000f]` (C major pentatonic ascending up to +1 octave).
  - Visual: Spawn `PoppingVFX.prefab` + quick `30 ms` additive glow pulse (`Bloom` blip `+0.6` for `60 ms` unscaled).
- **`CrackedBlock` Chain-Smash Payoff (`CrackedBlock.OnAnyBroken` vs Blocked `Hit`):**
  - **Blocked Hit (`currentTier < requiredTier`):** Fire `InteractionKind.Hit` -> small dust puff (`Particle Smoke.prefab` `0.4x`), dull ceramic bounce SFX (`wallImpactDrift` pitch `1.25`), weak `0.04` shake—instantly teaching *"not enough speed!"* without breaking block.
  - **Ram Shatter (`currentTier >= KineticRam`):** Fire `InteractionKind.Break` -> `TimeScaleCoordinator.RequestHitStop(0.083f)`, `WallImpactBreak.prefab` + `Particle Explosion.prefab`, `cameraShake = 0.22f + Mathf.Min(redirectChainCount, 3) * 0.03f`, `CinemachineCameraZoom2D` punch (`5.5 -> 5.25 -> 5.5` over `150 ms` unscaled), and `SoundManager.PlaySFX(crackedWallBreak, 1.0f + Mathf.Min(redirectChainCount, 4) * 0.04f)`.

#### C# Integration Hooks
- **`ComboFeedbackController : MonoBehaviour`** (Layer 1 on `Player` / Feedback Systems):
  - Subscribes to `PlayerMovement.OnRedirected`, `PlayerMovement.OnMoveStopped`, `Collectible.OnAnyCoinCollected`, `CrackedBlock.OnAnyBroken`, `LevelFlowController` reset events.
  - Exposes `redirectChainCount` / `coinChainCount` purely for audio/VFX pitch & intensity scaling (never touches gameplay score/economy).
- **`GameplayHapticsFeedback.cs`:** Subscribe `OnRedirected` -> `GameHaptics.Trigger(HapticEvent.PadRedirect)`.
- **`EntityAudio.cs`:** Remove redundant `moveStart` playback on redirects once `PlayerMovement.Redirect()` stops double-firing `OnMoveStarted`, and delegate `wedgeRedirect` + `coinCollect` + `crackedWallBreak` pitch overrides via `ComboFeedbackController`.

#### Unity Inspector / Prefab Wiring
- **`Assets/Prefabs/Gameplay/Wedge.prefab` (`InteractionEffectPlayer`):** Replace empty setup with **2 rules**:
  1. `kind = InteractionKind.Deflect`: assign cyan ricochet spark prefab (`Glow Particle.mat` directional burst), `alignToInteractionDirection = true`, `cameraShake = 0.055`.
  2. `kind = InteractionKind.Hit` (blunt side stop): assign dull wall spark (`WallImpactBurst.prefab`), `cameraShake = 0.035`.
- **`Assets/Prefabs/Gameplay/VectorPad.prefab` (`InteractionEffectPlayer`):** Assign cyan directional chevron burst prefab, `alignToInteractionDirection = true`, `defaultCameraShake = 0.05`.
- **`Assets/Prefabs/Gameplay/Cracked.prefab` (`InteractionEffectPlayer`):** Split empty `rules` into **2 rules**: `InteractionKind.Break` -> `WallImpactBreak.prefab` + `cameraShake = 0.22`, and `InteractionKind.Hit` -> `Particle Smoke.prefab` + `cameraShake = 0.04`.

### Pillar 4 — Close-Call "Adrenaline Snapshot" (`CloseCallDetector`)

#### Why It Makes Gravita Uniquely Addictive
`CloseCallDetector` already exists in the codebase (`closeCallWindowSeconds = 0.15s`, `enemyNearMissRadius = 1.08`, `perHazardCooldown = 1.5s`, `bonusCoins = 5`), firing `OnCloseCallTriggered(Vector3)` whenever the player barely escapes a `PulseLaser` flash or `SentinelDronePatrol` sweep. Currently, **zero scripts listen to it**, so players don't even realize they pulled off a clutch dodge. Weaponizing this moment with a micro time-dilation snapshot turns scary hazards from passive punishments into **thrilling skill expression**.

#### Exact Parameters, Curves & Tuning Values
| Feedback Channel | Exact Specification | Timing (Unscaled) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Time Dilation** | `TimeScaleCoordinator.RequestTimeDilation(scale: 0.35f, duration: 0.055f)` | `55 ms` hold -> instant restore to `1.0f` |

## 5. Architecture & Coupling Guardrails

- **Strict 4-Layer SRP Compliance:** Domain classes (`PlayerMovement`, `CloseCallDetector`, `HazardActivation`, `CircuitSwitch`, `CrackedBlock`) only emit C# events or state snapshots—never reference `SoundManager`, `DOTween`, `Volume`, or `CinemachineCameraZoom2D`.
- **Zero Procedural Scene Mutating:** No `new GameObject(...)` or runtime `AddComponent<...>()` for VFX/UI; every transient ring, spark, afterimage, bolt, or world callout must be a pre-authored prefab spawned through `VfxSpawner.Spawn` / `ObjectPoolManager` and wired via `[SerializeField]`.
- **Audio Anti-Spam at `30–48 tiles/sec`:** Per-tile ticks (`minInterval = 0.025s`), laser warning hums (`minInterval = 0.15s`), and coin arpeggios (`minInterval = 0.02s`) rely on `SoundManager` channel pooling (`initialPoolSize >= 16`) so high-speed corridors never clip voices.
- **Deterministic Physics Intact:** All camera pulses, squash/stretch deformations, hit-stops, and afterimages run strictly on child visuals or unscaled presentation timers (`Time.unscaledDeltaTime`, `.SetUpdate(true)`), guaranteeing identical puzzle trajectories on 30, 60, and 120 Hz devices.
