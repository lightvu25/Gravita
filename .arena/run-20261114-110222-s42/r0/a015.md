# Gravita (Puzzle 2D) — Game Feel Signature Upgrade Blueprint

## Assumptions & Codebase Calibration
- **Velocity Tier Source of Truth**: In the current codebase, `VelocityTierUtil.FromTiles` (`Assets/Scripts/Gameplay/VelocityTier.cs`) maps `1–2` tiles to `Drift`, `3–4` to `Cruise`, and `5+` to `KineticRam` (whereas the GDD lists `1–3`, `4–7`, `8+`). All feedback specifications below bind directly to the `VelocityTier` enum and `PlayerMovement.CurrentTier` so the game feel stays synchronized regardless of threshold tuning.
- **Scale & Timing Baseline**: At `moveSpeed = 30` units/sec and `cellSize = 1` (`GameScene.unity`), Subject G-0 crosses **1 tile every `33.3 ms`** (`~2` frames at 60 FPS), reaching `Cruise` in `100 ms` and `KineticRam` in `166.7 ms` (current thresholds) or `266.7 ms` (GDD thresholds). Feedback envelopes must snap within `20–120 ms` to feel attached to the input, not sluggish.
- **Audio Assets**: `Assets/Data/Sound/SoundConfig.asset` currently has empty `clips: []` slots across entries; new `SoundEntry` hooks include fallback routing so existing/imported clips work immediately via pitch/volume shaping.

## 1. Core Game Feel Diagnosis: Why Gravita Currently Feels Flat

Gravita already has solid event architecture (`PlayerMovement`, `InteractionEffectPlayer`, `CloseCallDetector`, `CinemachineCameraShake2D`, `CinemachineCameraZoom2D`), yet gameplay feels muted for four specific technical reasons observed in the current project:

1. **Zeroed Low-End Impact & Un-directed Camera Response**:
   - In `GameScene.unity`, `PlayerImpactFeedback` sets `tier1Shake = 0` and `redirectShake = 0` (`tier2Shake = 0.1`, `tier3Shake = 0.2`). Every 1–2 tile hop and every `DeflectionWedge`/`VectorPad` rebound has zero camera punch.
   - `CinemachineCameraShake2D.ShakeCamera(float)` calls isotropic `impulseSource.GenerateImpulse(force)` instead of directional velocity impulses along the slide axis.
   - `CinemachineCameraZoom2D` (wired on `CinemachineCamera` at `normalOrthographicSize = 5.5`, `zoomSpeed = 4`) is **never called anywhere in code**, and its `Update()` lerps with scaled `Time.deltaTime`, freezing during hit-stop.
2. **Hit-Stop Freezes Squash Animation + Bottom-Center Pivot Pulls Away From Ceilings**:
   - `PlayerImpactFeedback.PulseHitStop` drops `Time.timeScale = 0f` for `0.033s` (`Cruise`) and `0.083s` (`KineticRam`) inside `HandleStopped()`. At that exact frame, `PlayerSquashStretch.Play()` creates a `DOTween.Sequence()` **without `.SetUpdate(true)`**, freezing the sprite at frame 0 until hit-stop ends instead of locking into wall compression *during* the freeze. (Additionally, `PlayerImpactFeedback.OnDisable()` kills `hitStopRoutine` without restoring `Time.timeScale`, risking a stuck freeze if disabled mid-stop.)
   - Subject G-0's visual `Sprite` child sits at `localPosition = (0, -0.5, 0)` (bottom-center `(0.5, 0)` pivot) and `maxDeformation = 0.3` in `GameScene.unity` clips `impactHeightPx/movingHeightPx - 1` (`16/28 - 1 = -0.429` down to `-0.30`). Scaling around bottom-center on a North (`Vector2.up`) ceiling impact pulls the sprite top downward (`~5.6 px` gap away from the wall) instead of pressing flush into the impact face.
3. **Silent Momentum Escalation & Non-Musical Rebounds**:
   - `OnTileCrossed`, `OnVelocityTierChanged`, `OnPhasingChanged`, and `MazeTile.OnAnyPlayerInteracted` have **zero audio subscribers** in `EntityAudio`.
   - `EntityAudio.HandlePlayerRedirected` and `HandleCoinCollected` play fixed entries with random pitch (`±8%`) instead of rising harmonic pitch across chained bank shots and coin lines.
4. **Disconnected High-Drama Signals & Missing Forward Readability**:
   - `CloseCallDetector.OnCloseCallTriggered` has **0 subscribers** in audio, camera, haptics, or post-processing (`closeCallVfxPrefab` falls back to `Particle Star`).
   - `PlayerMovement` silently buffers perpendicular turns (`bufferTurnsUntilStop = true`) with no character-side indicator confirming a queued turn, and `PlayerLineTrail` only paints speed behind the player rather than ahead of their gaze.
   - `Brake.prefab`, `Wedge.prefab`, `Exit.prefab`, and `Hazard.prefab` all have empty `InteractionEffectPlayer` lists (`effectPrefabs: []`, `defaultCameraShake: 0`, `rules: []`), and `Global Volume Profile.asset` keeps `ChromaticAberration` (`0.2`), `Vignette` (`0.4`), and `Bloom` (`9`) static instead of reacting to `KineticRam`, `Phasing`, or `CloseCall`.

## 2. Five Gravita-Specific Game Feel Pillars

Every pillar below preserves deterministic `FixedUpdate` cell-center physics, avoids procedural `new GameObject(...)` / runtime `AddComponent<...>()`, and enforces `.DOKill()`, `.SetLink(gameObject)`, and explicit `TimeScale` hygiene.

### Pillar 1: Kinetic Runway Camera Breathing & Hit-Stop-Locked Directional Squash
- **Why it makes Gravita uniquely addictive**:
  At `30 u/s`, slides resolve in `100–300 ms`. Pulling the camera subtly wider as kinetic energy builds (`Drift -> Cruise -> KineticRam -> Phasing`), snapping *into* the wall along the exact impact vector, and freezing Subject G-0 at peak wall-flush compression during Hit-Stop makes stop-to-stop motion feel like firing a magnetized ball bearing inside a pressurized lab.
- **Exact numbers, curves, durations & Inspector parameters**:
  - **`CinemachineCameraZoom2D` (Base `normalOrthographicSize = 5.5f`)**:
    - `Drift`: `targetOrthographicSize = 5.50f` (`zoomSpeed = 6f`).
    - `Cruise`: `5.65f` (`+0.15` ortho pullback, `zoomSpeed = 7f`).
    - `KineticRam`: `5.85f` (`+0.35` ortho pullback, `zoomSpeed = 8f`).
    - `Phasing` (`48 u/s` at `1.6x`): `6.00f` (`+0.50` ortho pullback, `zoomSpeed = 12f`).
    - **Wall Impact Snap-In**: On `OnWallImpact` at `Cruise` (`5.42f`, hold `0.06s`) or `KineticRam` (`5.35f`, hold `0.09s` unscaled), then relax to `5.50f` at `zoomSpeed = 10f`.
    - **Exponential Unscaled Lerp**: Replace `Time.deltaTime * zoomSpeed` in `CinemachineCameraZoom2D.Update()` with `1f - Mathf.Exp(-activeZoomSpeed * Time.unscaledDeltaTime)` so zoom punches execute smoothly during `Time.timeScale = 0` Hit-Stop.
  - **`PlayerImpactFeedback` + Directional `CinemachineCameraShake2D`**:
    - `tier1Shake = 0.055f` (crisp tactile tick on short 1–2 tile hops, replacing `0`).
    - `tier2Shake = 0.12f`, `tier3Shake = 0.26f`.
    - `redirectShake = 0.05f` (impulsed along rebound bisector `(outgoing - incoming).normalized`, attenuated on rapid loops by `Mathf.Pow(0.85f, consecutiveRedirects)`).
    - `crackedBreakShake = 0.14f` + `crackedBreakHitStop = 0.045f` (`~3` frames unscaled crunch on `CrackedBlock.OnAnyBroken` so smashing through mid-flight carries weight without stopping movement).
    - Keep `tier1HitStop = 0f`, `tier2HitStop = 0.033f`, `tier3HitStop = 0.083f`.
  - **`PlayerSquashStretch` (Unscaled + Pivot-Compensated)**:
    - Add `.SetUpdate(true)` to `Play()` and `ResetScale()`.
    - Raise `maxDeformation` in `GameScene.unity` from `0.30` to `0.45` so `16/28` (`-0.429`) reaches full impact squash.
    - **Snap-in envelope**: Compress in `duration * 0.18f` (`~35 ms`, `Ease.OutExpo`), rebound in `duration * 0.82f` (`Ease.OutBack`).
    - **Pivot-flush wall compensation**: Derive rest local sprite bounds from `spriteRenderer.sprite.bounds` so the leading edge along `pressDirection` stays pinned to the hit wall regardless of pivot `(0.5, 0)`:
      for non-zero axis component `d`, `edge = d > 0 ? bounds.max : bounds.min`, `shift = edge * (baseScale - deformedScale) + d * (wallPressPx / 32f)`.
    - **Tier Elongation & Redirect Snap**: Maintain subtle in-flight elongation on `Cruise` (`+0.08` along / `-0.04` across) and `KineticRam` (`+0.15` / `-0.07`); on `OnRedirected`, trigger a `0.09s` snappy corner compression (`-0.14` along incoming, `+0.10` across) before stretching into the new vector.
- **Exact C# integration hooks**:
  - `CinemachineCameraShake2D.cs`: add `ShakeCamera(Vector2 direction, float force)` calling `impulseSource.GenerateImpulseWithVelocity((Vector3)(direction.normalized * force))`.
  - `CinemachineCameraZoom2D.cs`: switch `Update()` to `Time.unscaledDeltaTime`; add `PulseZoom(float punchOrthoSize, float holdUnscaledSeconds)` and `SetTargetOrthographicSize(float target, float speedOverride)`.
  - `PlayerSquashStretch.cs`: add `.SetUpdate(true)`, subscribe to `movement.OnRedirected`, compute wall-flush offset from `spriteRenderer.sprite.bounds`.
  - `PlayerImpactFeedback.cs`: track `private bool isHitStopActive` and `private float hitStopEndUnscaledTime` for re-entrant `CrackedBlock` + wall-stop hit-stops; restore `Time.timeScale` safely in `OnDisable()` and guard against Pause (`UIManager.Instance != null && UIManager.Instance.IsPanelOpen(UIPanelType.Pause)`). Drive `CinemachineCameraZoom2D.Instance` from `HandleTierChanged`, `HandlePhasingChanged`, `HandleWallImpact`, and `HandleStopped`.
- **Unity Inspector / Prefab wiring notes**:
  - On `Player` in `GameScene.unity`: set `PlayerImpactFeedback` to `tier1Shake = 0.055`, `tier2Shake = 0.12`, `tier3Shake = 0.26`, `redirectShake = 0.05`, and `PlayerSquashStretch.maxDeformation = 0.45`. No new scene objects needed.

## 3. Cross-System Safeguards (Edge Cases Resolved)

1. **Hit-Stop vs. Pause & Re-Entrancy**: `PlayerImpactFeedback` tracks `isHitStopActive` and `hitStopEndUnscaledTime` so a `CrackedBlock` smash (`0.045s`) followed within `66 ms` by a `KineticRam` wall stop (`0.083s`) extends the freeze cleanly without overwriting `preHitStopTimeScale = 0f`; `OnDisable()` restores `Time.timeScale = 1f` unless `PauseMenuUI` (`UIManager.Instance.IsPanelOpen(UIPanelType.Pause)`) owns pause.
2. **Pause Freeze Separation**: Player/Camera/Post-FX tweens use `.SetUpdate(true)` (`Time.unscaledDeltaTime`) to stay active during Hit-Stop, while `LabTileFeedback` uses scaled `.SetUpdate(false)` so world telegraphs pause cleanly.
