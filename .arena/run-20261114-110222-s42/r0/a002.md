# Gravita — Kinetic & Tactile Game Feel Upgrade Blueprint

**Target Project:** Puzzle 2D (`Gravita`) — Unity `6000.4.0f1` URP  
**Design Thesis:** Silence and tension before launch, crystalline precision while sliding at `30 u/s`, and heavy surgical punctuation on impact.

---

## Verified Baseline & Runtime Assumptions

All recommendations below are calibrated to verified project parameters:

- **Speed-to-Time Reality (`Assets/Scenes/GameScene.unity`):** `PlayerMovement` runs at `moveSpeed: 30` with `cellSize: 1`. Each crossed grid cell lasts **$33.33\text{ ms}$** ($1 / 30\text{ s}$).
- **Velocity Tiers (`Assets/Scripts/Gameplay/VelocityTier.cs`):** `VelocityTierUtil.FromTiles(int)` currently maps `<= 0` $\rightarrow$ `None`, `1–2` $\rightarrow$ `Drift`, `3–4` $\rightarrow$ `Cruise` (reached at **$100\text{ ms}$**), and `5+` $\rightarrow$ `KineticRam` (reached at **$166.7\text{ ms}$**). All feedback hooks should subscribe to `VelocityTier` transitions rather than hardcoding tile counts so level-pacing retunes (e.g., `1–3` / `4–7` / `8+`) work automatically.
- **Items Requiring Play-Mode Verification (`[Needs Verification]`):**
  - **`CinemachinePixelPerfect` vs. Camera Zoom:** `CinemachineCamera` in `GameScene.unity` holds both `CinemachineCameraZoom2D` and `CinemachinePixelPerfect`, alongside `PixelPerfectCamera` (`m_AssetsPPU: 32`, `320x180`, `m_GridSnapping: 1`) on `Main Camera`. Because `CinemachinePixelPerfect` may overwrite or quantize smooth `Lens.OrthographicSize` interpolation (`5.5` $\pm 0.2\text{–}0.42\text{ u}$), Pillar 1 includes a PixelPerfect-safe **Directional Lookahead** companion.
  - **Settings Persistence & Device Haptics:** `SettingUI` storage keys and `MostHapticsBackend` (`Solo.MOST_IN_ONE`) tactile differentiation on physical iOS/Android hardware were not executed in Play Mode and should be validated when wiring comfort toggles.

## 1. Core Game Feel Diagnosis: Why Gravita Currently Feels "Flat"

Gravita already owns impulse shake, squash/stretch, trails, close-call detection, and post-processing components, yet six specific technical disconnects mute the payoff:

1. **Zero Audio Reality (`Assets/Data/Sound/SoundConfig.asset`):** Every one of the `24` `SoundEntry` fields in `SoundConfig.asset` currently contains `clips: []` (empty), even though audio packs already exist in `Assets/Audio/Sounds/` (`Shapeforms Audio Free Sound Effects`, `kenney_sci-fi-sounds`, `sci-fi-sfx`). In addition, `SoundManager.PlaySFX(SoundEntry)` has no `pitchOverride` parameter, blocking ascending tile and ricochet pitch ladders.
2. **Impact Squash Freezes During Hit-Stop & Separates From North/Side Walls (`PlayerSquashStretch.cs`):**
   - `PlayerImpactFeedback` sets `Time.timeScale = 0f` for `33 ms` (`tier2HitStop: 0.033`) on `Cruise` and `83 ms` (`tier3HitStop: 0.083`) on `KineticRam`, while `PlayerSquashStretch.Play()` and `ResetScale()` build DOTween sequences without `.SetUpdate(true)`. Squash freezes motionless during hit-stop, then snaps afterward.
   - `Player`'s visual `Sprite` child sits at `localPosition (0, -0.5, 0)` with bottom-center pivot `(0.5, 0)` (`Player Icon.aseprite.meta`; cosmetic skins use `y = -0.16666667`). Squashing vertically shrinks *downward* toward the bottom edge, pulling the sprite visibly *off* the North wall instead of pinning flush against the surface. Moreover, `HandlePhasingChanged` writes `spriteRenderer.color` directly, conflicting with `PlayerCosmeticApplier`.
3. **Colliding `Time.timeScale` Ownership & `Redirect()` Double-Fire:**
   - `PlayerImpactFeedback.HitStopRoutine` saves `preHitStopTimeScale` per coroutine; overlapping wall stops, `CrackedBlock.OnAnyBroken`, or future skill hit-stops can capture `0f` and lock gameplay frozen.
   - `PlayerMovement.Redirect()` invokes *both* `OnRedirected` and `OnMoveStarted`. Listeners that reset combos or fire launch feedback inside `OnMoveStarted` break mid-ricochet.
4. **Dormant Framing (`CinemachineCameraZoom2D`) & Unwired Near-Miss (`CloseCallDetector`):**
   - `CinemachineCameraZoom2D` (`normalOrthographicSize: 5.5`, `zoomSpeed: 4`) has **zero callers** across `Assets/Scripts/`.
   - `CloseCallDetector.OnCloseCallTriggered` (`Action<Vector3>`) has **zero subscribers**, and its `bonusCoins` (`5`) reward is suppressed by `EconomyBalanceConfig.Active.EnableLegacyExtraRewardSystems == false`.
5. **Silent Tile Prefabs & Incomplete Haptics (`InteractionEffectPlayer`, `GameplayHapticsFeedback`):**
   - `Wedge.prefab`, `Brake.prefab`, `Exit.prefab`, and `Hazard.prefab` have empty `InteractionEffectPlayer` configs (`effectPrefabs: []`, `defaultCameraShake: 0`, `rules: []`). `Cracked.prefab` and `Coin.prefab` have `defaultCameraShake: 0`.
   - `GameplayHapticsFeedback` ignores `VelocityTier` scaling on walls and has no events for `CrackedBlock` breaks, `CloseCall`, or `LevelCompleted`.
6. **Runtime Procedural Objects in `PowerUpController.cs`:**
   - `SpawnRadialPulseVFX`, `SpawnDirectionalScanOrBurstVFX` (lines `1448–1503`), and the Echo fallback (`928–952`) instantiate `new GameObject` + `AddComponent<SpriteRenderer>()` at runtime rather than spawning pooled prefabs via `VfxSpawner.Spawn`.

### Pillar 1: Kinetic Runway Ramp & Pre-Turn Buffer Signalling
> *"Momentum you can hear accelerating cell-by-cell, track in peripheral framing, and see locked into the corner buffer."*

#### Why It Makes Gravita Uniquely Addictive
At `30 u/s` (`33.33 ms/tile`), straight corridors end almost immediately. Without rising sensory tension during transit, `Drift`, `Cruise`, and `KineticRam` only feel different *after* you crash. Pairing harmonic tile ticks, forward camera lead/breathing, neon ghost silhouettes in `KineticRam`, and an unmistakable queued-turn chevron turns travel time into active anticipation and gives players tactile confidence in `bufferTurnsUntilStop: 1` (`turnClearance: 0.35`).

### Pillar 2: Stop-to-Stop Impact Choreography & Surface Weight Differentiation
> *"Solid walls arrest momentum, `BrakeTile` absorbs it, `GravityBlock` clamps it, and `CrackedBlock` shatters beneath unbroken speed."*

#### Why It Makes Gravita Uniquely Addictive
Gravita's core rhythm is **slide $\rightarrow$ terminate $\rightarrow$ re-aim**. Right now, bottom-centered pivot squash drifts off North walls, DOTween freezes during hit-stop, and different stop mechanics feel nearly identical. Tuning each surface's impact envelope gives puzzle pieces true physical contrast.

#### Exact Numbers, Curves & Inspector Parameters
| Surface / Event | Hit-Stop (`unscaled`) | Shake Force | Squash & Sprite Choreography (`SetUpdate(true)`) | Audio & Haptics |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Solid Wall (`Drift`)** | `0 ms` | `0.025` | `0.45x` scaled amplitude from `PlayerSquashStretch` (`0.10 s OutQuad`), zero flash | Soft thud (`pitch 0.92`); `WallImpact` (`LightImpact`) |
| **Solid Wall (`Cruise`)** | `33 ms` (`0.033`) | `0.10` | Current `impactWidthPx: 28 / impactHeightPx: 16`, `squashDuration: 0.14 s OutBack`, `22 ms` `#FFFFFF` material flash | Sharp metallic clamp (`pitch 1.00`); `WallImpact` (`MediumImpact`) |
| **Solid Wall (`KineticRam`)** | `75 ms` (`0.075`) | `0.22` | Full `maxDeformation: 0.30` clamp (`0.16 s OutBack`) + `45 ms` `#FF52C7` flash + `-0.16 u` ortho slam pulse (`70 ms`) | Deep sub-bass slam + metallic ring (`pitch 0.85`); `WallImpact` (`RigidImpact`) |
| **`BrakeTile` Capture** | `0 ms` | `0.015` | Gentle longitudinal cushion (`0.5x` deformation, `0.15 s OutCubic`, no bounceback) | Descending magnetic scrub `1.15 → 0.65` (`0.12 s`); `GravityAnchor` |
| **`GravityBlock` Lock** | `18 ms` (`0.018`) | `0.06` (`0.08` Ram) | Symmetric inward snap `scale 0.86 → 1.0` (`0.09 s InOutQuad`) + lock-ring prefab | Precision latch click; `GravityAnchor` |
| **`CrackedBlock` Breakthrough** | `45 ms` (`0.045`) | `0.18` | **Continuous travel!** `stretchAmount * 1.6x` forward surge (`0.09 s OutQuad`) + ceramic/neon shard burst | Shatter crunch (`pitch 1.08`, vol `0.85`); new `HapticEvent.CrackedBreak` |

#### C# Integration Hooks
- **Fix `PlayerSquashStretch.cs` Geometry & Time-Scale:**
  - Add `.SetUpdate(true)` and `.SetLink(gameObject)` after `.DOKill()` on both `Play()` and `ResetScale()` so impact deformation executes *inside* the `33–75 ms` hit-stop window instead of after it.
  - **Fix Directional Pivot Gap:** Keep `Sprite` at `localPosition (0, -0.5, 0)` (pivot `(0.5, 0)`) untouched at rest, but during squash apply a `localPosition` compensation offset toward `impactDirection`:
    - South (`-Y`): bottom is already pinned (`wallPressPx` presses slightly downward).
    - North (`+Y`): shift `localPosition.y` upward by `spriteHeight * (1f - scaleY)` so the squashed top stays flush against the North wall instead of hovering `~0.30 u` below it.
    - East/West (`±X`): shift `localPosition.x` toward `impactDirection.x` by `0.5f * spriteWidth * (1f - scaleX)` to eliminate the half-gap on side walls.
  - Tighten `squashDuration` from `0.22 s` to `0.14 s` and `stretchDuration` from `0.18 s` to `0.12 s` so back-to-back moves at `30 u/s` remain crisp.
- **Prevent Runtime Material Leaks on `HitFlashSprite.shader`:**
  - Never mutate `.material` (`new Material` leak) or `_FlashColor` directly on `Hit Flash White.mat` (`sharedMaterial` dirtying). Instead, swap `SpriteRenderer.sharedMaterial` cleanly between pre-assigned `[SerializeField] Material` assets (`defaultSpriteMat`, `hitFlashWhiteMat`, `hitFlashRamMat`) for `22–45 ms` unscaled, OR add `[PerRendererData] _FlashAmount` to `HitFlashSprite.shader` and drive it via `MaterialPropertyBlock`.
- **Single-Owner `HitStopCoordinator` (`Assets/Scripts/Feedback/HitStopCoordinator.cs`):**
  - Replace local `Time.timeScale` capture in `PlayerImpactFeedback` with `RequestHitStop(float unscaledDuration)` and `RequestTimeDilation(float scale, float unscaledDuration)` tracked via `Time.unscaledTime` deadlines (`hitStopUntil`).
  - Track `bool isOwningTimeScale` and gate strictly on `LevelFlowController.State == LevelState.Playing`; if an external system (such as pause menu) holds `Time.timeScale == 0f` before a request, ignore requests and never restore `1f` on pause resume.
- **Stop Discrimination (`WasLastStopWallImpact`):**
  - Route `PlayerImpactFeedback` stop reactions through `PlayerMovement.WasLastStopWallImpact`: wall slam/hit-stop fires only on wall stops (`true`), while `BrakeTile` (`MazeTile.OnAnyPlayerInteracted`) and `GravityBlock.OnPlayerAnchored` execute their distinct capture/clamp signatures cleanly without wall slam false-positives.
  - Extend `HapticEvent` enum (`0..6` currently ends at `Death`) with `CrackedBreak` and map tier-scaled `WallImpact` presets (`LightImpact` / `MediumImpact` / `RigidImpact`) in `MostHapticsBackend`.

#### Unity Inspector & Prefab Wiring
- Wire `Brake.prefab` (`InteractionEffectPlayer`: cyan friction arc VFX, shake `0.015`), raise `Cracked.prefab` shake (`0` $\rightarrow$ `0.18`), and tune `GravityBlock.prefab` shake (`0.04` $\rightarrow$ `0.06` + clamp ring prefab).
- Assign `Hit Flash White.mat` (and optional `KineticRam` pink flash variant asset) via `[SerializeField]` slots—zero runtime shader lookups.

### Pillar 3: Pinball Rebound & Bank-Shot Chain Engine
> *"Turn `DeflectionWedge` and `VectorPad` sequences into a rising pentatonic instrument with directional ricochet recoil."*

#### Why It Makes Gravita Uniquely Addictive
Chained `DeflectionWedge` and `VectorPad` solutions (`maxConsecutiveRedirects: 8`) are Gravita's brightest puzzle dopamine spike—yet `Wedge.prefab` has zero effects, `redirectShake` is `0`, and `PlayerMovement.Redirect()` triggers `OnMoveStarted`, wiping naive launch trackers. Making every corner snap tighter and sing one step higher turns multi-bank shots into a signature hook.

#### Exact Numbers, Curves & Inspector Parameters
| Parameter | Target | Exact Value / Formula |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pentatonic Pitch Ladder (`chain 1..8`)** | `RedirectChainFeedback` | Semitones `[0, 2, 4, 7, 9, 12, 14, 16]` $\rightarrow$ `Mathf.Pow(2f, s / 12f)` (`1.00, 1.122, 1.260, 1.498, 1.682, 2.000, 2.245, 2.520`) |
| **Escalating Ricochet Shake** | `RedirectChainFeedback` | `0.05 + 0.015f * Mathf.Min(chainCount, 6)` (max `0.14`) |
| **Corner-Snap Stretch** | `PlayerSquashStretch` | `0.85` perpendicular / `1.18` along new `outgoingDir` for `0.08 s OutQuad` |
| **Wedge / Pad Tile Recoil** | `TileImpactRecoil` | Visual child only: `DOPunchScale(Vector3.one * 0.16f, 0.11 s, 1, 0.4f)` |
| **Bisector Corner Spark** | `RedirectCornerSpark.prefab` | Spawned via `VfxSpawner.Spawn`, oriented along `(-incomingDir + outgoingDir).normalized` |
| **Trail Elbow Flare** | `PlayerLineTrail` | Brief `widthMultiplier` spike `1.0 → 1.32` over `0.09 s` on `OnRedirected` |
| **Blunt-Side Rejection** | `PlayerImpactFeedback` | Flat-face wedge stop (`OnWallImpact`): muffled `pitch 0.72` clack + sprite `DOShakePosition(0.08 s, 0.045 u, 20)` |

#### C# Integration Hooks
- **Non-Breaking `SoundManager` Pitch Overload (`Assets/Scripts/Audio/SoundManager.cs`):**
  Add alongside existing `PlaySFX(SoundEntry)`:
  ```csharp
  public void PlaySFX(SoundEntry entry, float pitchOverride, float volumeMultiplier = 1f)
  ```
  When `pitchOverride > 0f`, bypass random pitch jitter so pentatonic chains hit clean musical intervals.
- **Chain Tracker (`Assets/Scripts/Feedback/RedirectChainFeedback.cs`):**
  - Increment `chainCount` on `PlayerMovement.OnRedirected(Vector2 newDir)` (plus a `Time.frameCount` / single-source guard so `VectorPad` + `OnRedirected` never double-count the same tile in one frame).
  - **Reset Rule:** Reset `chainCount = 0` **exclusively** on `PlayerMovement.OnMoveStopped`, `LevelFlowController.OnLevelFailed`, and `OnLevelRestarted`—never inside `OnMoveStarted`.
  - Compute bisector rotation from cached `previousDir` and `newDir` before updating `previousDir = newDir`.
- **Visual-Only Tile Recoil (`Assets/Scripts/Feedback/TileImpactRecoil.cs`):**
  Attach to `Wedge.prefab` and `VectorPad.prefab`, driving only `[SerializeField] Transform visualChild` (never collider root) on `IPlayerInteractable.OnPlayerInteracted` with `.DOKill().SetUpdate(true).SetLink(gameObject)`.

#### Unity Inspector & Prefab Wiring
- Author pooled `RedirectCornerSpark.prefab` and assign to `Wedge.prefab` / `RedirectChainFeedback`.
- Keep `PlayerImpactFeedback.redirectShake` at `0` so `RedirectChainFeedback` acts as the single authoritative shake owner per chain step (no double impulse).
