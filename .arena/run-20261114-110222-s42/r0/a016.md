# Gravita — Kinetic Lab Game Feel Upgrade Blueprint

> **Codebase Calibration Note (Current Truth vs. Design Targets)**
> 1. **Speed & Velocity Tiers**: In `Assets/Scenes/GameScene.unity`, `PlayerMovement.moveSpeed` is `30` units/sec (`cellSize = 1`), meaning Subject G-0 traverses **1 tile every `33.3 ms`**. In `VelocityTier.cs`, `VelocityTierUtil.FromTiles(int tilesTraveled)` currently maps `1–2` tiles → `Drift`, `3–4` → `Cruise`, and `5+` → `KineticRam` (reaching max tier in **`167 ms`**), whereas `docs/gdd.md` targets `1–3 / 4–7 / 8+`. Every timing below is calibrated to the live `33.3 ms/tile` (`30 u/s`) physics loop and adapts automatically if you adjust `FromTiles`.
> 2. **Close-Call Economy Flag**: `EconomyBalanceConfig.asset` sets `enableLegacyExtraRewardSystems: 0`, so `CloseCallDetector`'s `bonusCoins: 5` currently suppresses coin payouts. Near-misses therefore *must* pay off through immediate audiovisual and kinetic surge rather than silent score increments.

---

## 1. Core Game Feel Diagnosis: Why Gravita Currently Feels "Flat"

Gravita's deterministic stop-to-stop physics, tiered velocity model, squash/stretch, trails, shake, and hit-stop are already cleanly separated—but five concrete leaks cancel out the weight and speed of the game in live play:

1. **Hit-Stop Freezes `PlayerSquashStretch` & `CinemachineCameraZoom2D` at the Moment of Impact**
   - `PlayerImpactFeedback.PulseHitStop` pauses the game with `Time.timeScale = 0f` for `33 ms` (`Cruise`) and `83 ms` (`KineticRam`).
   - `PlayerSquashStretch.Play()` and `ResetScale()` do **not** call `.SetUpdate(true)` or `.DOKill()`, so DOTween freezes the squash tween during hit-stop, and rapid transitions (`HandleTierChanged` vs. `OnWallImpact`) overwrite each other mid-tween.
   - `PlayerLineTrail.LateUpdate()` returns early when `Time.deltaTime <= 0f`, and `CinemachineCameraZoom2D` relies on scaled `Time.deltaTime` (and currently has **zero callers**).
2. **Bottom-Center Sprite Pivot Distortion (`y = -0.5`)**
   - Subject G-0's `Sprite` child sits at `localPosition (0, -0.5, 0)` (bottom-center pivot `(0.5, 0)`). Horizontal and downward wall hits squash cleanly into the floor, but **upward ceiling slams** squash *away* from the top wall into empty space, visually disconnecting impact from the wall face.
3. **Omnidirectional Shake & Dead Energy Zones**
   - `CinemachineCameraShake2D.ShakeCamera(float force)` calls `impulseSource.GenerateImpulse(force)` with a fixed `DefaultVelocity = (0, -1, 0)` instead of `GenerateImpulseWithVelocity`, so smashing left, rebounding 90° off a wedge, and dropping down all jolt the camera downward.
   - `PlayerImpactFeedback` has `tier1Shake: 0`, `redirectShake: 0`, `impactAnimationPrefab: null`, and `blockBreakAnimationPrefab: null`.
   - `PlayerMovement.HaltAt` stops (`BrakeTile`, `GravityBlock`) do **not** fire `OnWallImpact`, `CrackedBlock.OnAnyBroken` triggers zero hit-stop/camera punch mid-dash, and `Brake.prefab`, `Wedge.prefab`, `Exit.prefab`, and `Hazard.prefab` all have empty `InteractionEffectPlayer`s (`effectPrefabs: []`, `defaultCameraShake: 0`, `rules: []`).
4. **167ms "Runway Blindness" & Event Collisions**
   - At `30 u/s`, Subject G-0 crosses `5` tiles in `0.167 s` without a single per-tile audio tick, camera breath, or speed afterimage—and `PlayerLineTrail` clamps `instSpeed` to `[4f, 28f]` despite `moveSpeed = 30`.
   - `PlayerMovement.Redirect()` invokes **both** `OnRedirected` and `OnMoveStarted`, causing launch squash/audio observers to double-fire on wedge bounces as if starting from standstill.
5. **Mute World & Unwired Signature Systems**
   - `CloseCallDetector.OnCloseCallTriggered` has **0 subscribers**.
   - `Global Volume Profile.asset` already holds `Bloom` (`threshold: 0.9, intensity: 9`), `Vignette` (`0.4`), `ChromaticAberration` (`0.2`), and `ColorAdjustments`, yet sits static at runtime.
   - `EntityAudio` ignores `OnTileCrossed`, `OnVelocityTierChanged`, `OnPhasingChanged`, `BrakeTile`/`GravityBlock`, `CircuitSwitch`, `EnergyGate`, `BrittleFloor`, `FailingTile`, and `HazardActivation`.

## 2. Six High-Impact Game Feel Pillars

All designs below strictly obey the 4-layer architecture, avoid runtime `new GameObject(...)` / `AddComponent<...>()` (unlike the legacy `PowerUpController` pattern), preserve deterministic `SimulateStep` grid physics, and keep all DOTween calls safe across hit-stop via `.DOKill()`, `.SetUpdate(true)`, and `.SetLink(gameObject)`.

### Pillar 1: Kinetic Runway Engine ("33ms-per-Tile Escalation & Dynamic Lens")

#### Why It Makes Gravita Uniquely Addictive
Because slides finish in fractions of a second (`167 ms` to hit `KineticRam`), static trail color swaps aren't enough for the player's nervous system to *feel* acceleration. Turning every crossed cell into an ascending harmonic synth tick paired with subtle unscaled camera zoom-out and `KineticRam` afterimages turns long straightaways into an accelerating slingshot—making players actively hunt longer lanes just to charge up a Tier 3 smash.

#### Exact Numbers, Curves & Inspector Parameters
| Parameter | Target / Value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Tile Tick Audio Pitch (`OnTileCrossed`) | Base `0.94f`, `+0.045f` per tile, clamped `1.44f` (~11 tiles) | Volume ramps `0.16f` (`Drift`) → `0.26f` (`Cruise`) → `0.36f` (`KineticRam`) |
| Camera Ortho Pull (`CinemachineCameraZoom2D`) | `Drift`: `5.50` · `Cruise`: `5.72` (`+0.22`) · `KineticRam`: `6.02` (`+0.52`) | Unscaled lerp `zoomSpeed = 9f` (`OutCubic`); never freezes during hit-stop |
| Impact Lens Squeeze | Dip `-0.18` (`5.32`) for `45 ms` (`OutQuad`) → return to `5.50` over `140 ms` (`OutSine`) | Fires on `Cruise` / `KineticRam` stops and `CrackedBlock` breaks |
| Afterimage Ghost Emission | Active in `KineticRam` or `Phasing`; spawn every `0.040 s` unscaled (`~1.2` tiles) | Max `5` active; fade alpha `0.42 → 0` over `0.15 s` (`OutQuad`), scaled `1.0 → 0.85` |
| `PlayerLineTrail` Speed Clamp | Raise `[4f, 28f]` ceiling in `PlayerLineTrail.LateUpdate()` to `[4f, 36f]` | Prevents width/length saturation below `moveSpeed = 30` |

#### Exact C# Integration Hooks
- **`SoundManager.cs`**: Alongside `PlaySFX(SoundEntry)` and `PlaySFX(AudioClip, float volume = 1f, bool randomPitch = true)`, add an explicit pitch-override overload (`PlaySFXWithPitch(SoundEntry entry, float pitch, float volumeScale = 1f)`) that clamps pitch (e.g., `0.5f–2.0f`) on its playback `AudioSource` cleanly without mutating the `SoundConfig` asset or falling back to random pitch.
- **`CinemachineCameraZoom2D.cs`**: Replace `Time.deltaTime` with `Time.unscaledDeltaTime`, expose `PulseZoom(float dipSize, float returnSize, float inDuration, float outDuration)` driven by a DOTween `Sequence` on a serialized/target ortho field with `.DOKill().SetUpdate(true).SetLink(gameObject)`.
- **`KineticRunwayFeedback.cs` (`Assets/Scripts/Feedback/`)**: Subscribe to `PlayerMovement.OnTileCrossed`, `OnVelocityTierChanged`, `OnPhasingChanged`, `OnMoveStopped`, and `OnWallImpact`. Spawn pooled `KineticAfterimage.prefab` via `ObjectPoolManager.SpawnObject(afterimagePrefab, visualTransform.position, visualTransform.rotation)`, tint using `PlayerSquashStretch`'s tier/phase color, and fade its `SpriteRenderer`:
  ```csharp
  // Inside KineticAfterimage : MonoBehaviour (on pooled prefab, returned via PooledObject)
  public void Play(Sprite sprite, Color tint, Vector3 scale, float fadeDuration)
  {
      spriteRenderer.sprite = sprite;
      spriteRenderer.color = tint;
      transform.localScale = scale;

      spriteRenderer.DOKill();
      transform.DOKill();
      DOTween.Sequence()
          .Join(spriteRenderer.DOFade(0f, fadeDuration).SetEase(Ease.OutQuad))
          .Join(transform.DOScale(scale * 0.85f, fadeDuration).SetEase(Ease.OutQuad))
          .SetUpdate(true)
          .SetLink(gameObject)
          .OnComplete(ReleaseToPool);
  }
  ```

#### Unity Inspector / Prefab Wiring
- Create `KineticAfterimage.prefab` with `PooledObject` + `SpriteRenderer` (additive or default sprite shader, sorting order 1 behind Player `Sprite`), assign it to `KineticRunwayFeedback.afterimagePrefab`.
- Add `tileTick`, `tierUpCruise`, and `tierUpKinetic` `SoundEntry` fields to `SoundConfig.cs`.
- No procedural GameObjects; `Player` simply carries `[SerializeField] private KineticRunwayFeedback` references.

### Pillar 2: Directional Impact Physics, Hit-Stop Unfreeze & Wall-Face Compression

#### Why It Makes Gravita Uniquely Addictive
In a stop-to-stop puzzle game, impact *is* the punctuation mark of every move. Fixing the hit-stop freeze bug so Subject G-0 visibly compresses **through** the pause and pops outward on resume, aiming camera impulses along the collision vector, and smashing through `CrackedBlock`s with a crisp micro-hitch makes every wall collision feel tactile and weighty.

#### Exact Numbers, Curves & Inspector Parameters
| Parameter | Current (`GameScene.unity`) | Recommended Target | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `PlayerImpactFeedback.tier1Shake` | `0` | `0.045` | Drift still taps cleanly instead of feeling dead |
| `PlayerImpactFeedback.tier2Shake` / `tier3Shake` | `0.1` / `0.2` | `0.13` / `0.28` | Stronger contrast between Cruise and KineticRam |
| `PlayerImpactFeedback.redirectShake` | `0` | `0.08` | Gives wedge redirects a pinball kick |
| `PlayerImpactFeedback.tier1HitStop` / `tier2` / `tier3` | `0` / `0.033` / `0.083` | `0` / `0.033` / `0.070` | Preserves instant 1-tile taps while keeping `KineticRam` snappy |
| `CrackedBlock` Smash Hit-Stop / Impulse | `0` / `0` | `0.040 s` / `0.22` | Breakthrough feels like shattering armor, not clipping air |
| Directional Impact Squash (`Cruise` / `KineticRam`) | `28x16 px`, `0.22 s` | `30x14 px` (scaled by `maxDeformation = 0.3`), `0.18 s` `OutBack` | Faster rebound after hit-stop ends |

#### Exact C# Integration Hooks
1. **Directional Camera Impulse (`CinemachineCameraShake2D.cs`)**:
   ```csharp
   // [NEW] Directional overload preserving existing parameterless/scalar API
   public void ShakeCamera(Vector2 direction, float force)
   {
       if (impulseSource == null || force <= 0f) return;
       Vector3 velocity = direction.sqrMagnitude > 0.001f
           ? new Vector3(direction.x, direction.y, 0f).normalized * force
           : Vector3.down * force;
       impulseSource.GenerateImpulseWithVelocity(velocity);
   }
   ```
2. **Unfrozen Squash/Stretch + Wall-Face Pivot Compensation (`PlayerSquashStretch.cs`)**:
   - Guard `OnMoveStarted` so if `Time.frameCount == lastRedirectFrame`, skip the launch stretch and let `OnRedirected` own the motion.
   - Fix `Play()` and `ResetScale()` to **always** call `.DOKill()`, `.SetUpdate(true)`, and `.SetLink(gameObject)`:
   ```csharp
   // Inside PlayerSquashStretch.Play(...)
   visualTransform.DOKill();
   DOTween.Sequence()
       .Join(visualTransform.DOScale(targetScale, duration * 0.35f).SetEase(Ease.OutQuad))
       .Join(visualTransform.DOLocalMove(pressOffset, duration * 0.35f).SetEase(Ease.OutQuad))
       .Append(visualTransform.DOScale(Vector3.one, duration * 0.65f).SetEase(returnEase))
       .Join(visualTransform.DOLocalMove(baseLocalPos, duration * 0.65f).SetEase(returnEase))
       .SetUpdate(true)
       .SetLink(gameObject);
   ```
   - **Wall-Face Offset Fix for `Sprite (0, -0.5, 0)`**: When `OnWallImpact(Vector2 normal)` fires on an **upward impact** (`normal.y < -0.5f`), offset `pressOffset.y` upward by `+(1f - targetScale.y) + wallPressWorld` so the sprite compresses *against the ceiling* rather than shrinking away toward its bottom pivot. On horizontal impacts (`Mathf.Abs(normal.x) > 0.5f`), push `pressOffset.x` by `-normal.x * wallPressWorld` (`4 px` in pixels-per-unit).
   - **Magnetic Lock (`BrakeTile` / `GravityBlock`)**: On `OnMoveStopped` where `!playerMovement.WasLastStopWallImpact`, play a subtle vertical settle (`scale (1.08, 0.90, 1)`, `0.12 s`, `OutBack`) with `.DOKill().SetUpdate(true).SetLink(gameObject)`.
3. **2D Particle Rotation & Breakthrough Smash (`PlayerImpactFeedback.cs`)**:
   - Replace `Quaternion.LookRotation((Vector3)normal)` (3D rotation on a 2D vector) with:
     ```csharp
     float angle = Mathf.Atan2(normal.y, normal.x) * Mathf.Rad2Deg;
     Quaternion rotation = Quaternion.Euler(0f, 0f, angle);
     ```
   - Subscribe `PlayerImpactFeedback` to `CrackedBlock.OnAnyBroken`: trigger `PulseHitStop(0.04f)` and `cameraShake.ShakeCamera(playerMovement.CurrentDirection, 0.22f)` via the component's `CinemachineCameraShake2D` reference.

#### Unity Inspector / Prefab Wiring
- Update serialized values on `Player` (`PlayerImpactFeedback`: `tier1Shake = 0.045`, `tier2Shake = 0.13`, `tier3Shake = 0.28`, `redirectShake = 0.08`, `tier3HitStop = 0.07`).
- Assign pooled particle/sprite-burst prefabs to `impactAnimationPrefab` and `blockBreakAnimationPrefab` (already present as serialized fields on `PlayerImpactFeedback`).

### Pillar 3: Billiard Corner-Whip & Buffered-Turn "Input Coil"

#### Why It Makes Gravita Uniquely Addictive
Gravita's signature puzzle rhythm combines `bufferTurnsUntilStop = true` with chained `DeflectionWedge` rebounds and `VectorLauncher` phase dashes. At `30 u/s`, players swipe *before* Subject G-0 reaches a stop or corner—if there's zero feedback that the turn is armed, swipes feel unresponsive or dropped; and hitting a wedge currently re-fires `OnMoveStarted`, wiping out chain momentum. Adding an "Input Coil" confirmation chevron and chaining wedge deflection pitch/sparks turns routing through mazes into high-speed kinetic pinball.

#### Exact Numbers, Curves & Inspector Parameters
| Feature | Parameters & Curves |
|---|---|
| **Input Coil Chevron** | Child `SpriteRenderer` on `Player`, offset `0.58 u` toward buffered turn; scales `0.35 → 1.0` in `0.065 s` (`OutBack`); opacity pulses `0.65 ↔ 1.0` at `10 Hz`; audio click `vol = 0.20, pitch = 1.18` |
| **Wedge Deflection Chain (`OnRedirected`)** | Bisector spark angled along `(-prevDir + newDir).normalized`; pitch escalates `1.00f + 0.07f * redirectChain` (up to `maxConsecutiveRedirects = 8` → `1.56f`); directional camera nudge `0.08` along `newDir` |
| **Corner-Whip Tween** | Rotate visual child toward travel axis `±10°` on perpendicular turn and snap `0°` over `0.10 s` (`OutExpo`, `.DOKill().SetUpdate(true).SetLink(gameObject)`) |
| **Phase Launch (`OnPhasingChanged`)** | Pulse `phaseSpriteTint` alpha `0.45 ↔ 0.85` (`12 Hz`); spawn pooled phase ring every `0.07 s`; on exit (`false`), trigger `-0.12` camera zoom pop + soft pop SFX |

#### Exact C# Integration Hooks
- **`PlayerMovement.cs` (Read-Only Surface Addition, Zero Physics Changes)**:
  Expose a read-only `PendingDirection` (`Vector2`) property and an `OnPendingDirectionChanged` (`Action<Vector2>`) event alongside `HasPendingDirection` wherever buffered turn input is armed or cleared. `FixedUpdate` / `SimulateStep` remains completely untouched.
- **`InputCoilIndicator.cs` (`Assets/Scripts/Feedback/`)**:
  Subscribes to `PlayerMovement.OnPendingDirectionChanged` and `OnMoveStopped`. Uses a **pre-wired child `[SerializeField] private Transform chevronPivot;` and `[SerializeField] private SpriteRenderer chevronRenderer;`** (never `new GameObject`). When `dir != Vector2.zero`, aligns rotation (`Atan2`), slides out to `dir * 0.58f`, plays DOTween pop with `.DOKill().SetUpdate(true).SetLink(gameObject)`, and cancels cleanly when cleared.
- **Deflection Bisector Feedback**:
  Cache `prevDirection` before `OnRedirected(Vector2 newDir)` overwrites it; pass `(-prevDirection + newDir).normalized` to `VfxSpawner.Spawn(wedgeSparkPrefab, transform.position, bisectorRotation)` and play `SoundConfig.wedgeRedirect` with `SoundManager.PlaySFXWithPitch(wedgeRedirect, 1f + 0.07f * chainCount)`.

#### Unity Inspector / Prefab Wiring
- In the `Player` prefab hierarchy, add a single inactive-by-default child (`InputCoilChevron` with `SpriteRenderer`) wired directly into `InputCoilIndicator` in the Inspector.
- Assign pooled spark/ring prefabs to `Wedge.prefab` and `VectorPad.prefab` `InteractionEffectPlayer` (`effectPrefabs` + `defaultCameraShake = 0.06`).

### Pillar 6: Tiered Haptics & Sub-Second Arcade Retry Loop

#### Why It Makes Gravita Uniquely Addictive
In precision kinetic puzzlers (*Super Meat Boy* / *Tomb of the Mask*), players tolerate high difficulty *only* when death-to-action feels instant and every collision carries distinct tactile weight in the hands. Right now, `GameplayHapticsFeedback` fires identical `LightImpact` for both a 1-tile `Drift` and a 6-tile `KineticRam`, and `CutsceneManager` adds `~1.6 s` of black-fade downtime on every hazard death (`deathFallbackDuration = 0.8f` + `endFadeDuration = 0.4f` fade-out and fade-in).

#### Exact Numbers, Curves & Inspector Parameters
- **Tiered Haptic Mapping (via `MOST_HapticFeedback.HapticTypes`)**:
  - `Drift` impact → `LightImpact` (`cooldown 0.04 s`)
  - `Cruise` impact → `MediumImpact` (`cooldown 0.05 s`)
  - `KineticRam` impact / `CrackedBlock` break → `RigidImpact` (`cooldown 0.06 s`)
  - `CloseCall` / `TierUp` → `Selection` / `SoftImpact` (`cooldown 0.12 s`)
  - `GravityAnchor` / `PadRedirect` → `SoftImpact` / `Selection`
  - `StarCollect` → `MediumImpact` (`0.08 s` cooldown)
  - `Death` → `Failure`
- **Snappy Arcade Retry Timings (`CutsceneManager.cs`)**:
  - Reduce `deathFallbackDuration`: `0.8f` → **`0.28f`** (time to register the electric death burst + failure chord).
  - Reduce `endFadeDuration`: `0.4f` → **`0.16f`** (each way = `0.32 s` wipe total).
  - Total death-to-ready loop drops from **`~1.60 s` → `~0.60 s`** (feeling `2.6x` snappier), paired with a `0.18 s` `OutBack` respawn pop on `LevelFlowController.OnPlayerRevived` (`.DOKill().SetUpdate(true).SetLink(gameObject)`).

#### Exact C# Integration Hooks
