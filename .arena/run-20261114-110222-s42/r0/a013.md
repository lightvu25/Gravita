# Gravita — Game Feel Signature Upgrade Blueprint

## Assumptions & Verified Baseline
- **Velocity Tier Thresholds**: `Assets/Scripts/Gameplay/VelocityTier.cs` defines `Drift = 1–2`, `Cruise = 3–4`, and `KineticRam = 5+` tiles (whereas the high-level brief mentions `1–3 / 4–7 / 8+`). Every feedback hook below subscribes to `PlayerMovement.CurrentTier`, `PlayerMovement.LastImpactTier`, and `PlayerMovement.OnVelocityTierChanged` so thresholds remain decoupled if you tune `VelocityTierUtil.FromTiles` later.
- **Actual Scene Speed (`moveSpeed = 30`)**: `GameScene.unity` overrides `PlayerMovement.moveSpeed` to `30` units/sec (`1 tile = 33.3 ms`). At 60 FPS (`16.7 ms/frame`), a 2-tile `Drift` takes `66.7 ms` (4 frames), `Cruise` starts at `100 ms`, and `KineticRam` hits at `166.7 ms`. Tuning below is calibrated specifically for `30` units/sec (with high confidence it also holds if `moveSpeed` sits in `24–36`).
- **Architecture & Scene Rules**: All additions live as Layer 4 observers in `Assets/Scripts/Feedback/` and `Assets/Scripts/Audio/`. Zero runtime `new GameObject(...)` or `AddComponent<...>()`, zero changes to `PlayerMovement.FixedUpdate` / `SimulateStep`, and all DOTween calls enforce `.DOKill()` + `.SetUpdate(true)` + `.SetLink(gameObject)`. *(Note: avoid replicating the runtime `new GameObject` helper pattern currently inside `PowerUpController.SpawnRadialPulseVFX`; route new pooled bursts through `VfxSpawner.Spawn` / `ObjectPoolManager`.)*

## 1. Core Game Feel Diagnosis: Why Gravita Currently Feels "Flat"

Gravita already has impact shake, squash/stretch, trails, audio, and haptics—yet five concrete technical mismatches in the current scene and scripts cancel each other out during live play:

1. **Hit-Stop Deadlocks Camera & Squash/Stretch Together**
   - `PlayerImpactFeedback.HitStopRoutine` drops `Time.timeScale = 0f` for `0.033s` (`Cruise`) and `0.083s` (`KineticRam`).
   - Meanwhile, in `GameScene.unity`, `CinemachineBrain` has `IgnoreTimeScale: 0`, `CinemachineImpulseManager.Instance.IgnoreTimeScale` defaults to `false`, `CinemachineCameraZoom2D.Update` uses `Time.deltaTime`, and `PlayerSquashStretch` omits `.SetUpdate(true)` on both DOTween `Sequence` calls.
   - **Result**: Instead of feeling like a snappy freeze-frame impact, *everything*—camera shake, squash recoil, and zoom—freezes silently for 83 ms and only begins moving *after* time resumes.
2. **Durations Calibrated for `10 u/s` Instead of `30 u/s`**
   - `PlayerSquashStretch` uses `stretchDuration = 0.18s` (180 ms) and `squashDuration = 0.22s` (220 ms) in `GameScene.unity`. On typical 2–4 tile dashes (`67–133 ms`), the launch stretch is truncated mid-tween by the wall squash, muting launch snap.
   - `PlayerLineTrail.LateUpdate` clamps `smoothedMoveSpeed` to `[4f, 28f]` despite `moveSpeed = 30f` (and `48f` during `1.6x` Phase Launch), capping trail length responsiveness.
3. **Downward-Only Shake & Misaligned 2D Particle Rotation**
   - In `GameScene.unity`, `CinemachineImpulseSource` has `DefaultVelocity: (0, -1, 0)`. Calling `CinemachineCameraShake2D.ShakeCamera(float force)` kicks the camera **straight down** even on left/right wall collisions, and `tier1Shake = 0` / `redirectShake = 0` leave short hops and deflections weightless.
   - `PlayerImpactFeedback.HandleWallImpact` spawns `impactBurstPrefab` with `Quaternion.LookRotation((Vector3)normal)`, rotating `+Z` rather than aligning 2D XY particle emissions around the Z axis.
4. **Pivot-Induced Detachment on `PlayerSquashStretch`**
   - `Sprite` (`visualTransform`) sits at `localPosition = (0, -0.5, 0)` in `GameScene.unity` because Subject G-0 uses a bottom-center `(0.5, 0)` sprite pivot. Scaling `visualTransform` during `Squash(Vector2.up)` compresses *downward away from the top wall* even as `wallPressPx` shifts upward, creating visual float rather than flush wall compression.
5. **Silent High-Skill & Rhythm Channels**
   - `PlayerMovement.OnTileCrossed` has **0 subscribers** (no runway buildup sensation).
   - `CinemachineCameraZoom2D` is **never called anywhere**.
   - `CloseCallDetector.OnCloseCallTriggered` has **0 audio, time, zoom, or UI subscribers**.
   - `EntityAudio.HandlePlayerRedirected` plays one flat `wedgeRedirect` pitch regardless of rebound chain length, and `Brake`, `Exit`, `Hazard`, and `Wedge` `InteractionEffectPlayer` slots lack effects/shake.

### Pillar 1: Kinetic Runway Cadence & Tier-Break Surge
*Making acceleration across tiles audible, rhythmic, and visually unmistakable.*

- **Why It Makes Gravita Uniquely Addictive**
  Because `PlayerMovement` glides at constant `30 u/s`, the player's brain only perceives "kinetic buildup" when distance crossed produces an escalating sensory beat. A rising musical tick per tile crossed + a distinct physical "sonic boom / FOV breath" at `Cruise` (tile 3) and `KineticRam` (tile 5) turns long corridors and wedge chains into a slot-machine tension arc: *tick-tick-THWUMP-BOOM!* Players instinctively hunt longer runways just to trigger the Tier 3 state.

    - Play subtle lab-synth click at `0.025s` cooldown (`minInterval = 0.025f` so `33.3 ms`/tile never drops).
    - **Pitch Curve** (pentatonic ramp, capped at tile 10): `pitch = 0.90f + Mathf.Min(tiles - 1, 9) * 0.055f` (`0.90 -> 1.395`).
    - **Volume Ramp**: `0.18f` (`Drift`) -> `0.28f` (`Cruise`) -> `0.38f` (`KineticRam`).
  - **Tier-Break Surge** (`OnVelocityTierChanged(VelocityTier tier)`):

    - **`KineticRam` (Tile 5)**: Ortho pull-out `+0.35` (`5.5 -> 5.85`, `zoomSpeed = 10f` unscaled), sprite axial pulse `+20%` (`0.07s` unscaled), pooled `tier3ThresholdRingPrefab` spawned at player center (`VfxSpawner.Spawn`), `PlayerLineTrail` momentary width flare (`1.45x -> 1.70x -> 1.45x` over `0.12s`), power-chord chime (`pitch = 1.18f`), subtle `0.045` camera impulse along movement vector.
    - **Reset on Stop** (`OnMoveStopped`): `CinemachineCameraZoom2D.SetNormalOrthographicSize()` snaps smoothly back to `5.5` (`zoomSpeed = 12f` on stop vs `9f` pull-out—fast return feels punchier than slow pull-out).
  - **Probabilistic Confidence**: High confidence (`~90%`) that `+0.15 / +0.35` ortho delta reads cleanly on portrait `5.5` ortho without clipping walls; if wide single-screen rooms sit right at screen edge, clamp max pull-out in Inspector (`maxTierZoomOffset = 0.35f`, `maxOrthoSize = 6.0f`).

### Pillar 2: Unscaled Hit-Stop, Directional Wall Recoil & Pivot-True Crush
*Fixing the frozen hit-stop pipeline and giving every stop directional physical mass.*

- **Why It Makes Gravita Uniquely Addictive**
  Stop-to-stop games live and die on the *moment of collision*. When Subject G-0 slams a wall at 30 tiles/sec, the brain expects a violent compression *into* the wall face, a directional camera jolt *along the travel vector*, and an unscaled 50–83 ms impact freeze where sparks fly and the sprite stays squashed before rebounding. Currently, time-scale freezing stops the camera, stops the squash, and shakes downward regardless of direction.

- **Exact Numbers, Curves & Inspector Parameters**
  - **Unscaled Camera Pipeline**:
    - Toggle BOTH `CinemachineBrain.IgnoreTimeScale = true` (via `[SerializeField] private CinemachineBrain cinemachineBrain`) and `CinemachineImpulseManager.Instance.IgnoreTimeScale = true` strictly inside `PlayerImpactFeedback.HitStopRoutine` (restoring previous values on completion or in `OnDisable`), OR enable `IgnoreTimeScale = true` directly on `CinemachineBrain` in `GameScene.unity` and call `CinemachineImpulseManager.Instance.Clear()` when pausing. *(Note: `CinemachineBrain.GetEffectiveDeltaTime(false)` returns `Time.deltaTime == 0` whenever `IgnoreTimeScale == false`, so toggling `CinemachineImpulseManager` alone is insufficient without `CinemachineBrain.IgnoreTimeScale = true` during Hit-Stop.)*
    - *Pause Safety Note*: `PlayerImpactFeedback.PulseHitStop` already guards `if (!hitStopEnabled || Time.timeScale < 0.5f) return;`, and restoring `Time.timeScale` via `LevelFlowController` / `PauseMenuUI` check avoids overriding a Pause triggered mid-hit-stop.
  - **Directional Camera Impulse (`PlayerImpactFeedback` -> `CinemachineCameraShake2D`)**:
    - `Drift` (`1–2` tiles): `tier1Shake = 0.04f`, `tier1HitStop = 0f` (crisp tap, zero sluggishness).
    - `Cruise` (`3–4` tiles): `tier2Shake = 0.13f`, `tier2HitStop = 0.033f` (~2 frames @ 60 Hz).
    - `KineticRam` (`5+` tiles): `tier3Shake = 0.28f`, `tier3HitStop = 0.080f` (~5 frames).
    - `CrackedBlock` Smash (`CrackedBlock.OnAnyBroken`): `blockSmashShake = 0.36f`, `blockSmashHitStop = 0.075f` + `CinemachineCameraZoom2D` punch `-0.24` ortho for `0.08s` unscaled (feels like bursting through drywall!).
    - Direction vector: pass `incomingVelocityDir = -wallNormal` into `CinemachineCameraShake2D.ShakeCamera(Vector2 direction, float force)` calling `impulseSource.GenerateImpulse((Vector3)(direction.normalized * force))`.
  - **Calibrated, Pivot-True `PlayerSquashStretch` (`30 u/s` + `(0.5, 0)` Pivot)**:
    - `stretchDuration = 0.065s` (completes inside a 2-tile `66.7 ms` dash!).
    - `squashDuration = 0.135s` (`35%` compress in `0.045s` `OutQuad`, `65%` recover in `0.090s` `OutBack` or `OutQuad`).
    - Scale squash by `LastImpactTier`: `Drift` = `0.55x` squash, `Cruise` = `0.85x` squash, `KineticRam` = `1.20x` squash (`maxDeformation = 0.40f`).
    - **Bottom-Center Pivot Fix**: Because `Sprite` has pivot `(0.5, 0)` at `baseLocalPosition = (0, -0.5, 0)` (sprite height `1.0` unit), scaling `y` by `(1 + sy)` shifts its visual center by `0.5 * sy`. To pin the sprite face flush against the wall:
      - Hitting **Top Wall** (`pressDir == Vector2.up`): add `(0, -sy * 1.0f, 0)` so the top edge stays pinned to the ceiling while compressing upward!
      - Hitting **Bottom Wall** (`pressDir == Vector2.down`): keep `(0, 0, 0)` offset (bottom pivot is already on the floor!).
      - Hitting **Left / Right Wall** (`pressDir.x != 0`): add `(pressDir.x * (-sx * 0.5f), -sy * 0.5f, 0)` so the leading edge stays pinned to the vertical wall while keeping vertical center aligned!
  - **2D Particle Alignment Fix**:
    - Replace `Quaternion.LookRotation((Vector3)normal)` in `PlayerImpactFeedback.HandleWallImpact` with 2D Z-angle `Quaternion.Euler(0f, 0f, Mathf.Atan2(normal.y, normal.x) * Mathf.Rad2Deg)` (configurable toggle `use2DRotation = true` in Inspector so existing prefab orientation isn't broken if authored for 3D).

- **Exact C# Integration Hooks**
  - `CinemachineCameraShake2D.cs`:
    ```csharp
    public void ShakeCamera(Vector2 direction, float force)
    {
        if (impulseSource == null || force <= 0f) return;
        if (direction.sqrMagnitude < 0.0001f) { impulseSource.GenerateImpulse(force); return; }
        impulseSource.GenerateImpulse((Vector3)(direction.normalized * force));
    }
    ```
  - `PlayerImpactFeedback.cs`:
    - Check `movement.WasLastStopWallImpact` inside `HandleStopped()` so `BrakeTile`/`GravityBlock` stops don't trigger wall-slam hit-stop/shake (Pillar 5 owns floor anchor feel).
    - Set both `CinemachineBrain.IgnoreTimeScale = true` (via `[SerializeField]`) and `CinemachineImpulseManager.Instance.IgnoreTimeScale = true` during `HitStopRoutine` (restoring previous values on exit and in `OnDisable`).
    - Subscribe `CrackedBlock.OnAnyBroken` to fire directional crush shake + zoom punch (`-0.24` ortho).
  - `PlayerSquashStretch.cs`:
    - Add `.SetUpdate(true)` on both `DOTween.Sequence()` instances (`Play` and `ResetScale`), plus `.DOKill()` before creation.
    - Guard `HandleMoveStopped()` with `LastImpactTier` multiplier and apply the pivot compensation math above.
  - `GameplayHapticsFeedback.cs`:
    - Extend `HapticEvent` with `WallImpactHeavy` (double-pulse or stronger pattern on `KineticRam` via `PlayerMovement.LastImpactTier >= VelocityTier.KineticRam`) so Tier 3 slams feel physically different in the palm.

- **Unity Inspector / Prefab Wiring**
  - On `PlayerImpactFeedback` in `GameScene.unity`: set `tier1Shake = 0.04`, `tier2Shake = 0.13`, `tier3Shake = 0.28`, `redirectShake = 0.045`.
  - On `PlayerSquashStretch`: set `stretchDuration = 0.065`, `squashDuration = 0.135`, `maxDeformation = 0.4`.
  - On `CinemachineBrain` (Main Camera): either enable `IgnoreTimeScale = 1` in `GameScene.unity`, or assign `[SerializeField] private CinemachineBrain cinemachineBrain` on `PlayerImpactFeedback` so it enables `IgnoreTimeScale` strictly for the `33–80 ms` Hit-Stop window.

### Pillar 3: Billiard Bank-Shot Chain Resonance (`DeflectionWedge` & `VectorPad`)
*Turning multi-corner deflections into rhythmic combo dopamine.*

- **Why It Makes Gravita Uniquely Addictive**
  In standard sliding puzzles, walls stop you. In Gravita, `DeflectionWedge` and `VectorPad` chain 90-degree corners while *preserving* `TilesTraveled` toward `KineticRam`. Right now, chaining 3 wedges plays the same flat sound and `redirectShake = 0`. Adding a rising harmonic chord + snap-whip across chained rebounds rewards foresight and turns every bank-shot puzzle into an instrument players want to play fast.

- **Exact Numbers, Curves & Inspector Parameters**
  - **Chain Counter**: `redirectChainCount` increments on `PlayerMovement.OnRedirected(Vector2 outDir)` and resets to `0` on `PlayerMovement.OnMoveStopped` or `LevelFlowController.OnLevelRestarted`.
  - **Harmonic Pitch Escalation**: `pitch = 1.00f + Mathf.Min(redirectChainCount - 1, 6) * 0.08f` (`1.00, 1.08, 1.16, 1.24, 1.32, 1.40, 1.48`), volume `0.78 -> 0.95`.
  - **Corner-Whip Squash (`PlayerSquashStretch`)**:
    - On `OnRedirected(Vector2 newDir)`: fire a razor-sharp `40 ms` `.SetUpdate(true)` whip compressing perpendicular to `newDir` (`-14%`) and stretching along `newDir` (`+16%`), snapping back cleanly so the sprite visibly "carves" the 90° corner.
  - **Bisector Directional Shake**:
    - `force = redirectShakeBase (0.045f) + 0.015f * Mathf.Min(redirectChainCount, 4)` (max `0.105f`) pushed along `(lastDir - newDir).normalized` (the outward diagonal of the wedge bounce!).
  - **Micro Corner Flash**:
    - On `Wedge.prefab` & `VectorPad.prefab`, wire `InteractionEffectPlayer` rule `triggerOn = InteractionKind.Deflect`, `alignToDirection = true`, `cameraShake = 0` (handled by chain tracker so force scales with combo), spawning a crisp 3-particle diagonal spark (`0.15s` lifetime).

- **Exact C# Integration Hooks**
  - `PlayerSquashStretch`: subscribe to `movement.OnRedirected += HandleRedirected`:
    ```csharp
    private void HandleRedirected(Vector2 newDirection)
    {
        lastDirection = newDirection;
        Play(Deform(newDirection, +stretchAmount * 1.35f, -stretchAmount * 1.15f), 0.045f);
    }
    ```
  - `EntityAudio`: track `redirectChainCount` across `OnRedirected` / `OnMoveStopped`; call `SoundManager.Instance.PlaySFXWithPitch(cfg.wedgeRedirect, 1f + Mathf.Min(chain - 1, 6) * 0.08f)`.
  - `PlayerImpactFeedback`: track `previousMoveDirection` via `OnMoveStarted` / `OnRedirected`; on `OnRedirected(newDir)`, call `CinemachineCameraShake2D.Instance.ShakeCamera((prevDir - newDir).normalized, redirectShake + 0.015f * Mathf.Min(chain, 4))`.

- **Unity Inspector / Prefab Wiring**
  - Populate `Wedge.prefab` and `VectorPad.prefab` -> `InteractionEffectPlayer.rules` (`InteractionKind.Deflect`) with a pooled spark prefab (`WallImpactBurst` variant or new `WedgeSparkVFX`).
  - Also add an `InteractionKind.Hit` rule on `Wedge.prefab` for blunt-face hits (`DeflectionWedge.NotifyBlockedImpact`) so players feel the hard clank difference between hitting the mirror face vs the blunt back.

### Pillar 5: Weight Contrast — Magnetic Anchors, Phase Tunneling & Coin Melody
*Making stillness, wall-phasing, and collectible sweeps feel physically distinct.*

- **Why It Makes Gravita Uniquely Addictive**
  Speed feels flat if *every* stop feels identical. A `GravityBlock` or `BrakeTile` shouldn't crunch like a brick wall—it should **suck the player dead into its core** with heavy magnetic finality. Conversely, `VectorLauncher` (Phase Launch at `1.6x` = `48 u/s`) should feel *weightless and ghostly* before slamming back into reality at `KineticRam`, and `Coin` rows along corridors should sing an ascending melody that pulls players down greedy lines.

- **Exact Numbers, Curves & Inspector Parameters**
  1. **Anchor Stops (`BrakeTile` & `GravityBlock`)**:
     - Differentiate from wall stops in `PlayerImpactFeedback` via `movement.WasLastStopWallImpact == false`: zero wall-recoil shake; instead trigger `0.025` downward/inward micro-settle impulse + `PlayerSquashStretch.AnchorLock()` (`scale (1.14, 0.86)` over `0.045s` -> `(1, 1)` over `0.11s` `Ease.OutBack` **centered**, `.SetUpdate(true)`).
     - `InteractionEffectPlayer` on `Brake.prefab` & `GravityBlock.prefab` (`InteractionKind.Brake`): spawn pooled implosion/ripple ring (`0.22s`) + low-frequency magnetic thump (`SoundConfig.anchorStop`, `pitch = 0.95f`).
  2. **Phase Launch Tunneling (`VectorLauncher` & `PlayerMovement.OnPhasingChanged`)**:
     - On `OnPhasingChanged(true)`: `CinemachineCameraZoom2D` expands `+0.28` ortho (`zoomSpeed = 10f`), `PlayerSquashStretch` tints `phaseSpriteTint` and stretches `+22%` along travel axis, and `PhaseAfterimageEmitter` spawns pooled `PlayerAfterimage` sprite snapshots every `0.022s` (max `6` pooled ghosts, fading `alpha 0.55 -> 0` over `0.16s` via DOTween `.DOKill()` + `.SetUpdate(true)` + `.SetLink(gameObject)`).
     - On `OnPhasingChanged(false)` (arrival at `KineticRam`): snap ortho back to `5.5`, spawn arrival shock-ring via `VfxSpawner.Spawn`, `0.03s` micro hit-stop if impacting immediately.
  3. **Ascending Coin Corridor Sweep (`Collectible.OnAnyCoinCollected`)**:
     - Track `coinCombo` and `lastCoinTime` (`Time.unscaledTime`) inside `EntityAudio` (or `CollectibleCadenceAudio`).
     - If `now - lastCoinTime <= 0.22f`, increment `coinCombo` (cap `12`), else reset to `0`.
     - Pitch: `1.00f + coinCombo * 0.035f` (`1.00 -> 1.42`, capped at `+7` semitones), overriding `SoundConfig.coinCollect`'s random pitch during active combos!
  4. **Brittle vs Cracked Surface Telltale (`BrittleFloor` & `CrackedBlock`)**:
     - `BrittleFloor` (`InteractionKind.Crack` at Tier 1): crisp light fracture spark + `0.02` micro-tick so players *feel* they barely skated safely across glass.
     - `BrittleFloor` (`InteractionKind.Break` at Tier 2+): `0.075` snap shake + dark pit shatter burst via `InteractionEffectPlayer`.
     - `CrackedBlock` hit at Tier 1–2 (non-breaking wall stop): add `OnFailedCrush` / `InteractionEffectPlayer` dust + dull stone clink (`pitch = 0.80f`) so players immediately realize *"I didn't have enough runway speed!"*

- **Exact C# Integration Hooks**
