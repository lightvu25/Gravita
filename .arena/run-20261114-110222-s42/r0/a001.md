# Gravita (Puzzle 2D) — Game Feel Signature Blueprint

## 0. Assumptions & Design Guardrails
- **Velocity Tier Thresholds**: `VelocityTier.cs` currently maps `1–2` tiles to `Drift`, `3–4` to `Cruise`, and `5+` to `KineticRam` (whereas the higher-level spec cites `1–3` / `4–7` / `8+`). All feedback hooks below bind exclusively to `PlayerMovement.CurrentTier`, `PlayerMovement.LastImpactTier`, `OnVelocityTierChanged`, and `OnTileCrossed` — never hardcoded tile counts — so adjusting `VelocityTierUtil.FromTiles` automatically scales every audio, camera, and VFX threshold.
- **Audio Assets**: `Assets/Data/Sound/SoundConfig.asset` currently has empty `clips: []` arrays on all entries. Code hooks assume clips will be populated in `SoundConfig.asset` (using existing clips in `Assets/Audio/` as placeholders until final mastering).
- **Determinism & Architecture**: `PlayerMovement.FixedUpdate` / `SimulateStep` (`moveSpeed = 30` units/sec in `GameScene.unity`, `1 tile = 33.3 ms`) and `LevelClock` hazard cycles stay strictly deterministic. **Nothing outside `PlayerImpactFeedback` modifies `Time.timeScale`**, zero `new GameObject(...)` or runtime `AddComponent<...>()` calls are used, and every DOTween call enforces `.DOKill()`, `.SetUpdate(true)`, and `.SetLink(gameObject)`.
- **Accessibility & Speedrun Guards**: Camera shake, hit-stop, chromatic spikes, and full-screen flashes expose Inspector/setting multipliers so "Speedrun Minimal" and "Photosensitivity Safe" modes degrade gracefully to non-flashing cues.

## 1. Core Game Feel Diagnosis: Why Gravita Feels "Flat" Today

Inspecting the live scripts, `GameScene.unity`, and gameplay prefabs reveals four concrete sensory breaks causing a `30 u/s` high-speed slide puzzle to feel strangely weightless:

1. **Mid-Slide Sensory Vacuum (`33.3 ms/tile`)**: `PlayerMovement.OnTileCrossed` has **zero subscribers** across the entire codebase. Because `moveSpeed = 30` clears a 3-tile `Drift` in `100 ms` and a 5-tile `KineticRam` in `167 ms`, the slide between start and wall stop has no rising rhythmic pulse or velocity escalation beyond `PlayerLineTrail` (which also clamps `smoothedMoveSpeed` to `28f`, below `30f` normal and `48f` Phase Launch speed).
2. **Hit-Stop Freezes Before Squash Happens**: `PlayerImpactFeedback` drops `Time.timeScale = 0f` for `0.033s` (`Cruise`) and `0.083s` (`KineticRam`). However, `PlayerSquashStretch` omits `.SetUpdate(true)` and `PlayerLineTrail.LateUpdate` aborts when `Time.deltaTime <= 0f`. Meanwhile, `GameScene.unity` caps `PlayerSquashStretch.maxDeformation = 0.3` (clipping the authored `28x16` impact ratio) and offsets the `Sprite` child at `(0, -0.5, 0)`. Result: during the exact freeze frames meant to sell kinetic crunch, Subject G-0 locks rigid and only deforms *after* time resumes.
3. **Zeroed-Out Core Interactions in Scene & Prefab Data**:
   - `GameScene.unity` sets `PlayerImpactFeedback.tier1Shake = 0` and `redirectShake = 0`, stripping all short 1–2 tile hops and `DeflectionWedge` rebounds of tactile kick.
   - `Brake.prefab`, `Wedge.prefab`, `Exit.prefab`, and `Hazard.prefab` have `InteractionEffectPlayer.effectPrefabs: []` and `defaultCameraShake: 0`. `StarCollectFeedback` in `GameScene.unity` has `collectibleParticlePrefab = null` and `collectibleAnimationPrefab = null`.
   - `GravityBlock` and `BrakeTile` halt via `HaltAt` (`WasLastStopWallImpact = false`), bypassing `OnWallImpact` and leaving `EntityAudio` silent on anchor stops.
4. **Dormant Signature Systems**: `CinemachineCameraZoom2D` (`normalOrthographicSize = 5.5`) has **zero callers** and updates on scaled `Time.deltaTime`; `CloseCallDetector.OnCloseCallTriggered` has **zero subscribers** (and reuses the star particle prefab); `Assets/Volume/Global Volume Profile.asset` (`Bloom`, `Vignette`, `ChromaticAberration`) is completely static; and `PlayerMovement.HasPendingDirection` (buffered perpendicular turn) has no visual telegraph.

## 2. Five Gravita-Specific Game Feel Pillars

### Pillar 1: Kinetic Runway Escalation (Mid-Slide Momentum)

#### Why It Makes Gravita Uniquely Addictive
In stop-to-stop inertia puzzles, players rarely plan 8-tile runs unless crossing that distance *feels* noticeably different from a 2-tile hop. By converting every traversed cell (`33.3 ms`) into an ascending rhythmic tick, holding an aerodynamic stretch while sliding, subtly widening the camera FOV per velocity tier, and shedding stark white lab afterimages at `KineticRam`, long corridors and chained rebounds create an unmistakable **"ignition rush"**—turning velocity thresholds from invisible math into an addictive kinetic reward.

#### Exact Specs, Curves & Inspector Parameters
- **Per-Tile Kinetic Tick (`OnTileCrossed`)**:
  - Semitone pitch curve: `pitch = basePitch * Mathf.Pow(1.059463f, Mathf.Min(tilesTraveled - 1, maxSemitoneSteps))` with `basePitch = 0.88f`, `maxSemitoneSteps = 12` (caps at `1.76f`, +1 octave), `minInterval = 0.025f` (supports `33.3 ms/tile` without dropping beats).
  - Volume ramp: `volumeScale = Mathf.Lerp(0.35f, 0.85f, Mathf.Clamp01(tilesTraveled / 8f))`.
- **Camera Runway Pull-Out (`CinemachineCameraZoom2D`)**:
  - `Drift` (`baseOrtho = 5.50f`) -> `Cruise` (`+0.18f` = `5.68f`, `zoomSpeed = 6f`) -> `KineticRam` (`+0.38f` = `5.88f`, `zoomSpeed = 7f`) -> `Phasing` (`+0.45f` = `5.95f`). Uses `Time.unscaledDeltaTime`.
- **Sustained Aerodynamic Stretch (`PlayerSquashStretch`)**:
  - Hold stretch **until `OnMoveStopped`** instead of bouncing back mid-slide: `Drift` (`+8%` along / `-4%` across), `Cruise` (`+14%` along / `-7%` across), `KineticRam` (`+20%` along / `-10%` across) over `0.06s` (`Ease.OutQuad`, `.SetUpdate(true)`).
- **Kinetic Ram & Phase Afterimages**:
  - Active only when `CurrentTier == VelocityTier.KineticRam || IsPhasing`.
  - Spawn interval: `0.032s` (~1 ghost per tile at `30 u/s`, max pool size `10`), ghost lifetime `0.16s` (unscaled), alpha `0.62 -> 0f` (`Ease.OutQuad`), scale `1.0 -> 0.86` (`Ease.InQuad`).
  - Colors (`_FlashColor` HDR on existing `Assets/Shaders/Hit/HitFlashSprite.shader`): `KineticRam` = `#FF52C7` (`a = 0.62`), `Phasing` = `#B86BFF` (`a = 0.70`).
- **URP Volume Escalation (`Global Volume`)**:
  - `KineticRam`: `ChromaticAberration.intensity` `0.20f -> 0.40f` (`0.08s`), `Bloom.intensity` `9.0f -> 11.2f` (`0.08s`); eases back to baseline over `0.14s` on stop.
- **`PlayerLineTrail` Speed Clamp Fix**:
  - Raise `Mathf.Clamp(instSpeed, 4f, 28f)` to `Mathf.Clamp(instSpeed, 4f, 60f)` (`30 u/s` slide & `48 u/s` Phase Launch) and step `LateUpdate` retraction with `Time.unscaledDeltaTime` during Hit-Stop.

#### Exact C# Integration Hooks
- **Upgrade `SoundManager.cs`**: Add `public void PlaySFX(SoundEntry entry, float pitchMultiplier, float volumeScale = 1f)` reusing `lastPlayedTimeByClip` and `PlayResolvedClip` so deterministic pitch curves require zero duplicate pooling logic.
- **Upgrade `CinemachineCameraZoom2D.cs`**: Initialize `private float baseSize;` to `normalOrthographicSize` in `Awake()`, track `private float punchOffset;` and `private Tween punchTween;`, and compose them cleanly in `Update()` using `Time.unscaledDeltaTime`:
  ```csharp
  baseSize = Mathf.Lerp(baseSize, targetOrthographicSize, Time.unscaledDeltaTime * zoomSpeed);
  cinemachineCamera.Lens.OrthographicSize = baseSize + punchOffset;

  public void PunchZoom(float orthoDelta, float returnDuration = 0.18f)
  {
      punchTween?.Kill();
      punchOffset = orthoDelta;
      punchTween = DOVirtual.Float(orthoDelta, 0f, Mathf.Max(0.05f, returnDuration), v => punchOffset = v)
          .SetEase(Ease.OutCubic)
          .SetUpdate(true)
          .SetLink(gameObject);
  }
  ```
- **New `Assets/Scripts/Feedback/KineticRunwayFeedback.cs`** (attached to Player): Subscribes to `PlayerMovement.OnTileCrossed`, `OnVelocityTierChanged`, `OnPhasingChanged`, `OnMoveStopped`. Emits pooled `afterimagePrefab` via `ObjectPoolManager.SpawnObject(...)` (with `MaterialPropertyBlock` setting `_FlashColor` on `PooledAfterimage`) and calls `CinemachineCameraZoom2D.Instance.SetTargetOrthographicSize(...)` + `VolumePulseFeedback.SetSustainedTier(...)`.

#### Unity Inspector / Prefab Wiring Notes
- Author **`KineticAfterimage.prefab`** (`SpriteRenderer` using `Assets/Shaders/Hit/HitFlash White.mat` + `PooledAfterimage.cs` which runs `DOVirtual.Float` `.SetUpdate(true).SetLink(gameObject, LinkBehaviour.KillOnDisable)` and calls `ObjectPoolManager.ReturnObjectToPool(gameObject)` on complete). Wire into `KineticRunwayFeedback.afterimagePrefab`.
- Wire `SoundConfig.tileCruiseTick` (`minInterval = 0.025f`, `randomizePitch = false`) and scene `Global Volume` into `VolumePulseFeedback`.

### Pillar 2: Unscaled Elastic Impact & Magnetic Anchor Choreography

#### Why It Makes Gravita Uniquely Addictive
Because Gravita locks perpendicular steering mid-corridor (`bufferTurnsUntilStop = true`), **every stop is a decision beat**. Fixing Hit-Stop so Subject G-0 compresses *during* the freeze frame—while giving small Drift taps a crisp click, `CrackedBlock` smashes a forward "punch-through" zoom kick, and `GravityBlock`/`BrakeTile` stops a distinct magnetic implosion—makes every wall, block, and anchor feel physically distinct through touch and sight alone.

#### Exact Specs, Curves & Inspector Parameters
- **Unscaled 2-Stage Impact Squash (`PlayerSquashStretch`)**:
  - Set `maxDeformation = 0.45f` in `GameScene.unity` (unlocks full `28x16` deformation).
  - Stage 1 (`0.035s`, `Ease.OutExpo`, `.SetUpdate(true)`): Immediate snap into squash *inside* the Hit-Stop window (`Tier 1`: `60%` deform, `wallPressPx = 2.5f`; `Tier 2`: `85%` deform, `wallPressPx = 4.0f`; `Tier 3`: `100%` deform, `wallPressPx = 5.0f`).
  - Stage 2 (`0.145s`, `Ease.OutBack(1.35f)`, `.SetUpdate(true)`): Elastic spring back to `baseScale` / `baseLocalPosition`.
  - **Bottom-Pivot `(0.5, 0)` Compensation**: Because `Sprite` sits at `localPosition (0, -0.5, 0)`, apply a vertical center-bias `float pivotCompY = (1f - deformedScale.y) * 0.25f` when squashing vertically so upward wall impacts don't pull the sprite away from the ceiling.
- **Tiered Trauma & `CrackedBlock` Smash Distinction (`PlayerImpactFeedback`)**:
  - `Tier 1 (Drift)` Wall Stop: `tier1Shake = 0.045f` (up from `0`), `tier1HitStop = 0f`.
  - `Tier 2 (Cruise)` Wall Stop: `tier2Shake = 0.12f`, `tier2HitStop = 0.033f`, `PunchZoom(-0.10f, 0.14f)`.
  - `Tier 3 (KineticRam)` Solid Wall Stop: `tier3Shake = 0.24f`, `tier3HitStop = 0.067f`, `PunchZoom(-0.18f, 0.16f)`.
  - `CrackedBlock` Break (`CrackedBlock.OnAnyBroken`): `blockBreakShake = 0.28f`, `blockBreakHitStop = 0.055f`, `PunchZoom(-0.26f, 0.18f)`, plus directional shockwave & debris cone aligned to `movement.CurrentDirection`, and `HapticEvent.KineticImpact` (`HeavyImpact`).
- **Magnetic Anchor Lock (`GravityBlock` / `BrakeTile`, `WasLastStopWallImpact == false`)**:
  - **Implosion Scale Pulse**: `0.04s` shrink to `(0.84, 0.84, 1)` (`Ease.OutQuad`, `.SetUpdate(true)`), `0.12s` spring to `(1.06, 1.06, 1)` -> `(1, 1, 1)` (`Ease.OutBack`), `anchorShake = 0.05f`, zero hit-stop (`0f`), and dedicated `SoundConfig.gravityAnchor` low-end lock thud.

#### Exact C# Integration Hooks
- **`PlayerSquashStretch.cs`**:
  - Always call `visualTransform.DOKill(); KillActive();` before building `activeSequence`, append `.SetUpdate(true).SetLink(gameObject)`, and reset `visualTransform.localRotation = Quaternion.identity` in `OnDisable()`.
  - In `HandleMoveStopped()`, branch on `movement.WasLastStopWallImpact`: wall stops call `Squash(lastDirection, tierIntensity)` while floor stops call `PlayAnchorImplosion()`.
- **`PlayerImpactFeedback.cs`**:
  - Add `[SerializeField] private float blockBreakShake = 0.28f`, `blockBreakHitStop = 0.055f`, `anchorShake = 0.05f`, and `CinemachineCameraZoom2D` punch calls inside `HandleStopped()` and `HandleBlockBroken(CrackedBlock)`.
  - Trigger `PulseHitStop(blockBreakHitStop)`, `ShakeCamera(blockBreakShake)`, and `PunchZoom(-0.26f, 0.18f)` in `HandleBlockBroken` *before* the `block.HasCustomBreakEffect` particle early-return, and rotate `blockBreakParticlePrefab` using `Quaternion.LookRotation((Vector3)movement.CurrentDirection)` when non-zero.
- **`EntityAudio.cs` + `SoundConfig.cs`**:
  - Subscribe `EntityAudio` symmetrically in `OnEnable`/`OnDisable` to `MazeTile.OnAnyPlayerInteracted` (filtering `GravityBlock` / `BrakeTile` with `InteractionKind.Brake`) -> play `SoundConfig.gravityAnchor`.
- **`GameplayHapticsFeedback.cs` + `HapticEvent.cs` + `GameHaptics.cs` + `MostHapticsBackend.cs`**:
  - Add `KineticImpact = 7` (`MOST_HapticFeedback.HapticTypes.HeavyImpact`) and `CloseCall = 8` (`RigidImpact`) to `HapticEvent`, map them in `MostHapticsBackend.MapPreset`, and bump `GameHaptics.EventCount` from `7` to `9` (extending `EventCooldownSeconds` with `0.08f, 0.10f`).
  - In `HandleWallImpact`, play `HapticEvent.KineticImpact` when `movement.LastImpactTier == VelocityTier.KineticRam`, and subscribe to `CrackedBlock.OnAnyBroken`.

#### Unity Inspector / Prefab Wiring Notes
- In `GameScene.unity` (`Player`): set `PlayerImpactFeedback` -> `tier1Shake = 0.045`, `tier2Shake = 0.12`, `tier3Shake = 0.24`, `tier3HitStop = 0.067`, `redirectShake = 0.07`; set `PlayerSquashStretch` -> `maxDeformation = 0.45`.
- On `Brake.prefab` & `GravityBlock.prefab`: assign pooled implosion-ring VFX to `InteractionEffectPlayer.effectPrefabs` and set `defaultCameraShake = 0.05` on `Brake.prefab`.

### Pillar 3: Harmonic Bank-Shot & Corridor Arpeggio

#### Why It Makes Gravita Uniquely Addictive
Gravita's puzzle identity shines when a player banks off 3 `DeflectionWedge` tiles through a line of coins and lands a `KineticRam` smash. Right now, random `±8%` pitch on `wedgeRedirect` and `coinCollect` turns combos into flat noise, `redirectShake = 0`, and `Wedge.prefab` spawns nothing. Replacing random variance with **deterministic major-pentatonic pitch chains that reset only when Subject G-0 stops** turns multi-wedge routes and coin corridors into tactile musical phrases.

#### Exact Specs, Curves & Inspector Parameters
- **Wedge / VectorPad Harmonic Escalation (`OnRedirected`)**:
  - Major pentatonic semitones: `int[] { 0, 2, 4, 7, 9, 12, 14, 16 }` (`redirectChainCount` increments per `OnRedirected`, resets to `0` on `OnMoveStopped`).
  - Pitch: `Mathf.Pow(1.059463f, Pentatonic[Mathf.Min(chain, 7)])` (`1.00x` -> `2.52x`); volume `Mathf.Min(1f, 0.72f + chain * 0.04f)`.
- **Corridor Coin Arpeggio (`Collectible.OnAnyCoinCollected`)**:
  - Track `coinChainCount` in `EntityAudio` (reset on `OnMoveStopped`). Walk same pentatonic array capped at `+12` semitones (`2.00x`), `minInterval = 0.02f` (cleanly tracks `30 u/s` 1-tile spacing).
- **Snap-Bank Visual & Camera Punch**:
  - `PlayerImpactFeedback.HandleRedirected`: `shake = redirectShake + 0.015f * Mathf.Min(redirectChainCount, 4)` (`0.07f -> 0.13f`).
  - `PlayerSquashStretch.SnapRedirect(Vector2 newDir)`: `0.09s` unscaled Z-tilt whip (`±9°` based on cross product `oldDir x newDir`, returning to `0°` `Ease.OutBack`) + axial stretch `+14%` along `newDir`.
- **Phase Launch (`VectorLauncher` / `OnPhasingChanged`)**:
  - Launch (`OnPhasingChanged(true)`): `PunchZoom(-0.22f, 0.12f)`, `ChromaticAberration` spike `0.20f -> 0.52f` over `0.10s`, `SoundConfig.phaseLaunchStart`.
  - Landing (`OnPhasingChanged(false)`): `ShakeCamera(0.16f)` + `SoundConfig.phaseLaunchLand` (locks directly into Pillar 1's `KineticRam` state).

#### Exact C# Integration Hooks
- **`EntityAudio.cs`**: Subscribe to `playerMovement.OnMoveStopped` (reset `redirectChainCount = 0; coinChainCount = 0;`) and `playerMovement.OnPhasingChanged`. Update `HandlePlayerRedirected` and `HandleCoinCollected` to call `SoundManager.Instance.PlaySFX(entry, ResolvePentatonicPitch(index), volumeScale)`.
- **`PlayerSquashStretch.cs`**: Subscribe to `movement.OnRedirected += HandleRedirected` -> execute `SnapRedirect(lastDirection, newDir)` using `visualTransform.DOKill()`, `visualTransform.DOLocalRotate(...)`, `.SetUpdate(true)`, `.SetLink(gameObject)`.
- **`PlayerImpactFeedback.cs`**: Track `redirectChainCount` (reset in `HandleStopped()`) to scale `redirectShake`.

#### Unity Inspector / Prefab Wiring Notes
- **`Wedge.prefab`**: Populate `InteractionEffectPlayer.effectPrefabs` with a directional spark burst prefab, check `alignToInteractionDirection = true`, and set `defaultCameraShake = 0.07`.
- **`GameScene.unity` (`StarCollectFeedback`)**: Assign a tiny lightweight pooled burst prefab to `collectibleParticlePrefab` (currently `null`) so `30 u/s` coin lines leave crisp sparkling pops.

### Pillar 4: Close-Call Adrenaline Surge (Time-Neutral)

#### Why It Makes Gravita Uniquely Addictive
Gliding across a `PulseLaser` `100 ms` before ignition or grazing past an active `SentinelDronePatrol` at `30 u/s` is pure arcade tension—yet `CloseCallDetector.OnCloseCallTriggered` currently fires with **zero subscribers**, and `CloseCallDetector` strictly forbids touching `Time.timeScale` so `LevelClock` hazard cycles stay deterministic. Synthesizing a perceptual "time-dilation surge" purely via lens snap, aberration flash, streak-pitched chime, and floating world callout turns near-misses into signature highlight moments.

#### Exact Specs, Curves & Inspector Parameters
- **Lens & Trauma Punch**:
  - `CinemachineCameraZoom2D.PunchZoom(-0.32f, 0.22f)` (`5.50f -> 5.18f` instant snap, `Ease.OutCubic` recovery in unscaled time).
  - Keep `CloseCallDetector.cameraPulseForce = 0.08f`.
- **URP Chromatic & Vignette Flash (`VolumePulseFeedback`)**:
  - `ChromaticAberration.intensity`: spike `0.20f -> 0.55f`, decay `0.18s` (`Ease.OutExpo`, `.SetUpdate(true)`).
  - `Vignette.intensity`: brief tighten `0.40f -> 0.52f`, decay `0.20s` (skipped/halved when `photosensitivitySafeMode = true`).
- **Pooled World Callout & Dedicated Ring (`VfxSpawner` / `ObjectPoolManager`)**:
  - Replace `CloseCallDetector.closeCallVfxPrefab` (currently pointing to the star particle GUID) with dedicated `CloseCallBurst.prefab` (cyan-magenta `0.3 -> 2.0` tile expanding ring over `0.22s`).
  - Spawn pooled `CloseCallCallout.prefab` (World-Space TMP `"CLOSE!"` / `"+5"`) at `worldPos + Vector3.up * 0.45f`: scale `0.6 -> 1.15 -> 1.0` (`0.12s`, `Ease.OutBack`), float `+0.65` units (`0.42s`, `Ease.OutQuad`), fade `1 -> 0` over last `0.18s`, `.SetUpdate(true).SetLink(gameObject, LinkBehaviour.KillOnDisable)` -> `ObjectPoolManager.ReturnObjectToPool(gameObject)`.
- **Streak-Pitched Audio & Haptics**:
  - `closeCallStreak` increments within a `2.5s` unscaled window (max `4`): `pitch = 1.0f * Mathf.Pow(1.059463f, streak * 2)` (`1.00x -> 1.59x`) on `SoundConfig.closeCallPing`.
  - `GameplayHapticsFeedback` triggers `GameHaptics.Play(HapticEvent.CloseCall)`.

#### Exact C# Integration Hooks
- **New `Assets/Scripts/Feedback/CloseCallFeedbackBridge.cs`** (placed on Feedback/Audio manager object in `GameScene`):
  - `[SerializeField] private GameObject calloutPrefab;`
  - `[SerializeField] private VolumePulseFeedback volumePulse;`
  - `[SerializeField] private bool photosensitivitySafeMode = false;`
  - Subscribes in `OnEnable`/`OnDisable` to `CloseCallDetector.OnCloseCallTriggered += HandleCloseCall`.
  - Triggers `CinemachineCameraZoom2D.Instance?.PunchZoom(-0.32f, 0.22f)`, `volumePulse?.PulseCloseCall(photosensitivitySafeMode)`, `ObjectPoolManager.SpawnObject(calloutPrefab, ...)`, and `SoundManager.Instance?.PlaySFX(cfg.closeCallPing, streakPitch)`.
- **`GameplayHapticsFeedback.cs`**: Subscribe in `BindListeners`/`UnbindListeners` to `CloseCallDetector.OnCloseCallTriggered += HandleCloseCall`.

#### Unity Inspector / Prefab Wiring Notes
- Create `CloseCallBurst.prefab` and `CloseCallCallout.prefab` (with `PooledFloatCallout.cs`), assign `CloseCallBurst` to `CloseCallDetector.closeCallVfxPrefab`, and assign `CloseCallCallout` + scene `VolumePulseFeedback` to `CloseCallFeedbackBridge`.

### Pillar 5: Input Anticipation & Lab World Tactility

#### Why It Makes Gravita Uniquely Addictive
At `30 u/s` with `bufferTurnsUntilStop = true`, expert players swipe their *next* turn before Subject G-0 even reaches the wall, and actively weave around `GravityBlock` anchors, `CircuitSwitch` gates, and `PowerUpController` tools (`Breaker`, `Freeze`, `Teleport`). Making buffered turns visible *before* impact, giving `GravityBlock` anchors an unmistakable lock-in state, and giving active skills + interactive tiles crisp lab feedback eliminates "did my swipe register?" uncertainty and elevates the sci-fi test-chamber atmosphere.

#### Exact Specs, Curves & Inspector Parameters
- **Buffered Turn Telegraph (`PlayerMovement.HasPendingDirection`)**:
  - Pre-wired child `PendingTurnChevron` (`SpriteRenderer` placed `0.58` units outward from player center, inactive by default) rotates to `PendingDirection` and pops scale `0.5 -> 1.1 -> 0.95` over `0.08s` (`Ease.OutBack`, `.SetUpdate(true)`) the instant a perpendicular turn is queued mid-slide, then hides immediately on consume/clear.
  - Plays ultra-subtle `SoundConfig.turnBufferClick` (`volume = 0.35f`, `minInterval = 0.04f`) + `HapticEvent.UISelect` (`Selection`) so players *feel* the queued corner turn lock in mid-flight.
- **`AwaitingFreshInput` Anchor Lock Cue (`GravityBlock` / `BrakeTile`)**:
  - While `movement.IsAwaitingFreshInput == true`, a pre-wired child `AnchorRing` on the Player pulses subtly (`alpha 0.35 <-> 0.85`, `scale 0.95 <-> 1.08`, period `0.65s` unscaled loop) to signal "locked — swipe fresh to launch". Stops immediately on `OnMoveStarted`.
- **Lab Skill Signatures (`PowerUpController.OnPowerUpActivated`)**:
  - `Breaker`: `PulseHitStop(0.045f)`, `ShakeCamera(0.22f)`, `PunchZoom(-0.18f, 0.16f)`, pooled directional shockwave prefab (replacing `PowerUpController`'s runtime `new GameObject` calls).
  - `Freeze`: `VolumePulseFeedback` `ColorAdjustments.colorFilter` flash to `#9EEBFF` (`0.18s`) settling to subtle `#DAF5FF` tint while `PowerUpController.IsFreezeActive`, plus `SoundConfig.powerUpFreeze`.
  - `Teleport`: `PunchZoom(-0.20f, 0.15f)` + `ChromaticAberration` spike `0.50f -> 0.20f` (`0.15s`), pooled entry/exit warp rings via `VfxSpawner.Spawn(...)`.
- **Silent Prefab Wiring (`Exit`, `Hazard`, `Brittle`, `CircuitSwitch`)**:
  - Populate `Exit.prefab` (`InteractionEffectPlayer.effectPrefabs` + `defaultCameraShake = 0.10`), `Hazard.prefab` (`defaultCameraShake = 0.18` + sharp burst), and wire `EntityAudio` to `MazeTile.OnAnyPlayerInteracted` for `Switch`, `BrittleFloor` (`Crack` vs `Break`), and `FailingTile` (`Crack` vs `Break`).

#### Exact C# Integration Hooks
- **`PlayerMovement.cs`** (pure read-only telemetry — zero physics/solvability impact):

  - Holds `[SerializeField] private Transform pendingTurnChevron;` and `[SerializeField] private SpriteRenderer anchorLockRing;`.
  - Subscribes to `PlayerMovement.OnPendingDirectionChanged`, `OnMoveStarted`, `OnMoveStopped` (`IsAwaitingFreshInput`). All DOTween animations use `.DOKill()`, `.SetUpdate(true)`, `.SetLink(gameObject)`.
