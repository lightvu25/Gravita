# Gravita (Puzzle 2D) — Game Feel Signature Upgrade Blueprint

> **Goal**: Transform Gravita from a clean stop-to-stop grid puzzle with isolated juice effects into a rhythmic, high-speed kinetic experience where every buffered swipe, 30-tile/sec corridor run, 90° wedge bank-shot, and razor-thin laser graze feels unmistakably tactile, readable, and addictive—without altering deterministic `FixedUpdate` grid physics or creating runtime UI/VFX hierarchy procedurally.

---

## Assumptions & Grounded Baseline

- **Actual Code/Scene Velocity Metrics**: In `Assets/Scenes/GameScene.unity`, `PlayerMovement` runs at `moveSpeed = 30` units/sec (`33.3 ms` per `1.0`-unit cell at `32 PPU`) with `bufferTurnsUntilStop = true` and `maxConsecutiveRedirects = 8`.
- **Velocity Tier Mapping**: `Assets/Scripts/Gameplay/VelocityTier.cs` (`VelocityTierUtil.FromTiles`) currently maps `1–2` tiles → `Drift`, `3–4` tiles → `Cruise`, and `5+` tiles → `KineticRam` (whereas earlier design notes reference `1–3` / `4–7` / `8+`). All hooks below subscribe to `OnVelocityTierChanged` and `OnTileCrossed(int)`, so re-tuning tile thresholds in `VelocityTierUtil` updates every feedback system automatically.
- **Audio & Camera State**: `Assets/Data/Sound/SoundConfig.asset` currently has empty `clips: []` across all entries, and `Main Camera` uses `PixelPerfectCamera` (`320×180`, `32 PPU`, `m_GridSnapping = 1`) paired with `CinemachinePixelPerfect` (`m_Enabled = 1`) on `CinemachineCamera`.
- **Zero-Procedural-Hierarchy Rule**: Every visual element below uses Inspector-assigned child objects, `MaterialPropertyBlock` shader properties, or pooled prefabs spawned through `VfxSpawner.Spawn(...)` (`ObjectPoolManager` / `ParticleSpawner` / `PrefabAnimationSpawner`). *(Note: existing runtime `new GameObject(...)` calls in `PowerUpController.SpawnRadialPulseVFX` should be treated as legacy debt and not replicated.)*

## 1. Core Game Feel Diagnosis: Why Gravita Currently Feels Flat

Gravita already owns shake, squash-and-stretch, trails, hit-stop, haptics, and a close-call detector—yet feels muted in play because **four mechanical and technical bottlenecks neutralize its juice**:

1. **Hit-Stop Freezes Its Own Visual Payoff**: `PlayerImpactFeedback.PulseHitStop` drops `Time.timeScale = 0` for `0.033s` (`Cruise`) and `0.083s` (`KineticRam`), yet:
   - `PlayerSquashStretch.Play()` and `ResetScale()` do **not** call `.SetUpdate(true)`, so Subject G-0 freezes mid-frame instead of snapping into the wall during Hit-Stop.
   - `Main Camera`'s `CinemachineBrain` has `IgnoreTimeScale = 0` in `GameScene.unity`, pausing camera impulse decay during Hit-Stop and making impact pauses feel like framerate hitches rather than heavy compression.
2. **Camera Pixel-Lock Conflicts with `CinemachineCameraZoom2D` & Zeroed Tier-1/Wedge Shake**:
   - `CinemachineCameraZoom2D` (`normalOrthographicSize = 5.5`, `zoomSpeed = 4`) has **zero callers** in the codebase, lerps using `Time.deltaTime` (halting during Hit-Stop), and sits beneath an enabled `CinemachinePixelPerfect` component that overwrites `Lens.OrthographicSize` every frame.
   - `PlayerImpactFeedback` in `GameScene.unity` sets `tier1Shake = 0` and `redirectShake = 0`, while `CinemachinePositionComposer` uses `Damping = (1, 1, 1)` with `Lookahead.Enabled = 0`—causing the camera to lag behind `30 u/s` slides instead of leading momentum.
3. **Silent Mid-Slide Escalation & Vanishing Deformation**:
   - `PlayerMovement.OnTileCrossed` has **0 subscribers**. At `30 tiles/sec`, crossing into `Cruise` (`100 ms`) or `KineticRam` (`167 ms+`) produces no rising rhythmic cadence.
   - `PlayerSquashStretch.HandleTierChanged` pulses stretch for `stretchDuration * 0.85f` (`0.153s`) and immediately returns to `baseScale`, leaving Subject G-0 visually identical at `KineticRam` vs idle. Additionally, Subject G-0's `SpriteRenderer` child sits at `localPosition = (0, -0.5, 0)` (bottom-center `(0.5, 0)` pivot), causing horizontal wall squashes to anchor around the bottom edge instead of the impacted wall normal.
4. **Disconnected Signature Loops & Static Post-Processing**:
   - `CloseCallDetector.OnCloseCallTriggered` has **0 subscribers** (and its coin reward is bypassed because `EconomyBalanceConfig.Active.EnableLegacyExtraRewardSystems` defaults to `false`).
   - `Brake.prefab`, `Exit.prefab`, `Hazard.prefab`, and `Wedge.prefab` have empty `InteractionEffectPlayer` lists and `0` camera shake.
   - `Global Volume Profile.asset` permanently applies static `ChromaticAberration` (`intensity = 0.2`), softening pixel art at rest while leaving zero headroom for kinetic spikes.

## 2. Five High-Impact, Gravita-Specific Game Feel Pillars

### Pillar 1: "Chambered Turn" Diegetic Input Lock & Anchor Contrast

#### Psychological & Rhythmic Hook
With `bufferTurnsUntilStop = true`, high-skill Gravita play relies on **swiping early mid-corridor** so the next perpendicular dash fires on the exact frame Subject G-0 hits the wall. Right now, queuing `pendingDirection` gives zero sensory confirmation, and stopping on a `GravityBlock` / `BrakeTile` (`MovementState.AwaitingFreshInput`) looks visually identical to normal wall rest. Making buffered turns feel like *chambering a directional charge*—and making Gravity/Brake tiles feel like a *centripetal magnetic clamp*—turns planning ahead into a crisp mechanical rhythm.

#### Explicit Trade-Off
- **Choice**: Render a compact **on-body 1-tile directional chevron** (`0.55` units ahead of Subject G-0) + subtle relay click rather than a multi-tile dotted trajectory laser.
- **What this gives up**: Players do not see full wall-to-wall landing predictions across the maze.
- **Why for Gravita**: On a `320×180` portrait grid where puzzle routing is the core challenge, full trajectory lasers clutter corridors and solve routing visually; a local 1-tile chevron confirms input registration without spoiling the puzzle.

### Pillar 2: Harmonic Runway Cadence & Sustained Kinetic Posture ("The 30-Tile/s Accelerando")

#### Psychological & Rhythmic Hook
At `moveSpeed = 30`, `Drift` (`1–2` tiles = `33–67 ms`), `Cruise` (`3–4` tiles = `100–133 ms`), and `KineticRam` (`5+` tiles = `167 ms+`) happen too quickly for players to count tiles visually. Right now `OnTileCrossed` is completely unsubscribed and `PlayerSquashStretch` snaps back to idle proportions mid-dash. Linking every crossed grid boundary to an **ascending pentatonic pulse**, locking Subject G-0 into a **sustained speed silhouette**, and emitting **cadence-locked afterimages** at `KineticRam` gives players an unmistakable visceral speedometer they can *feel* without ever looking away from the character.

#### Explicit Trade-Off
- **Choice**: Quantize `OnTileCrossed` audio to a **10-step ascending harmonic pitch table** (clamped at tile 10) and restrict pooled afterimages strictly to `KineticRam` / `Phasing` (throttled to `35 ms` = `~1.05` tiles), rather than playing continuous pitch-gliding loops or spawning trails at `Drift`.
- **What this gives up**: No continuous turbine whine during long slides and no ghostly echoes on short 1–2 tile repositioning hops.
- **Why for Gravita**: Long wedge-chained slides (`10–25` tiles) would produce shrill, fatiguing pitch climbs if uncapped, and spawning sprites during `Drift` blurs the visual distinction between safe (`Drift`) and destructive (`KineticRam`) momentum.

### Pillar 3: Alive Hit-Stop, Pivot-Corrected Wall Compression & Pixel-Safe Camera Kick

#### Psychological & Rhythmic Hook
In a stop-to-stop maze game, **colliding with a wall is the downbeat of every sentence**. Currently `Drift` (`tier1Shake = 0`) is silent in camera/squash during short stops, while `KineticRam` (`tier3HitStop = 0.083s`) freezes both `PlayerSquashStretch` and `CinemachineBrain`, dulling its impact. Keeping `Drift` crisp and vibration-free while making `Cruise` / `KineticRam` impacts **visually alive inside the freeze frames** (pivot-corrected wall slam + 2-frame white silhouette flash + directional camera recoil + dynamic URP Bloom/Chromatic spike) delivers massive weight contrast between "reposition" and "ram".

#### Explicit Trade-Off
- **Choice (Recommended): Keep `CinemachinePixelPerfect` locked on `CinemachineCamera` (`32 PPU`) and express speed/impact through `CinemachinePositionComposer.TargetOffset` directional look-ahead + unscaled recoil + URP post-process spikes**, rather than enabling `CinemachineCameraZoom2D` continuous ortho-zoom.
- **What this gives up**: Smooth continuous lens zoom-in/zoom-out (`5.5 ↔ 5.15`) during high-speed runs.
- **Why for Gravita**: With `PixelPerfectCamera` (`320×180`, `m_GridSnapping = 1`) and `CinemachinePixelPerfect` active in `GameScene.unity`, arbitrary `OrthographicSize` changes either get overwritten every frame by `CinemachinePixelPerfect` or force disabling pixel snapping—introducing sub-pixel tilemap seam shimmer and yo-yo disorientation during `0.15s` dashes.
- *(Optional Alternative if smooth zoom is explicitly desired: Disable `CinemachinePixelPerfect` on `CinemachineCamera`, switch `CinemachineCameraZoom2D.Update()` to `Time.unscaledDeltaTime` with `zoomSpeed = 14f`, and call `SetTargetOrthographicSize(5.18f)` for `0.09s` ONLY on `CrackedBlock.OnAnyBroken` or `5.80f` during `VectorLauncher` `IsPhasing`, snapping back via `SetNormalOrthographicSize()` on stop—accepting transient non-integer pixel scaling during those peaks.)*

### Pillar 4: Bisector Pinball Ricochet & Phase-Tunnel Sonic Boom

#### Psychological & Rhythmic Hook
Chaining `DeflectionWedge` and `VectorPad` rebounds **without resetting `TilesTraveled`** (`PlayerMovement.Redirect`) is what transforms Gravita from a basic grid slider into a **billiard-table kinetic puzzle**, while `VectorLauncher` (`1.6x` Phase Launch through walls) is its high-drama climax. Right now `redirectShake = 0`, `Wedge.prefab` has an empty `InteractionEffectPlayer`, and `OnPhasingChanged` only swaps flat sprite color—so chained 4-corner `KineticRam` trajectories and wall-tunneling launches feel flat.

#### Explicit Trade-Off
- **Choice**: Keep **0 ms Hit-Stop** on `DeflectionWedge` / `VectorPad` redirects and communicate bank-shot momentum through **diagonal mirror-plane spark alignment + escalating musical pitch per corner + 40 ms tile bounce**, reserving Hit-Stop strictly for `VectorLauncher` landing re-entry.
- **What this gives up**: Heavy freeze-frame weight on individual wedge corners.
- **Why for Gravita**: With `maxConsecutiveRedirects = 8` at `30 u/s`, even a `25 ms` freeze per corner on a 4-wedge chain injects `100 ms` of stutter into what should feel like a lightning-fast frictionless ricochet.

### Pillar 5: Rhythm-Safe "Electro-Graze" Close Calls & Hazard Beat Telegraphing

#### Psychological & Rhythmic Hook
Slipping past a `PulseLaser`, `OverloadedFloor`, or `SentinelDronePatrol` within `150 ms` of ignition is peak risk-reward thrill in a timed maze puzzle. Currently `CloseCallDetector.OnCloseCallTriggered` has zero audio, zero post-process, zero UI callout, and `0` coins under campaign economy settings (`EnableLegacyExtraRewardSystems == false`). Meanwhile, `HazardActivation` changes flat colors without kinetic anticipation. Turning close calls into a **lightning-arc "Electro-Graze" combo** and giving timed hazards a **physical beat telegraph** turns survival tension into pure arcade flow.

#### Explicit Trade-Off
- **Choice**: **Zero `Time.timeScale` dilation** on `CloseCallDetector` (keeping `FixedUpdate` cadence 100% continuous) + compact **world-space pooled floating badge**, rather than triggering `0.25×` slow-motion or HUD corner popups.
- **What this gives up**: Dramatic bullet-time slow-down during near-misses.
- **Why for Gravita**: `CloseCallDetector.cs` explicitly forbids modifying `Time.timeScale`, and at `30 u/s` players buffer corner swipes by muscle-memory rhythm (`100–200 ms`). Mid-slide slow-motion breaks swipe timing and feels like input stutter on subsequent turns.
