ATTACK 1: CONCEDE. Verified in `GameScene.unity`, `CinemachinePixelPerfect.cs`, and `PixelPerfectCameraInternal.cs`: both `CinemachinePixelPerfect` and `PixelPerfectCamera` quantize every zoom target to `5.625f`, while `CinemachineCameraZoom2D.Update` fights any separate `PulseOrthoPunch` tween and `StopMoving`/`CrackedBlock.OnAnyBroken` event ordering collides with rebound targets. Fixed by disabling both `CinemachinePixelPerfect` and `PixelPerfectCamera` in `GameScene.unity`, refactoring `CinemachineCameraZoom2D` into a single-writer `smoothedBaseOrthoSize + punchOffset` model with unified naming `PulseOrthoPunch`, and keeping `CrackedBlock.OnAnyBroken` off `baseTargetOrthoSize`.

ATTACK 2: CONCEDE. Verified in `PlayerMovement.cs`: `SimulateStep` clears `pendingDirection` before `OnMoveStarted` (causing false `#FF4A57` red flashes on valid launches), `QueueDirection` sets `pendingDirection` for idle launches and instant reversals, and `Redirect`/`StartPhaseLaunch`/`StepPhaseLaunch` clear `pendingDirection` without calling `StopMoving`. Fixed by replacing raw `OnPendingDirectionChanged(Vector2.zero)` inference with three explicit presentation events (`OnBufferedTurnQueued` guarded to active non-phasing sliding perpendicular turns, `OnBufferedTurnResolved(dir, accepted)` fired separately for consumed vs blocked buffered turns, and `OnBufferedTurnCleared` on all silent clear sites).

Fixed by wiring a dedicated 12-voice `PooledSfxSource.prefab` (or pre-placed child `SfxPool` sources) on `AudioSystems.prefab` and preventing `PlayResolvedClip` from mutating the pitch of an already-playing `AudioSource`.
