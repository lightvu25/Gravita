# Gravita (Puzzle 2D) — Game Feel Signature Upgrade Blueprint

> **Goal**: Transform Gravita from a crisp stop-to-stop grid puzzle with isolated juice scripts into an unmistakable, rhythm-driven kinetic experience where every chambered swipe, 30-tile/sec corridor accelerando, 90° wedge bank-shot, phase tunnel boom, and laser electro-graze feels razor-sharp and tactile—without altering deterministic `FixedUpdate` grid physics or creating runtime UI/VFX hierarchy procedurally.

---

## Grounded Baseline & Engineering Constraints

- **Physics & Timestep Metrics**: In `Assets/Scenes/GameScene.unity`, `PlayerMovement` slides at `moveSpeed = 30` units/sec (`1.0` world unit = `32 PPU` cell; `~33.3 ms` per tile on average) with `bufferTurnsUntilStop = true` and `maxConsecutiveRedirects = 8`. In `ProjectSettings/TimeManager.asset`, `Fixed Timestep` is `2822399 / 141120000` (`~0.02s`), so `FixedUpdate` advances `0.6` tiles per physics tick during normal dashes (`0.96` tiles per tick during `1.6x` `VectorLauncher` Phase Launch).
- **Velocity Tier Mapping**: `Assets/Scripts/Gameplay/VelocityTier.cs` (`VelocityTierUtil.FromTiles`) maps `1–2` tiles → `Drift`, `3–4` tiles → `Cruise`, and `5+` tiles → `KineticRam` (whereas earlier design docs reference `1–3` / `4–7` / `8+`). All upgraded feedback hooks subscribe directly to `OnVelocityTierChanged` and `OnTileCrossed(int)`, so any tier-threshold rebalancing propagates automatically.
- **Camera, Audio & Post-Processing State**: `Assets/Data/Sound/SoundConfig.asset` currently has empty `clips: []` entries. `Main Camera` uses `PixelPerfectCamera` (`320×180`, `32 PPU`, `m_GridSnapping = 1`) with `CinemachineBrain.IgnoreTimeScale = 0`, paired on `CinemachineCamera` with `CinemachinePixelPerfect` (`m_Enabled = 1`) and `CinemachinePositionComposer` (`Damping = (1, 1, 1)`, `Lookahead.Enabled = 0`). `Global Volume Profile.asset` statically applies `ChromaticAberration` (`intensity = 0.2`), `Bloom` (`intensity = 9`), and `Vignette` (`intensity = 0.4`).
- **Zero-Procedural-Hierarchy & DOTween Discipline**: Every visual element uses Inspector-assigned child objects (explicitly separated from `Collider2D` roots), `MaterialPropertyBlock` shader parameters, or pooled prefabs via `VfxSpawner.Spawn(...)` (`ObjectPoolManager` / `ParticleSpawner` / `PrefabAnimationSpawner`). Every DOTween tween/sequence calls `.DOKill()` before creation (restoring cached base scale/position before relative punch/shake tweens), `.SetUpdate(true)` so motion continues smoothly during `Time.timeScale = 0` Hit-Stop, and `.SetLink(gameObject)`.

## 1. Core Game Feel Diagnosis: Why Gravita Currently Feels Flat

Gravita already owns hit-stop, squash-and-stretch, trails, impulse shake, haptics, and a close-call scanner—yet feels muted in play because **four coupled timing, ownership, and hierarchy bottlenecks neutralize its kinetic payoff**:

1. **Hit-Stop Misses Squash Peaks & Freezes Camera Recoil**:
   - `PlayerImpactFeedback.PulseHitStop` drops `Time.timeScale = 0` for `0.033s` (`Cruise`) and `0.083s` (`KineticRam`). Although `PlayerSquashStretch.Play()` and `ResetScale()` already call `.SetUpdate(true)` and `.SetLink(gameObject)`, `GameScene.unity` sets `squashDuration = 0.22` (so its `0.4f` press-in phase takes `0.088s`, longer than the entire `0.033s`–`0.083s` freeze) and clamps `maxDeformation = 0.3` (truncating the `16/28 = -42.9%` pixel crush). Meanwhile, `Main Camera`'s `CinemachineBrain` has `IgnoreTimeScale = 0`, freezing camera shake recoil until Hit-Stop ends.
2. **Pixel-Lock Camera Rig Neutralizes `CinemachineCameraZoom2D` & Lags Behind `30 u/s` Motion**:
   - `CinemachineCameraZoom2D` (`normalOrthographicSize = 5.5`, `zoomSpeed = 4`) has **zero callers**, lerps with scaled `Time.deltaTime`, and sits beneath enabled `CinemachinePixelPerfect`, which overwrites `Lens.OrthographicSize` every frame. Simultaneously, `CinemachinePositionComposer` uses `Damping = (1, 1, 1)` with `Lookahead.Enabled = 0`, making the camera trail behind sudden `30 u/s` dashes, while `PlayerImpactFeedback` sets `tier1Shake = 0` and `redirectShake = 0`.
3. **Silent Mid-Slide Escalation, Vanishing Deformation & Bottom-Pivot Sprite Drift**:
   - `PlayerMovement.OnTileCrossed` has **0 subscribers**, and `PlayerMovement` invokes `OnWallImpact` *before* `StopMoving(true)` updates `LastImpactTier = CurrentTier` (creating a one-dash-late trap if post-impact systems read `LastImpactTier` during `OnWallImpact`).
   - `PlayerSquashStretch.HandleMoveStarted` fires an immediate `0.18s` `Stretch()` snap-back toward `baseScale`, fighting `HandleTierChanged`'s transient stretch pulse and returning Subject G-0 to idle proportions mid-dash. Because Subject G-0's `Sprite` child (`Player Icon.aseprite`, `32×32` at `32 PPU` = `1.0 × 1.0` u, bottom-center `(0.5, 0)` pivot) sits at `localPosition = (0, -0.5, 0)`, uncompensated vertical compression shrinks away from ceilings (`dir.y > 0`) and shifts horizontally stretched frames off the corridor centerline. Additionally, `PlayerLineTrail.RefreshTierVisuals()` unconditionally overwrites `lineRenderer.widthMultiplier` and `LateUpdate()` exits early when `Time.deltaTime <= 0f`.
4. **Disconnected Signature Loops, Single-Root Prefab Bottlenecks & Static Post-Processing**:
   - `CloseCallDetector.OnCloseCallTriggered` has **0 subscribers** (and `GameHaptics.EventCount = 7` has no `CloseCall` slot or cooldown entry).
   - `Brake.prefab`, `Exit.prefab`, `Hazard.prefab`, and `Wedge.prefab` have empty `InteractionEffectPlayer` lists; `VectorLauncher.prefab` independently owns `defaultCameraShake = 0.1` alongside `GameplayHapticsFeedback`'s `LaunchStart` call; and `Wedge.prefab`, `CircuitSwitch.prefab`, `PulseLaser.prefab`, `OverloadedFloor.prefab`, plus `FailingTile.prefab` keep `SpriteRenderer` and `BoxCollider2D` on the same root GameObject with no `Visual` child to deform safely.
   - `Global Volume Profile.asset` applies permanent `ChromaticAberration` (`intensity = 0.2`) at idle, blurring crisp pixel art at rest while starving kinetic bursts of dynamic contrast.

## 2. Five High-Impact, Gravita-Specific Game Feel Pillars

### Pillar 1: "Chambered Turn" Diegetic Input Lock & Anchor Contrast

#### Why It Makes Gravita Uniquely Addictive
With `bufferTurnsUntilStop = true`, skilled play depends on **swiping early mid-corridor** so the perpendicular dash triggers cleanly on impact. Currently queuing `pendingDirection` gives zero sensory confirmation, and halting on `GravityBlock` or `BrakeTile` (`MovementState.AwaitingFreshInput`) looks visually identical to normal wall rest. Turning buffered inputs into a *chambered mechanical latch*—and `GravityBlock`/`BrakeTile` anchors into a *centripetal magnetic clamp*—makes high-speed corner routing crisp and rhythmic.

#### Explicit Trade-Off
- **Choice**: Render a compact **on-body 1-tile directional chevron** (`0.56` units ahead of Subject G-0) + relay click rather than projecting a multi-tile dotted landing trajectory laser.
- **What this gives up**: Players cannot preview downstream corridor landing cells from mid-slide inputs.
- **Why for Gravita**: On a `320×180` screen where maze pathfinding is the puzzle, full path previews clutter tight labs and auto-solve routing; a local 1-tile latch confirms swipe registration without spoiling navigation.

### Pillar 2: Harmonic Runway Cadence & Sustained Kinetic Posture ("The 30-Tile/s Accelerando")

#### Why It Makes Gravita Uniquely Addictive
At `30 u/s`, `Drift` (`1–2` tiles = `33–67 ms`), `Cruise` (`3–4` tiles = `100–133 ms`), and `KineticRam` (`5+` tiles = `167 ms+`) pass too quickly for eyes to count cells consciously. Subscribing `OnTileCrossed` to an **ascending pentatonic grid click**, locking Subject G-0 into a **sustained tier-scaled flight silhouette**, and emitting **cadence-locked afterimages** at `KineticRam` turns every corridor into a tangible rhythmic speedometer.

#### Explicit Trade-Off
- **Choice**: Quantize tile-cross ticks to a **10-step ascending harmonic array** (clamped at tile 10), bypass `SoundManager`'s time cooldown strictly for grid-gated `OnTileCrossed` ticks (while muting ticks during `IsPhasing`), and reserve afterimages for `KineticRam` / `Phasing`, rather than running continuous synth pitch sweeps or spawning ghosts during `Drift`.
- **What this gives up**: No continuous Doppler hum on 20-tile wedge chains, and no trail ghosts on short 1–2 tile hops.
- **Why for Gravita**: Unclamped climbs turn shrill over chained wedge runs, and spawning ghosts during `Drift` blurs the life-or-death readability separating safe `Drift` stops from wall-smashing `KineticRam` state.

### Pillar 3: Alive Hit-Stop, Exact Bottom-Pivot Wall Compression & Pixel-Safe Camera Kick

#### Why It Makes Gravita Uniquely Addictive
In a stop-to-stop maze game, **colliding with a wall is the rhythmic downbeat**. Right now `PlayerSquashStretch` takes too long (`0.088s` press-in) to reach peak compression before `0.033s`–`0.083s` Hit-Stop ends, while `CinemachineBrain` halts camera recoil during `Time.timeScale = 0`. Aligning compression peaks *inside* unscaled Hit-Stop, anchoring ceiling impacts flush at `+0.5`, driving dynamic URP spikes with real-frame tier evaluation, and pairing them with crisp look-ahead recoil separates casual `Drift` stops from seismic `KineticRam` slams.

#### Explicit Trade-Off
- **Choice (Recommended): Keep `CinemachinePixelPerfect` enabled on `CinemachineCamera` (`32 PPU`) and express speed and impact weight through `CinemachinePositionComposer.TargetOffset` directional look-ahead + unscaled impact recoil + URP post-process spikes**, rather than enabling continuous `CinemachineCameraZoom2D` ortho-size shifts.
- **What this gives up**: Smooth lens zoom-in (`5.5 → 5.18`) during high-speed corridors.
- **Why for Gravita**: With `PixelPerfectCamera` (`320×180`, `m_GridSnapping = 1`) and `CinemachinePixelPerfect` active in `GameScene.unity`, arbitrary `OrthographicSize` changes either get overwritten by `CinemachinePixelPerfect` or force non-integer pixel scaling—producing tilemap seam shimmer and yo-yo camera jitter on `0.15s` dashes.
- *(Optional Zoom Alternative: Disable `CinemachinePixelPerfect` on `CinemachineCamera`, change `CinemachineCameraZoom2D.Update()` to use `Time.unscaledDeltaTime` with `zoomSpeed = 14f`, and pulse `SetTargetOrthographicSize(5.18f)` for `0.09s` strictly on `CrackedBlock.OnAnyBroken` or `5.80f` during `IsPhasing`, returning via `SetNormalOrthographicSize()`—explicitly accepting transient non-integer pixel scaling during those peaks.)*

### Pillar 4: Bisector Pinball Ricochet, Single-Owner Phase Launch & Safe Tile Feedback

#### Why It Makes Gravita Uniquely Addictive

Isolating Wedge/Pad pitch chains and Phase Launch lifecycle events cleanly—without mixing launch starts into wedge redirects, double-shaking prefabs, or detonating Sonic Booms on death/restart—makes every corner snap and wall breach punchy and bug-free.

#### Explicit Trade-Off
- **Choice**: Maintain **0 ms Hit-Stop** on `DeflectionWedge`/`VectorPad` redirects (communicating speed through bisector spark alignment, rising harmonic chain pitch, and `0.04f` micro-kick) and reserve Hit-Stop strictly for `VectorLauncher` re-entry (`OnPhaseLaunchArrived`), rather than pausing per wedge corner or reusing `OnPhasingChanged` for one-shot impact FX.
- **What this gives up**: Heavy freeze-frame weight on individual wedge corners.
- **Why for Gravita**: With `maxConsecutiveRedirects = 8` at `30 u/s`, even `25 ms` of Hit-Stop per corner injects `100+ ms` of rhythm-breaking stutter into rapid 4-wedge chains; meanwhile `OnPhasingChanged(false)` fires on hazard death (`Freeze()`), `TeleportTo()`, and restarts (`ResetDirectionHistory()`), so separating continuous phase state (`OnPhasingChanged`) from true launch/arrival events (`OnPhaseLaunchStarted` / `OnPhaseLaunchArrived`) guarantees zero phantom booms.
