# Gravita (Puzzle 2D) — Kinetic Game Feel Upgrade Blueprint

## Baseline Architecture & Constraints
- **Velocity Tier Thresholds**: `Assets/Scripts/Gameplay/VelocityTier.cs` (`VelocityTierUtil.FromTiles`) currently maps `1–2` tiles to `Drift`, `3–4` to `Cruise`, and `5+` to `KineticRam` (design notes also discuss `1–3 / 4–7 / 8+`). Every feedback hook below subscribes to `PlayerMovement.CurrentTier`, `PlayerMovement.OnVelocityTierChanged`, `PlayerMovement.LastImpactTier`, and `PlayerMovement.OnTileCrossed`, so threshold tuning requires zero feedback-script rewrites.
- **Physics, Camera & Art Baseline (`Assets/Scenes/GameScene.unity`)**: `PlayerMovement` runs at `moveSpeed = 30` units/sec (`33.33 ms/tile`), `Phase Launch` (`1.6x`) runs at `48` units/sec (`20.83 ms/tile`), `bufferTurnsUntilStop = 1`, `32 PPU` pixel art (`1 px = 0.03125` world units), `CinemachineCamera.Lens.OrthographicSize = 5.5`, `CinemachinePositionComposer.Damping = (1, 1, 1)`, `Lookahead.Enabled = 0`, and both `PixelPerfectCamera` (on `Main Camera`) and `CinemachinePixelPerfect` (on `CinemachineCamera`) are currently enabled.
- **Strict Engineering Rules**: All additions obey SRP presentation-layer event observation (`Assets/Scripts/Feedback/` & `Assets/Scripts/Audio/`), never alter `PlayerMovement.FixedUpdate` / `SimulateStep` cell physics, never use procedural `new GameObject()` or runtime `AddComponent<T>()` for UI, VFX, or managers, use pre-configured pooled prefabs (`VfxSpawner.Spawn` / `ObjectPoolManager.SpawnObject`) with `[SerializeField]` Inspector wiring, and enforce `.DOKill()`, `.SetUpdate(true)`, `.SetLink(gameObject)` on every DOTween animation.

## 1. Core Game Feel Diagnosis: Why Gravita Currently Feels Flat

Despite having basic camera shake, squash/stretch, and a trail renderer, Gravita loses its kinetic punch across five codebase-level bottlenecks:

1. **Hit-Stop Freezes the Visual Impact It Should Accentuate (`33–83 ms` Deadzone)**
   At `moveSpeed = 30 u/s`, a `1–5` tile dash finishes in `33–167 ms`. `PlayerImpactFeedback.PulseHitStop` sets `Time.timeScale = 0f` for `tier2HitStop = 0.033s` and `tier3HitStop = 0.083s`, which freezes:
   - `Assets/Prefabs/Particles/WallImpactBurst.prefab` and `WallImpactBreak.prefab` — both have `useUnscaledTime: 0` on their `ParticleSystem`, pausing impact sparks motionless at frame 1 until Hit-Stop ends.
   - `Assets/Scripts/Feedback/PlayerLineTrail.cs` — `LateUpdate()` exits early on `float dt = Time.deltaTime; if (dt <= 0f) return;` (line 253), freezing the trail ribbon behind the impacted sprite, while line 292 clamps `smoothedMoveSpeed` to `[4f, 28f]` below `30` (`48` during Phase).
   - `Assets/Scripts/Feedback/CinemachineCameraZoom2D.cs` — interpolates using `Time.deltaTime` instead of `Time.unscaledDeltaTime`.

2. **Flat Low-End Impact & Bottom-Center Pivot / Mid-Flight Reset Bugs in `PlayerSquashStretch`**
   - In `GameScene.unity`, `PlayerImpactFeedback` sets `tier1Shake = 0` and `redirectShake = 0`, making `Drift` steps and `DeflectionWedge` rebounds weightless.
   - `PlayerSquashStretch` uses `.SetUpdate(true)` and `.SetLink(gameObject)`, **but**:
     - `GameScene.unity` overrides `maxDeformation = 0.3`, clipping the authored `impactHeightPx = 16 / movingHeightPx = 28` pancake (`-42.9%`) down to `-30%`.
     - The `Sprite` child sits at `localPosition = (0, -0.5, 0)` (bottom-center `(0.5, 0)` pivot). On `Vector2.up` ceiling impacts, shrinking `localScale.y` pulls the top edge down away from the wall (`-0.30` units while `wallPressPx = 4` shifts only `+0.125` units), leaving a visible `~5.6 px` air gap against the ceiling.
     - `HandleMoveStarted` and `HandleTierChanged` run transient `Play(...)` pulses (`stretchDuration = 0.18s`) that relax back to `baseScale` mid-slide instead of sustaining an aerodynamic needle posture until `OnMoveStopped`.

3. **Dead Corners, Layered Launch SFX, and Missing Prefab Effects**
   - `PlayerMovement.Redirect()` (`Assets/Scripts/Gameplay/PlayerMovement.cs:997-998`) emits **both** `OnRedirected` and `OnMoveStarted` (necessary because `LevelFlowController` counts `movesUsedThisRun++` on `OnMoveStarted`), causing `EntityAudio` to layer `moveStart` on top of `wedgeRedirect` instead of letting chained wedges escalate cleanly.
   - While `GravityBlock.prefab` already configures `InteractionEffectPlayer` (`1` effect + `defaultCameraShake = 0.04`), `Wedge.prefab`, `Brake.prefab`, `Exit.prefab`, and `Hazard.prefab` have completely empty `InteractionEffectPlayer` arrays (`effectPrefabs: []`, `defaultCameraShake: 0`), leaving corner rebounds and brake stops visually quiet.

4. **Dormant & Locked-Down Signature Systems (`CinemachinePixelPerfect`, `CloseCallDetector`, Shaders, Global Volume)**
   - `CinemachineCameraZoom2D` is attached to `CinemachineCamera` and **never called anywhere in code**; moreover, `Main Camera` enables `PixelPerfectCamera` (`32 PPU`, `320x180`, `m_UpscaleRT: 0`) and `CinemachineCamera` enables `CinemachinePixelPerfect`, which quantizes every fractional orthographic target in `(4.821, 6.428)` across `720p/1080p/1440p` to a locked `5.625f`! `CinemachinePositionComposer` also uses uniform `Damping = (1, 1, 1)` with `Lookahead.Enabled = 0`, trailing stiffly behind `30 u/s` bursts.
   - `CloseCallDetector.OnCloseCallTriggered` (`Action<Vector3>`) has **0 subscribers**, `closeCallVfxPrefab` reuses generic `Particle Star.prefab`, and `bonusCoins` never executes because `EconomyBalanceConfig.Active.EnableLegacyExtraRewardSystems` is `false`.
   - `Assets/Shaders/Hit/HitFlashSprite.shader` (`Hit Flash White.mat`) and `Assets/Volume/Global Volume Profile.asset` (`Bloom`, `ChromaticAberration`, `Vignette`) stay static regardless of kinetic energy.

5. **Silent Audio Catalog, Single-Voice Audio Pool, and Unscaled Wall Haptics**
   - `Assets/Data/Sound/SoundConfig.asset` currently has **`clips: []` across all 24 entries**, even though ready-to-use sci-fi packs (`Assets/Audio/Sounds/kenney_sci-fi-sounds/`, `Assets/Audio/Sounds/sci-fi-sfx/`) already exist in the repository.
   - `AudioSystems.prefab` has `pooledAudioSourcePrefab: {fileID: 0}` and `m_Children: []` (and `GameScene.unity` clears `sfxPool[0]`), leaving `SoundManager` with a single shared `AudioSource` (and `MusicManager.musicSource` unassigned), meaning overlapping pitched SFX would bend active voices mid-playback.
   - `EntityAudio` does not listen to `OnTileCrossed`, `OnVelocityTierChanged`, `OnPhasingChanged`, `CloseCallDetector.OnCloseCallTriggered`, `MazeTile.OnAnyPlayerInteracted` (`Switch` / `Brake`), `HazardActivation.OnPhaseChanged`, or `FailingTile.OnStateChanged`, and `EnergyGate` lacks a static broadcast matching `CrackedBlock.OnAnyBroken`.
   - `GameplayHapticsFeedback` treats `Drift` taps and `KineticRam` wall slams identically (`LightImpact`) and ignores `CloseCall` and `CrackedBlock.OnAnyBroken` (while `LevelFlowController` already handles `HapticEvent.Death` directly).

## 2. Six High-Impact Game Feel Pillars for Gravita

### Pillar 1: Unscaled Kinetic Impact Rig, Pivot-Locked Squash & Two-Frame Breach Snap
* **Psychology & Rhythmic Feel**: Stop-to-stop inertia lives on crisp launch-slide-slam punctuation. Synchronizing sparks, pivot-locked sprite compression, directional recoil, and a localized material flash during the `33–83 ms` Hit-Stop turns walls from weightless blockers into solid lab bulkheads—making `KineticRam` slams and `CrackedBlock` shatters feel explosively physical without delaying buffered swipes.
* **Exact Specs & Inspector Parameters**:
  * **Particle Unscaled Sync**: Set `useUnscaledTime = true` on `WallImpactBurst.prefab` and `WallImpactBreak.prefab` so impact bursts explode across frames `0–3` *during* the `Time.timeScale = 0` freeze.
  * **`PlayerImpactFeedback` Scene Tuning & Guarded Stops**:
    * `tier1Shake = 0.045f` (`Drift` tap, up from `0`), `tier2Shake = 0.12f` (`Cruise`), `tier3Shake = 0.24f` (`KineticRam`), `redirectShake = 0.035f` (up from `0`).
    * **Strict Wall-Only Guard**: Guard `PlayerImpactFeedback.HandleStopped()` with `if (!movement.WasLastStopWallImpact) return;` and do **not** add brake shake inside `PlayerImpactFeedback`. Because `Freeze()` (death/victory), `TeleportTo()` (respawn/revive), `HaltAt()` (`BrakeTile`/`GravityBlock`), and zero-post-direction Phase landings all pass `wallImpact: false`, this guard eliminates false shakes/hit-stops on death, victory, and respawn while leaving anchor shakes solely on `GravityBlock.prefab` (`0.04`) and `Brake.prefab` (`0.025`).
    * **Directional Impulse**: Replace omnidirectional shake with `Vector2 impulseDir = -movement.LastMoveDirection` weighted by `(1f, 0.25f)` orthogonal jitter so hitting a ceiling wall punches the camera downward/rebound along physical momentum. Scale by GDD §12.3 `traumaScale` (`0f..1f`).
  * **Sustained Flight Silhouette & Two-Axis Pivot Fix (`PlayerSquashStretch`)**:
    * Raise `maxDeformation` in `GameScene.unity` from `0.3` to `0.48` so `impactHeightPx = 16 / movingHeightPx = 28` (`-0.429`) executes fully.
    * **Pivot Compensation (`localPosition = (0, -0.5, 0)`)**: compute `spriteLocalSize = spriteRenderer.sprite.bounds.size` and `pivotNorm = spriteRenderer.sprite.pivot / Vector2.Max(Vector2.one, spriteRenderer.sprite.rect.size)`. When compressing into a wall along `pressDirection`:
      - If `pressDirection.y > 0.5f` (ceiling): `pressed.y += (baseScale.y - deformed.y) * spriteLocalSize.y * (1f - pivotNorm.y)`.
      - If `pressDirection.y < -0.5f` (floor): `pressed.y -= (baseScale.y - deformed.y) * spriteLocalSize.y * pivotNorm.y`.
      - Symmetrically on X (`(1f - pivotNorm.x)` for `+X`, `pivotNorm.x` for `-X`).
      Every wall direction stays flush against the bulkhead plus `wallPressPx = 4` (`0.125` u) instead of pulling back into mid-air.
    * **Hold Flight Form Until Stop**: `HandleMoveStarted` deforms into `movingWidthPx / movingHeightPx` over `0.055s` (`Ease.OutQuad`, unscaled) and **holds** that stretched needle posture (`Cruise` adds `+0.04` axial / `-0.02` lateral; `KineticRam` adds `+0.08` axial / `-0.05` lateral) until `OnMoveStopped` triggers the `28x16` pancake slam (`0.045s` compress + `0.15s` `Ease.OutBack` rebound to `baseScale`). On `OnRedirected`, snap the flight stretch to `newDirection` and suppress the paired `OnMoveStarted` emitted immediately after by `PlayerMovement.Redirect()` so corners never reset tier elongation.
  * **Two-Frame Breach Flash & Ring (`HitFlashSprite.shader` + `MaterialPropertyBlock`)**:
    * Swap `playerSpriteRenderer.sharedMaterial` temporarily to `Assets/Shaders/Hit/Hit Flash White.mat` and push `_FlashColor` through a pre-allocated `MaterialPropertyBlock` (never mutating the `.mat` asset, never allocating `new Material`, and never clobbering `PlayerCosmeticApplier` tint):
      - `Cruise` wall hit: `#B8F3FFFF` for `0.033s` unscaled.
      - `KineticRam` wall hit or `CrackedBlock.OnAnyBroken`: `#FF70D4FF` (`0.030s`) -> `#FFFFFFFF` (`0.030s`), then `SetPropertyBlock(null)` and restore cached base material (`OnDisable` also restores cleanly).
    * Spawn pooled `KineticRamRing.prefab` (`Glow VFX.mat`, `Scale 0.35 -> 2.6` over `0.18s` unscaled, alpha `0.9 -> 0`, `#FF52C8`). *(GDD §12.4 Photosensitivity Guard: localized sprite flash + expanding ring only—zero full-screen strobe.)*
* **Concrete C# Integration Hooks**:
  * `CinemachineCameraShake2D`: add `public void ShakeDirectional(Vector2 direction, float force)` calling `impulseSource?.GenerateImpulse((Vector3)(direction.normalized * force))`.
  * `PlayerImpactFeedback`: guard `HandleStopped()` with `if (!movement.WasLastStopWallImpact) return;`; call `ShakeDirectional(-movement.LastMoveDirection, shake)`; drive unscaled `MaterialPropertyBlock` flash via `[SerializeField] private Material hitFlashMaterial` + `[SerializeField] private SpriteRenderer playerSpriteRenderer`; spawn `[SerializeField] private GameObject kineticRamRingPrefab` via `VfxSpawner.Spawn`.
  * `PlayerSquashStretch`: subscribe to `movement.OnRedirected += HandleRedirected`, use `suppressNextMoveStart` across `Redirect()`, split `HoldFlightDeform(...)` vs `ImpactSquashAndRecover(...)`, and apply two-axis pivot compensation (all sequences use `.DOKill()`, `.SetUpdate(true)`, `.SetLink(gameObject)`).
* **Inspector / Prefab Wiring**:
  * Toggle `Main -> Use Unscaled Time = true` on `WallImpactBurst.prefab` and `WallImpactBreak.prefab`.
  * Create `Assets/Prefabs/VFX/KineticRamRing.prefab` with a `ParticleSystem` (`Glow VFX.mat`, `useUnscaledTime = true`) **and** an authored `PooledParticlePlayer` (`playOnSpawn = true`) so `VfxSpawner.Spawn` never calls runtime `AddComponent`.
  * On `Player` in `GameScene.unity`: set `PlayerSquashStretch.maxDeformation = 0.48`; assign `hitFlashMaterial` (`Assets/Shaders/Hit/Hit Flash White.mat`), `playerSpriteRenderer` (`Sprite`), and `kineticRamRingPrefab` on `PlayerImpactFeedback`.

### Pillar 2: Bank-Shot Chain Resonance & Afterimage Runway
* **Psychology & Rhythmic Feel**: Gravita's defining puzzle twist is that `DeflectionWedge` and `VectorPad` preserve `TilesTraveled` across 90-degree turns. Making chained corners visually trace like an oscillating circuit—holding the peak-tier trail briefly on stop, stamping kinetic afterimages per tile, and popping tier-up flares—turns a multi-bounce `KineticRam` line into the game's signature rush.
* **Exact Specs & Inspector Parameters**:
  * **`PlayerLineTrail` Hit-Stop, Color-Lock & Buffered-Re-Launch Fixes (`Assets/Scripts/Feedback/PlayerLineTrail.cs`)**:
    * Switch `LateUpdate()` from `Time.deltaTime` to `Time.unscaledDeltaTime`, and raise speed clamp `[4f, 28f]` -> `[4f, 52f]` to match `30 u/s` (`Drift/Cruise/Ram`) and `48 u/s` (`Phase`).
    * **Preserve Peak Tier Color Through `StopMoving` (`postStopFadeDuration = 0.24f`)**: `PlayerMovement.StopMoving()` invokes `ResetDashTracking()` -> `OnVelocityTierChanged(VelocityTier.None)` *before* `LateUpdate()` runs. Guard `RefreshTierVisuals()` so when `tier == VelocityTier.None && !phasing` and `waypoints.Count > 0`, it **skips** overwriting colors—keeping the dash's peak `Cruise` (`#FFD940`), `KineticRam` (`#FF52C8`), or `Phase` (`#B86BFF`) colors intact for the fade!
    * **Unscaled Hold + Clean Buffered Cancellation**: When `wasMovingLastFrame && !isActivelyMoving` and `waypoints.Count > 0`, set `isPostStopFading = true`, pause `UpdateTrailShortening`, and fade `LineRenderer` alpha (`1.0 -> 0.0` over `0.24s` via `DOTween.To`, `Ease.OutQuad`, `.DOKill()`, `.SetUpdate(true)`, `.SetLink(gameObject)`), clearing `waypoints`, resetting `isPostStopFading = false`, `alpha = 1f`, and restoring Drift base colors on completion. If `OnMoveStarted` (or `!wasMovingLastFrame && isActivelyMoving` in `LateUpdate()`) triggers while `isPostStopFading == true` (such as a buffered perpendicular turn launching right after Hit-Stop), **immediately** kill the fade tween, reset `isPostStopFading = false`, `alpha = 1f`, restore Drift `RefreshTierVisuals()`, and call `ResetAt(GetStableSamplePosition())` so the new dash never draws into fading stale waypoints or fades to `0`. (When `isPostStopFading == false` during active mid-slide `Redirect()` / `StepPhaseLaunch()` `OnMoveStarted` emissions, keep `waypoints` intact so multi-wedge trajectories stay connected.)
  * **Tier-Driven Pooled Afterimages (`KineticRunwayFeedback`)**:
    * Deterministic trigger on `PlayerMovement.OnTileCrossed(int tiles)` (never frame-rate dependent):

      * `VelocityTier.KineticRam`: spawn every `1` tile (`33.3 ms`), start color `#FF52C8` (`alpha 0.62`), fade `0.22s` unscaled, scale `1.04 -> 0.85`.
      * `IsPhasing == true`: spawn every `1` tile (`20.8 ms`), start color `#B86BFF` (`alpha 0.68`), fade `0.24s` unscaled, scale `1.12 -> 0.80`.
  * **Tier-Shift Ignition Pop & Empty Prefab Effects**:
    * On `OnVelocityTierChanged(Cruise / KineticRam)`: spawn pooled `TierIgnitionFlare.prefab` at `Player` center rotated to `-CurrentDirection` (`#FFE066` for Cruise, `#FF52C8` for KineticRam) + a `0.08s` unscaled scale pop (`1.18x`) on `PlayerSquashStretch`.
    * Populate empty `InteractionEffectPlayer` prefabs (leaving `GravityBlock.prefab`'s already-wired `defaultCameraShake = 0.04` intact):
      * `Wedge.prefab`: `PoppingVFX.prefab` (or `WedgeDeflectSpark.prefab` with `PooledParticlePlayer`, `alignToInteractionDirection = true`, `defaultCameraShake = 0.04`).
      * `Brake.prefab`: inward `BrakeAnchorRing.prefab` (`PooledParticlePlayer`, `defaultCameraShake = 0.025`).

* **Concrete C# Integration Hooks**:

  * `KineticRunwayFeedback` (Layer-4 MonoBehaviour on `Player`): subscribes to `OnTileCrossed`, `OnVelocityTierChanged`, `OnPhasingChanged`, `OnRedirected`, `OnMoveStopped`. ### Pillar 3: Kinetic Camera Breathing & URP Optical Speed Tunnel
* **Psychology & Rhythmic Feel**: At `30 u/s`, `CinemachinePositionComposer` with `Damping = (1, 1, 1)` lags behind dashes while locked `5.625f` ortho framing hides distant walls and traps. Freeing smooth fractional orthographic zoom, decoupling base tier framing from impact punches, adding directional lookahead, and pulsing URP `ChromaticAberration` / `Bloom` turns long dashes into an addictive sensation of widening tunnel momentum followed by a tight compression snap on wall impact.
* **Exact Specs & Inspector Parameters**:
  * **Unlocking Smooth Ortho Zoom (`PixelPerfectCamera` & `CinemachinePixelPerfect` Fix)**:
    * Disable **both** `CinemachinePixelPerfect` on `CinemachineCamera` **and** `PixelPerfectCamera` on `Main Camera` in `GameScene.unity`. Disabling only `CinemachinePixelPerfect` leaves `PixelPerfectCamera.m_CinemachineCompatibilityMode == false`, causing `PixelPerfectCamera.OnBeginCameraRendering` to overwrite `Main Camera.orthographicSize = m_Internal.orthoSize` (`5.625f` at `720p/1080p/1440p`), whereas disabling `CinemachinePixelPerfect` alone or leaving both enabled quantizes every zoom target in `(4.821, 6.428)` to `5.625f`. (Optionally expose a Settings toggle in `KineticCameraRig` for players wanting fixed integer pixel snapping with dynamic zoom disabled.)
  * **Single-Writer `CinemachineCameraZoom2D` (`normalOrthographicSize = 5.5f`)**:
    * Refactor `CinemachineCameraZoom2D` so `Update()` is the **sole writer** to `cinemachineCamera.Lens.OrthographicSize` using `Time.unscaledDeltaTime`:
      - `smoothedBaseOrthoSize = Mathf.Lerp(smoothedBaseOrthoSize, baseTargetOrthoSize, Time.unscaledDeltaTime * zoomSpeed);`
      - `cinemachineCamera.Lens.OrthographicSize = Mathf.Max(2f, smoothedBaseOrthoSize + punchOffset);`
    * Expose `SetBaseTargetSize(float size)` (or `SetTargetOrthographicSize(float)`), `SetZoomSpeed(float)`, and `PulseOrthoPunch(float punchDelta, float inDuration, float outDuration)` which animates **only** `punchOffset` (`0 -> punchDelta` over `inDuration`, `-> 0` over `outDuration` `Ease.OutCubic`) via `DOTween.To` (`.DOKill()`, `.SetUpdate(true)`, `.SetLink(gameObject)`). Because `punchOffset` is additive onto `smoothedBaseOrthoSize`, `Update()` never fights `PulseOrthoPunch`, and `OnVelocityTierChanged(None)` resetting `baseTargetOrthoSize = 5.50f` on the same frame as `OnWallImpact` never cancels the inward snap punch!
    * **Tier Targets**: `None / Drift = 5.50f`, `Cruise = 5.72f` (`+0.22` u), `KineticRam = 6.00f` (`+0.50` u), `IsPhasing = 6.15f` (`+0.65` u). (`zoomOutLerp = 5.5f`, `recoverLerp = 8.5f`).
    * **Impact & Mid-Ram Breach Punches (`PulseOrthoPunch`)**:
      - On `OnWallImpact`: read `VelocityTier tier = movement.CurrentTier != VelocityTier.None ? movement.CurrentTier : movement.LastImpactTier;` (`Cruise`: `-0.14f`, `0.04s` in / `0.18s` out; `KineticRam`: `-0.28f`, `0.045s` in / `0.18s` out).
      - On `CrackedBlock.OnAnyBroken`: call **only** `PulseOrthoPunch(-0.30f, 0.04f, 0.16f)` without altering `baseTargetOrthoSize`, so smashing a block mid-dash keeps `KineticRam` smoothly framed at `6.00f` as Subject G-0 keeps sliding!
  * **Directional Velocity Lookahead (`KineticCameraRig`)**:
    * Lower `CinemachinePositionComposer.Damping` from `(1, 1, 1)` to `(0.35, 0.35, 1)` so camera stops crisply on wall grid boundaries.
    * Smoothly drive `CinemachinePositionComposer.TargetOffset` (`Time.unscaledDeltaTime * 9f` during slide, `12f` return on stop) ahead of `PlayerMovement.CurrentDirection`: `Drift = 0.45f`, `Cruise = 1.05f`, `KineticRam = 1.65f` units.
  * **Dynamic URP `Global Volume Profile` Modulation**:
    * Baseline values from `Assets/Volume/Global Volume Profile.asset`: `Bloom.intensity = 9.0f`, `ChromaticAberration.intensity = 0.20f`, `Vignette.intensity = 0.40f` (restored cleanly in `OnDisable`).
    * During `KineticRam`: `ChromaticAberration` ramps to `0.42f` (`0.08s` unscaled), `Bloom.intensity` rises `+2.2f` (`11.2f`), `Vignette.intensity` tightens to `0.48f`.
    * On `KineticRam` slam / `CrackedBlock` smash: spike `ChromaticAberration` to `0.62f` and decay back to `0.20f` across `0.16s` (`Ease.OutExpo`, unscaled), guarded by a serialized `chromaticAberrationEnabled` flag (GDD §12.3).
* **Concrete C# Integration Hooks**:
  * `CinemachineCameraZoom2D`: switch `Time.deltaTime` -> `Time.unscaledDeltaTime`; separate `smoothedBaseOrthoSize` from `punchOffset`; expose `SetZoomSpeed(float)` and `PulseOrthoPunch(float punchDelta, float inDuration, float outDuration)`.
  * `KineticCameraRig` (Layer-4 MonoBehaviour on `CinemachineCamera`): `[SerializeField] private PlayerMovement playerMovement`, `[SerializeField] private CinemachineCameraZoom2D cameraZoom`, `[SerializeField] private CinemachinePositionComposer positionComposer`, `[SerializeField] private Volume globalVolume`. Subscribes to `OnMoveStarted`, `OnRedirected`, `OnVelocityTierChanged`, `OnPhasingChanged`, `OnMoveStopped`, `OnWallImpact`, and `CrackedBlock.OnAnyBroken`.
* **Inspector / Prefab Wiring**:
  * In `GameScene.unity`: disable `PixelPerfectCamera` on `Main Camera` and `CinemachinePixelPerfect` on `CinemachineCamera`; set `CinemachinePositionComposer.Damping = (0.35, 0.35, 1)`.
  * Attach `KineticCameraRig` to `CinemachineCamera`; drag `Player` (`PlayerMovement`), `CinemachineCameraZoom2D`, `CinemachinePositionComposer`, and `Global Volume` (`Volume`) in Inspector.

### Pillar 4: "Close-Call Overclock" & Spatial Hazard Telemetry
* **Psychology & Rhythmic Feel**: Grazing a `SentinelDronePatrol` within `1.08` units or crossing a `PulseLaser` within `0.15s` of ignition is peak skill expression—yet `CloseCallDetector.OnCloseCallTriggered` currently has zero subscribers. Giving near-misses an unmistakable micro-punch, rising streak callout, and audio sting makes players actively *hunt* tight timing windows; giving timed hazards and collapsing floors crisp visual-child pre-ignition ticks makes those windows learnable without ever perturbing colliders.
* **Exact Specs & Inspector Parameters**:
