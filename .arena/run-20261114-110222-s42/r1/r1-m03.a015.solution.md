# Gravita (Puzzle 2D) — Game Feel Signature Upgrade Blueprint

## Assumptions & Codebase Calibration
- **Velocity Tier Source of Truth**: In the current codebase, `VelocityTierUtil.FromTiles` (`Assets/Scripts/Gameplay/VelocityTier.cs`) maps `1–2` tiles to `Drift`, `3–4` to `Cruise`, and `5+` to `KineticRam` (whereas the GDD lists `1–3`, `4–7`, `8+`). All feedback hooks bind directly to the `VelocityTier` enum and `PlayerMovement.CurrentTier` so feedback scales cleanly regardless of threshold tuning.
- **Scale & Timing Baseline**: At `moveSpeed = 30` units/sec and `cellSize = 1` (`GameScene.unity`), Subject G-0 crosses **1 tile every `33.3 ms`** (`~2` frames at 60 FPS), reaching `Cruise` in `100 ms` and `KineticRam` in `166.7 ms` (current thresholds) or `266.7 ms` (GDD thresholds). Envelopes must snap within `20–120 ms` to feel physically attached to input.
- **Audio Assets**: `Assets/Data/Sound/SoundConfig.asset` currently has empty `clips: []` slots across entries; new `SoundEntry` hooks include safe null/empty-clip guards so existing and newly imported clips play cleanly via pitch and volume shaping.

## 1. Core Game Feel Diagnosis: Why Gravita Currently Feels Flat

Gravita already has solid event architecture (`PlayerMovement`, `InteractionEffectPlayer`, `CloseCallDetector`, `CinemachineCameraShake2D`, `CinemachineCameraZoom2D`), yet gameplay feels muted for four specific technical reasons observed in the current project:

1. **Zeroed Low-End Shake, Pixel-Locked Zoom, and Time-Scale Frozen Camera/Impulses**:
   - In `GameScene.unity`, `PlayerImpactFeedback` sets `tier1Shake = 0` and `redirectShake = 0` (`tier2Shake = 0.1`, `tier3Shake = 0.2`), stripping tactile punch from short hops and bank shots, while `CinemachineCameraShake2D.ShakeCamera(float)` emits isotropic impulses rather than directional pulses along the slide vector.
   - `CinemachineCameraZoom2D` (on `CinemachineCamera`, `normalOrthographicSize = 5.5`) is **never called anywhere in code** and lerps with scaled `Time.deltaTime`. Moreover, `CinemachinePixelPerfect` (`m_Enabled: 1` on `CinemachineCamera`) calls `PixelPerfectCamera.CorrectCinemachineOrthoSize` every `LateUpdate`, snapping orthographic sizes (`5.28..6.00`) straight to `5.625` at 1080p/720p (`32 PPU`, `320x180` ref). At the same time, `CinemachineBrain` has `IgnoreTimeScale: 0` and `CinemachineImpulseManager.Instance.IgnoreTimeScale` is `false`, freezing camera motion and impulse shakes throughout `Time.timeScale = 0` Hit-Stop.
2. **Hit-Stop Freezes Trails & Impact Bursts + Bottom-Center Pivot Pulls Away From Ceilings**:
   - While `PlayerSquashStretch.Play()` and `ResetScale()` already run with `.SetUpdate(true).SetLink(gameObject)`, `PlayerLineTrail.LateUpdate()` returns immediately when `Time.deltaTime <= 0f`, and `WallImpactBurst.prefab` / `WallImpactBreak.prefab` have `useUnscaledTime: 0` — freezing both the momentum ribbon and impact sparks during `0.033s`/`0.083s` Hit-Stop. Additionally, `PlayerImpactFeedback.HitStopRoutine` lacks re-entrancy guards and overwrites `Time.timeScale` with a stale `preHitStopTimeScale` without re-checking `UIManager` freeze state upon waking or disabling.
   - Subject G-0's visual `Sprite` child sits at `localPosition = (0, -0.5, 0)` (bottom-center `(0.5, 0)` pivot) with `maxDeformation = 0.3` in `GameScene.unity`, clipping `16/28 - 1 = -0.429` down to `-0.30` and pulling the sprite top downward (`~5.6 px` gap) on North (`Vector2.up`) ceiling impacts instead of pressing flush against the wall face.
3. **Silent Escalation, Event-Stacking Collisions, and Non-Musical Rebounds**:
   - `OnTileCrossed`, `OnVelocityTierChanged`, `OnPhasingChanged`, `OnPendingDirectionChanged`, and `MazeTile.OnAnyPlayerInteracted` have **zero audio subscribers** in `EntityAudio`, while `wedgeRedirect` and `coinCollect` rely on flat random pitch (`±8%`).
   - `PlayerMovement.Redirect()` overwrites `lastMoveDirection` before emitting `OnRedirected` and immediately fires `OnMoveStarted` on the very same frame (killing same-frame squash transitions and stacking `moveStart` over `wedgeRedirect`), while `VectorLauncher.StartPhaseLaunch()` fires `KineticRam`, `SetPhasing(true)`, and `OnRedirected` in one call.
4. **Disconnected High-Drama Signals, Unsplit Tile Prefabs, and Linear Goal Climax**:
   - `CloseCallDetector.OnCloseCallTriggered` has **0 subscribers**; `Global Volume Profile.asset` keeps `ChromaticAberration` (`0.2`), `Vignette` (`0.4`), and `Bloom` (`9`) static.
   - `Brake.prefab`, `Wedge.prefab`, `Brittle.prefab`, `Exit.prefab`, and `Hazard.prefab` have empty `InteractionEffectPlayer` lists; `PulseLaser`, `OverloadedFloor`, `CircuitSwitch`, `EnergyGate`, and `FailingTile` place their `SpriteRenderer` directly on collider roots; and `CutsceneManager.GoalRoutine` drags the player linearly over `0.5s` while fighting `PlayerSquashStretch`'s active stop-squash tween.

## 2. Five Gravita-Specific Game Feel Pillars

Every pillar below preserves deterministic `FixedUpdate` cell-center physics, forbids runtime `new GameObject(...)` or `AddComponent<...>()`, and strictly enforces `.DOKill()`, explicit `.SetUpdate(...)`, and `.SetLink(gameObject)`.

### Pillar 1: Kinetic Runway Camera Breathing, Pixel-Perfect Zoom Gate & Unscaled Hit-Stop
- **Why it makes Gravita uniquely addictive**:
  At `30 u/s`, slides resolve in `100–300 ms`. Pulling the camera subtly wider as kinetic energy builds (`Drift -> Cruise -> KineticRam -> Phasing`), snapping *into* the wall on impact, keeping pixel-perfect snapping clean when at rest, and letting impact sparks and trails stay alive while Subject G-0 freezes flush into the wall turns stop-to-stop motion into a crisp physical spring.
- **Exact numbers, curves, durations & Inspector parameters**:
  - **Pixel-Perfect Rest Baseline & Relative Zoom Offsets (`CinemachineCameraZoom2D`)**:
    - Cache `restOrthoSize = pixelPerfectCamera != null ? pixelPerfectCamera.CorrectCinemachineOrthoSize(normalOrthographicSize) : normalOrthographicSize` (`5.625f` at 1080p and 720p for `normalOrthographicSize = 5.5f`, refreshed when resolution changes; initialize `currentOrthoSize = targetOrthoSize = restOrthoSize`).
    - `Drift`: `restOrthoSize + 0f` (`zoomSpeed = 6f`).
    - `Cruise`: `restOrthoSize + 0.15f` (`zoomSpeed = 7f`).
    - `KineticRam`: `restOrthoSize + 0.35f` (`zoomSpeed = 8f`).
    - `Phasing` (`48 u/s` at `1.6x`): `restOrthoSize + 0.50f` (`zoomSpeed = 12f`).
    - **Wall Impact Snap-In**: `PulseZoom(restOrthoSize - 0.14f, 0.06f)` at `Cruise`; `PulseZoom(restOrthoSize - 0.20f, 0.09f)` unscaled hold at `KineticRam`, relaxing at `zoomSpeed = 10f`.
    - **Pixel-Perfect Handoff**: While `Mathf.Abs(currentOrthoSize - restOrthoSize) > 0.002f || Mathf.Abs(targetOrthoSize - restOrthoSize) > 0.002f`, set `cinemachinePixelPerfect.enabled = false`, call `pixelPerfectCamera.CorrectCinemachineOrthoSize(normalOrthographicSize)` each frame to keep `m_CinemachineCompatibilityMode = true` (preventing `PixelPerfectCamera.OnBeginCameraRendering` from overwriting `m_Camera.orthographicSize`), and step using `1f - Mathf.Exp(-activeZoomSpeed * Time.unscaledDeltaTime)` when not pause-frozen. When settled within `0.002f` of `restOrthoSize` with idle target, snap to `restOrthoSize` and re-enable `cinemachinePixelPerfect.enabled = true`.
  - **Unscaled Brain/Impulses & Directional Shake (`CinemachineCameraShake2D` + `PlayerImpactFeedback`)**:
    - Set `CinemachineBrain.IgnoreTimeScale = true` in `GameScene.unity` and `CinemachineImpulseManager.Instance.IgnoreTimeScale = true` once in `CinemachineCameraShake2D.Awake()` (constant monotonic `Time.realtimeSinceStartup` clock—never toggled per frame). On `UIManager.OnPopupStackChanged`, if `UIManager.Instance.ShouldFreezeTime`, call `CinemachineImpulseManager.Instance.Clear()` and freeze `CinemachineCameraZoom2D` updates.
    - `tier1Shake = 0.055f`, `tier2Shake = 0.12f`, `tier3Shake = 0.26f`.
    - `redirectShake = 0.05f` triggered **only** by `PlayerMovement.OnWedgeRedirected(incoming, outgoing)`, pulsed along bisector `(outgoing - incoming).normalized` and attenuated by `Mathf.Pow(0.85f, consecutiveRedirects)` (max `10` steps).
    - `crackedBreakShake = 0.14f` + `crackedBreakHitStop = 0.045f` (`~3` unscaled frames on `CrackedBlock.OnAnyBroken` so mid-flight shatters punch without stopping velocity).
    - Keep `tier1HitStop = 0f`, `tier2HitStop = 0.033f`, `tier3HitStop = 0.083f`.
  - **Unscaled Trail/Sparks & Re-Entrant Hit-Stop Guard**:
    - Update `PlayerLineTrail.LateUpdate()` to use `float dt = IsGameplayPaused() ? 0f : Time.unscaledDeltaTime;` so ribbon shrinking/fading remains alive across `33–83 ms` Hit-Stop while pausing cleanly under UI modals.
    - Enable `useUnscaledTime: 1` on `WallImpactBurst.prefab` and `WallImpactBreak.prefab` (and track unscaled elapsed start-delay checks in `PooledParticlePlayer.MonitorParticlesRoutine`) so impact sparks burst outward during the sprite freeze.
    - In `PlayerImpactFeedback`, track `isHitStopActive` and `hitStopEndUnscaledTime` so overlapping Hit-Stops extend cleanly without storing `preHitStopTimeScale = 0f`. Both upon `HitStopRoutine` completion and in `OnDisable()` / `OnPopupStackChanged`, check `bool paused = UIManager.Instance != null && UIManager.Instance.ShouldFreezeTime;` and assign `Time.timeScale = paused ? 0f : 1f;` (never blindly restoring `1f` if a freeze-time popup opened mid-stop).
  - **`PlayerSquashStretch` (Pivot-Compensated + Clean Redirect Sequence)**:
    - Raise `maxDeformation` in `GameScene.unity` from `0.30` to `0.45` so `16/28` (`-0.429`) reaches full impact squash.
    - Compress in `duration * 0.18f` (`Ease.OutExpo`), rebound in `duration * 0.82f` (`Ease.OutBack`) using `.DOKill().SetUpdate(true).SetLink(gameObject)`.
    - **Pivot-flush compensation**: Cache `baseLocalPosition` (`(0, -0.5, 0)`) and derive rest sprite bounds from `spriteRenderer.sprite.bounds`; for non-zero impact axis `d`, keep the leading face pinned flush (`edge = d > 0 ? bounds.max : bounds.min`, `shift = edge * (baseScale - deformedScale) + d * (wallPressPx / 32f)` added to `baseLocalPosition`).
    - Maintain subtle in-flight elongation (`Cruise`: `+0.08` along / `-0.04` across; `KineticRam`: `+0.15` / `-0.07`).
    - On `OnWedgeRedirected(incoming, outgoing)`, play a `0.09s` sequence: `0.03s` corner compression (`-0.14` along `incoming`, `+0.10` across `incoming`, `Ease.OutQuad`) followed by `0.06s` launch stretch along `outgoing` into active tier elongation. Skip `HandleMoveStarted` whenever `playerMovement.IsRedirectMoveStart` is true so `Redirect()`'s same-frame `OnMoveStarted` never kills the `0.09s` sequence on frame 0.
- **Exact C# integration hooks**:
  - `PlayerMovement.cs`: add `public event Action<Vector2, Vector2> OnWedgeRedirected;`, `public bool IsRedirectMoveStart { get; private set; }`, and `public bool IsStartingPhaseLaunch { get; private set; }`. In `Redirect(Vector2 newDirection)`, capture `Vector2 incoming = lastMoveDirection;` before assigning `lastMoveDirection = moveDirection`, invoke `OnWedgeRedirected?.Invoke(incoming, moveDirection)`, keep `OnRedirected` intact, and bracket `OnMoveStarted?.Invoke(moveDirection)` with `IsRedirectMoveStart = true / false` so `LevelFlowController.movesUsedThisRun` retains parity while squash/audio skip redundant work. In `StartPhaseLaunch()`, bracket `EnforceMinimumTier(minTier)` with `IsStartingPhaseLaunch = true / false` and invoke `OnRedirected` (NOT `OnWedgeRedirected`).
  - `UIManager.cs`: expose `public bool ShouldFreezeTime` querying `popupStack` via `GetFreezeTime` (matching `UpdateTimeScale()`).
  - `CinemachineCameraShake2D.cs`, `CinemachineCameraZoom2D.cs`, `PlayerLineTrail.cs`, `PooledParticlePlayer.cs`, `PlayerSquashStretch.cs`, `PlayerImpactFeedback.cs`.
- **Unity Inspector / Prefab wiring notes**:
  - In `GameScene.unity`: wire `CinemachineCameraZoom2D` fields (`cinemachineCamera`, `cinemachinePixelPerfect`, `pixelPerfectCamera`); set `Main Camera` `CinemachineBrain.IgnoreTimeScale = true`; set `PlayerImpactFeedback` `tier1Shake = 0.055`, `tier2Shake = 0.12`, `tier3Shake = 0.26`, `redirectShake = 0.05`, and `PlayerSquashStretch.maxDeformation = 0.45`. Set `useUnscaledTime = true` on `WallImpactBurst.prefab` and `WallImpactBreak.prefab`.

## 3. Cross-System Safeguards (Edge Cases Resolved)

1. **Hit-Stop vs. Pause Freeze Race & Re-Entrancy**: `PlayerImpactFeedback` tracks `isHitStopActive` and `hitStopEndUnscaledTime` so overlapping impacts extend freezes without capturing `preHitStopTimeScale = 0f`. Upon `HitStopRoutine` completion, in `OnDisable()`, and on `UIManager.OnPopupStackChanged`, `Time.timeScale` always queries `UIManager.Instance.ShouldFreezeTime` (`0f` if paused, `1f` otherwise).
2. **Pixel-Perfect Snapping vs. Dynamic Zoom & Unscaled Shakes**: `CinemachinePixelPerfect` stays enabled at rest (`restOrthoSize`) and turns off only while zoomed (`<= 0.002f` hysteresis), preserving `m_CinemachineCompatibilityMode = true` on `PixelPerfectCamera` every zoomed frame. `CinemachineBrain.IgnoreTimeScale` and `CinemachineImpulseManager.Instance.IgnoreTimeScale` remain `true` constantly, and pause popups clear active impulses.
3. **Event Separation (`Redirect` vs. `StartPhaseLaunch` vs. `OnMoveStarted`)**: `OnWedgeRedirected`, `IsRedirectMoveStart`, and `IsStartingPhaseLaunch` prevent corner squash, audio overlap, combo inflation, and duplicate `Wedge.prefab` shakes, while `KineticPostProcessFeedback.ReconcilePostFxState()` ensures `Phasing -> KineticRam` landings transition smoothly without wiping dash post-FX.
4. **Cutscene Ownership & Cross-Level Reset**: `GoalRoutine` locks and kills `PlayerSquashStretch` before scale-to-zero suction, and `LevelFlowController.RespawnPlayer()` restores scale/rotation/position every level load or retry inside persistent `GameScene`.
5. **Collider Protection & Lifecycle Cleanup**: `LabTileFeedback.Awake()` rejects any `visualRoot` equal to root or containing `Collider2D`; `KineticPostProcessFeedback`, `CinemachineCameraZoom2D`, `PlayerKineticVisuals`, and `EntityAudio` clear active tweens/streaks/chevrons on state change or disable.
