# Gravita (Puzzle 2D) — Game Feel Signature Blueprint

## 0. Assumptions & Design Guardrails
- **Velocity Tier Thresholds**: `Assets/Scripts/Gameplay/VelocityTier.cs` currently maps `1–2` tiles to `Drift`, `3–4` to `Cruise`, and `5+` to `KineticRam` (whereas higher-level design notes cite `1–3` / `4–7` / `8+`). All feedback hooks below bind exclusively to `PlayerMovement.CurrentTier`, `PlayerMovement.LastImpactTier`, `OnVelocityTierChanged`, and `OnTileCrossed` — never hardcoded tile counts — so editing `VelocityTierUtil.FromTiles` automatically scales every audio, camera, and VFX threshold.
- **Audio Assets**: `Assets/Data/Sound/SoundConfig.asset` currently has `clips: []` on every entry. All code hooks assume clips are populated in `SoundConfig.asset` (using placeholder/mastered SFX in `Assets/Audio/`).
- **Determinism & Architecture**: `ProjectSettings/TimeManager.asset` runs a discrete `Fixed Timestep = 0.02s` (`moveSpeed = 30` units/sec in `GameScene.unity` = `0.6` tiles per physics tick; `48` units/sec = `0.96` tiles/tick during `1.6x` Phase Launch), and `LevelClock` drives timed hazards deterministically. **Only `PlayerImpactFeedback` modifies `Time.timeScale`**, exposing `public static bool IsAnyHitStopActive { get; private set; }` so unscaled feedback never advances during a Pause Menu (`Time.timeScale == 0f && !IsAnyHitStopActive`). Zero `new GameObject(...)` or runtime `AddComponent<...>()` calls are used, and every DOTween tween/sequence invokes `.DOKill()` before creation, `.SetUpdate(true)`, and `.SetLink(gameObject)`.
- **Accessibility & Speedrun Guards**: Hit-stop, screen shake, chromatic spikes, and vignette flashes expose Inspector toggles (`hitStopEnabled`, `photosensitivitySafeMode`, shake/zoom multipliers) so "Speedrun Minimal" and "Photosensitivity Safe" modes degrade cleanly to non-flashing feedback.

## 1. Core Game Feel Diagnosis: Why Gravita Feels "Flat" Today

Inspecting the live scripts, `GameScene.unity`, and gameplay prefabs reveals four concrete sensory breaks causing a `30 u/s` high-speed slide puzzle to feel weightless:

1. **Mid-Slide Sensory Vacuum (`0.6` tiles per `0.02s` tick)**: `PlayerMovement.OnTileCrossed` has **zero subscribers** across the codebase. Between launch and wall stop, there is no rising rhythmic cadence or escalation outside `PlayerLineTrail` (which clamps `smoothedMoveSpeed` to `28f` at `PlayerLineTrail.cs:292`, below `30f` normal and `48f` Phase Launch speeds, and aborts `LateUpdate` whenever `Time.deltaTime <= 0f` at `line 254`).
2. **Squash Timing & Clipping Mismatch During Hit-Stop**: `PlayerSquashStretch` already chains `.SetUpdate(true)` and `.SetLink(gameObject)` (`lines 182–186, 209–215`), yet impacts still look stiff and disconnected during Hit-Stop because: (a) `GameScene.unity:37697` caps `maxDeformation = 0.3`, clipping the authored `28x16` (`+40% / -43%`) impact ratio; (b) `Play()` eases into squash over `squashDuration * 0.4f = 0.088s` (`Ease.OutQuad`), which is slower than `tier2HitStop` (`0.033s`) and `tier3HitStop` (`0.083s`), so time resumes before full compression lands; (c) `HandleTierChanged` (`line 123`) calls `Play()`, bouncing back to `baseScale` mid-slide instead of holding aerodynamic stretch; and (d) the `Sprite` child sits at `localPosition (0, -0.5, 0)` with bottom-center `(0.5, 0)` pivot without anchor-preserving Y compensation on ceiling or horizontal deformations.
3. **Zeroed, Unaligned, and Stale-Tier Core Interactions**:
   - `GameScene.unity` sets `PlayerImpactFeedback.tier1Shake = 0` and `redirectShake = 0` (`lines 37710, 37713`), leaving 1–2 tile hops and `DeflectionWedge` bounces without tactile kick.
   - `EntityAudio.HandlePlayerWallImpact` (`EntityAudio.cs:117`) reads `playerMovement.LastImpactTier` inside `OnWallImpact` — which `PlayerMovement` fires *before* `StopMoving()` latches `LastImpactTier` (`PlayerMovement.cs:400–401, 429–430, 769`) — causing wall-impact SFX to reflect the *previous* dash's stale tier. Meanwhile `GravityBlock` and `BrakeTile` halt via `HaltAt` (`wallImpact: false`), leaving `EntityAudio` silent on anchor stops.
   - `Brake.prefab`, `Wedge.prefab`, `Exit.prefab`, and `Hazard.prefab` have `InteractionEffectPlayer.effectPrefabs: []` and `defaultCameraShake: 0` (whereas `GravityBlock.prefab` already has an effect + `defaultCameraShake: 0.04`). `Cracked.prefab` wires `WallImpactBreak.prefab`, but `alignToInteractionDirection = 0` and `WallImpactBreak`'s `ShapeModule` cone emits along `+Z`, while `PlayerImpactFeedback.HandleBlockBroken` early-returns on `block.HasCustomBreakEffect` (`line 128`). `StarCollectFeedback.collectibleParticlePrefab` is `null`.
4. **Dormant Signature Systems**: `CinemachineCameraZoom2D` (`normalOrthographicSize = 5.5` in `GameScene.unity:35168`) has **zero callers** and lerps on `Time.deltaTime`; `CloseCallDetector.OnCloseCallTriggered` has **zero subscribers** (and reuses `Particle Star.prefab`); `Assets/Volume/Global Volume Profile.asset` is completely static; `PlayerMovement.HasPendingDirection` has no telegraph; and `PowerUpController` builds VFX with runtime `new GameObject` + `AddComponent<SpriteRenderer>`.

## 2. Five Gravita-Specific Game Feel Pillars

### Pillar 1: Kinetic Runway Escalation (Mid-Slide Momentum)

#### Why It Makes Gravita Uniquely Addictive
In stop-to-stop inertia puzzles, players rarely plan 5–8 tile runs unless crossing distance *feels* noticeably different from a 2-tile hop. Turning each crossed grid cell into an ascending rhythmic pulse, holding a sleek aerodynamic stretch until stop, subtly widening camera FOV per tier, and shedding stark glowing afterimages at `KineticRam` gives long corridors and chained rebounds an unmistakable **"ignition rush"**.

#### Exact Specs, Curves & Inspector Parameters
- **Per-Tile Kinetic Tick (`OnTileCrossed`)**:
  - Suppressed while `movement.IsPhasing == true` (`VectorLauncher` already fires dedicated launch/land audio instead of `0.96` tiles/tick `48 u/s` spam).
  - Semitone pitch curve: `pitch = basePitch * Mathf.Pow(1.059463f, Mathf.Min(tilesTraveled - 1, maxSemitoneSteps))` with `basePitch = 0.88f`, `maxSemitoneSteps = 12` (caps at `1.76f`, +1 octave), and volume ramp `volumeScale = Mathf.Lerp(0.35f, 0.85f, Mathf.Clamp01(tilesTraveled / 8f))`.
  - **Discrete `0.02s` Fixed-Timestep & Frame-Jitter Protection**: At `moveSpeed = 30` and `Fixed Timestep = 0.02s`, `TrackDistance` moves `0.6` tiles/tick and crosses integers at `0.04s, 0.08s, 0.10s, 0.14s, 0.18s, 0.20s` (every 3rd crossing is `0.020s` after the previous crossing, and render-frame jitter can run consecutive `FixedUpdate` ticks `< 0.015s` apart in `Time.unscaledTime`). Therefore, per-tile ticks call `SoundManager.PlaySFX(..., bypassCooldown: true)` and coalesce only duplicate triggers within the **same physics tick** via `Mathf.Abs(Time.fixedUnscaledTime - lastTickFixedTime) < 0.015f` (preserving every `0.02s` step while blocking `0s` same-tick doubles from `EnforceMinimumTier` or catch-up substeps).
- **Camera Runway Pull-Out (`CinemachineCameraZoom2D`)**:
  - `Drift` (`baseOrtho = 5.50f`) -> `Cruise` (`5.68f`, `zoomSpeed = 6f`) -> `KineticRam` (`5.88f`, `zoomSpeed = 7f`) -> `Phasing` (`5.95f`).
  - Steps `baseSize` on `Time.unscaledDeltaTime` whenever `Time.timeScale > 0f || PlayerImpactFeedback.IsAnyHitStopActive` (stays frozen while paused), with additive `punchOffset` driven by DOTween `.SetUpdate(true)`.
- **Sustained Aerodynamic Stretch (`PlayerSquashStretch`)**:
  - Hold deformed scale **until `OnMoveStopped`** instead of bouncing back mid-slide: `Drift` (`+8%` along / `-4%` across, `0.05s`), `Cruise` (`+14%` / `-7%`, `0.06s`), `KineticRam` (`+20%` / `-10%`, `0.06s`), `Ease.OutQuad`, `.SetUpdate(true)`, using center-preserving Y pivot compensation (`targetAnchorY = 0.5f`).
- **Kinetic Ram & Phase Afterimages (`KineticAfterimage.prefab` + `PooledAfterimage.cs`)**:
  - Active only when `CurrentTier == VelocityTier.KineticRam || IsPhasing`.
  - Spawn interval: `0.032s` unscaled (~1 ghost/tile at `30 u/s`, pool cap `10`), ghost lifetime `0.16s` unscaled, alpha `flashColor.a -> 0f` (`Ease.OutQuad`), scale `1.0 -> 0.86` (`Ease.InQuad`).
  - `_FlashColor` (HDR on existing `Assets/Shaders/Hit/Hit Flash White.mat` / `HitFlashSprite.shader`): `KineticRam` = `#FF52C7` (`a = 0.62`), `Phasing` = `#B86BFF` (`a = 0.70`).
- **URP Volume Escalation (`Global Volume`)**:
  - `KineticRam`: `ChromaticAberration.intensity` `0.20f -> 0.40f` (`0.08s`), `Bloom.intensity` `9.0f -> 11.2f` (`0.08s`); eases back to baseline over `0.14s` on stop.
- **`PlayerLineTrail` Hit-Stop & Speed Clamp Fix**:
  - Raise `Mathf.Clamp(instSpeed, 4f, 28f)` (`PlayerLineTrail.cs:292`) to `Mathf.Clamp(instSpeed, 4f, 60f)` (`30 u/s` slide & `48 u/s` Phase Launch).
  - Replace `float dt = Time.deltaTime; if (dt <= 0f) return;` (`lines 253–255`) with `float dt = PlayerImpactFeedback.IsAnyHitStopActive ? Time.unscaledDeltaTime : Time.deltaTime; if (dt <= 0f) return;` so trails smoothly retract during Hit-Stop while remaining frozen in the Pause Menu.

### Pillar 2: Unscaled Elastic Impact & Magnetic Anchor Choreography

#### Why It Makes Gravita Uniquely Addictive
Because `bufferTurnsUntilStop = true` locks perpendicular steering mid-corridor, **every stop is a decision beat**. Fixing squash timing so full compression lands *inside* the Hit-Stop freeze — while giving short Drift hops a crisp tap, `CrackedBlock` smashes a forward directional break burst and zoom punch, and `GravityBlock`/`BrakeTile` stops a magnetic implosion — makes walls, breakable masonry, and gravity anchors immediately distinguishable by feel.

#### Exact Specs, Curves & Inspector Parameters
- **Snappy Unscaled 2-Stage Impact Squash (`PlayerSquashStretch`)**:
  - Set `maxDeformation = 0.45f` in `GameScene.unity` (unlocks full `28x16` deformation).
  - Stage 1 (`0.030s`, `Ease.OutExpo`, `.SetUpdate(true)`): Immediate snap into peak squash *inside* the Hit-Stop window (`Tier 1`: `60%` deform, `wallPressPx = 2.5f`; `Tier 2`: `85%`, `4.0f`; `Tier 3`: `100%`, `5.0f`).
  - Stage 2 (`0.145s`, `Ease.OutBack(1.35f)`, `.SetUpdate(true)`): Elastic spring back to `baseScale` / `baseLocalPosition`.
  - **General Anchor-Preserving Pivot Y Compensation**: Read `spriteWorldHeight = (sprite.rect.height / sprite.pixelsPerUnit) * baseScale.y` and `pivotNormY = sprite.pivot.y / sprite.rect.height` dynamically from `spriteRenderer.sprite` (handles `(0.5, 0)` bottom-pivot, `(0.5, 0.5)` center-pivot, and any equipped skin sprite). Given `sy = deformedScale.y / baseScale.y`, select `targetAnchorY`:
    - Upward wall impact (`pressDirection.y > 0.5f`): `targetAnchorY = 1f` (pins top edge to ceiling).
    - Downward wall impact (`pressDirection.y < -0.5f`): `targetAnchorY = 0f` (pins bottom edge to floor).
    - Horizontal impacts, mid-slide stretches, and center-anchored floor stops: `targetAnchorY = 0.5f` (pins visual center at `y = 0` for both `sy < 1` squash and `sy > 1` widen).
    - Offset: `localPos.y = baseLocalPosition.y + (targetAnchorY - pivotNormY) * spriteWorldHeight * (1f - sy) + pressDirection.y * (wallPressPx / 32f)`.
- **Tiered Trauma & Directional `CrackedBlock` Smash (`PlayerImpactFeedback`)**:
  - `Tier 1 (Drift)` Wall Stop: `tier1Shake = 0.045f` (up from `0`), `tier1HitStop = 0f`.
  - `Tier 2 (Cruise)` Wall Stop: `tier2Shake = 0.12f`, `tier2HitStop = 0.033f`, `PunchZoom(-0.10f, 0.14f)`.
  - `Tier 3 (KineticRam)` Solid Wall Stop: `tier3Shake = 0.24f`, `tier3HitStop = 0.067f`, `PunchZoom(-0.18f, 0.16f)`.
  - `CrackedBlock` Break (`CrackedBlock.OnAnyBroken`): `blockBreakShake = 0.28f`, `blockBreakHitStop = 0.055f`, `PunchZoom(-0.26f, 0.18f)`, `HapticEvent.KineticImpact` (`HeavyImpact`), plus directional 2D debris spray along impact vector (even when shattered from rest by `Breaker`).
- **Magnetic Anchor Lock (`GravityBlock` / `BrakeTile`, `WasLastStopWallImpact == false`)**:
  - **Implosion Pulse**: `0.04s` shrink to `(0.84, 0.84, 1)` (`Ease.OutQuad`, center-anchored `targetAnchorY = 0.5f`, `.SetUpdate(true)`), `0.12s` spring to `(1.06, 1.06, 1)` -> `(1, 1, 1)` (`Ease.OutBack`), `anchorShake = 0.05f`, zero hit-stop (`0f`), and `SoundConfig.gravityAnchor`.

### Pillar 3: Harmonic Bank-Shot & Corridor Arpeggio

#### Why It Makes Gravita Uniquely Addictive
Gravita shines when a player banks off 3 `DeflectionWedge` tiles through a row of coins and smashes a `CrackedBlock`. Currently, random `±8%` pitch variance on `wedgeRedirect` and `coinCollect` flattens combos into noise, `redirectShake = 0`, and `Wedge.prefab` spawns nothing. Replacing random variance with **deterministic major-pentatonic pitch chains that reset only when Subject G-0 stops** turns multi-wedge routes and coin corridors into tight musical phrases.

#### Exact Specs, Curves & Inspector Parameters
- **Wedge / VectorPad Harmonic Escalation (`OnRedirected`)**:
  - Major pentatonic semitones: `int[] { 0, 2, 4, 7, 9, 12, 14, 16 }` (`redirectChainCount` increments per `OnRedirected`, resets to `0` on `OnMoveStopped`).
  - Pitch: `Mathf.Pow(1.059463f, Pentatonic[Mathf.Min(chain, 7)])` (`1.00x -> 2.52x`); volume `Mathf.Min(1f, 0.72f + chain * 0.04f)`; `bypassCooldown = true`.
- **Corridor Coin Arpeggio (`Collectible.OnAnyCoinCollected`)**:
  - `coinChainCount` in `EntityAudio` (reset on `OnMoveStopped`), walking the same pentatonic array capped at `+12` semitones (`2.00x`), called with `bypassCooldown = true` so `30 u/s` 1-tile coin lines never drop a note on `0.02s` fixed steps.
- **Snap-Bank Visual & Camera Punch**:
  - `PlayerImpactFeedback.HandleRedirected`: `shake = redirectShake + 0.015f * Mathf.Min(redirectChainCount, 4)` (`0.07f -> 0.13f`).
  - `PlayerSquashStretch.SnapRedirect(Vector2 oldDir, Vector2 newDir)`: `0.09s` unscaled Z-tilt whip (`±9°` from `Mathf.Sign(oldDir.x * newDir.y - oldDir.y * newDir.x)`, returning to `0°` `Ease.OutBack`) + sustained `+14% / -7%` stretch along `newDir` with center-preserving Y offset (`targetAnchorY = 0.5f`).
- **Phase Launch (`VectorLauncher` / `OnPhasingChanged`)**:
  - Launch (`OnPhasingChanged(true)`): `PunchZoom(-0.22f, 0.12f)`, `ChromaticAberration` spike `0.20f -> 0.52f` over `0.10s`, `SoundConfig.phaseLaunchStart`.
  - Landing (`OnPhasingChanged(false)` when `wasPhasingInPlay && !movement.InputLocked`): `ShakeCamera(0.16f)` + `SoundConfig.phaseLaunchLand`.

#### Exact C# Integration Hooks
- **`PlayerSquashStretch.cs` (Safe `Redirect()` + `OnMoveStarted` Ordering)**:
  - `PlayerMovement.Redirect()` invokes `OnRedirected` (`line 997`) immediately followed by `OnMoveStarted` (`line 998`). To prevent `HandleMoveStarted` from killing `SnapRedirect` and stranding the sprite tilted:
    1. In `HandleRedirected(Vector2 newDir)`: if `movement != null && movement.IsPhasing` return (Phase Launch emits `OnRedirected` without `OnMoveStarted`); otherwise capture `Vector2 oldDir = lastDirection; lastDirection = newDir; suppressNextMoveStartStretch = true;` and call `SnapRedirect(oldDir, newDir)`.
    2. In `HandleMoveStarted(Vector2 dir)`: if `suppressNextMoveStartStretch` { `suppressNextMoveStartStretch = false; lastDirection = dir; return;` }. Also clear `suppressNextMoveStartStretch = false;` in `HandleMoveStopped()`, `HandlePhasingChanged(true)`, and `OnDisable()`.
    3. Drive the Z-tilt inside `SnapRedirect(Vector2 oldDir, Vector2 newDir)` via an isolated `tiltTween = DOVirtual.Float(startAngle, 0f, 0.09f, a => { if (visualTransform != null) visualTransform.localRotation = Quaternion.Euler(0f, 0f, a); }).SetTarget(this).SetEase(Ease.OutBack).SetUpdate(true).SetLink(gameObject)` (preceded by `tiltTween?.Kill(); DOTween.Kill(this);`) so subsequent `visualTransform.DOKill()` calls on `OnVelocityTierChanged` never kill the Z-whip. ### Pillar 5: Input Anticipation & Lab World Tactility

#### Why It Makes Gravita Uniquely Addictive
At `30 u/s` with `bufferTurnsUntilStop = true`, players buffer their *next* perpendicular turn mid-slide and weave through `GravityBlock` anchors, `CircuitSwitch` gates, and `PowerUpController` skills (`Breaker`, `Freeze`, `Teleport`). Telegraphing buffered turns *strictly during mid-slide*, lighting up `AwaitingFreshInput` anchor locks, and replacing `PowerUpController`'s runtime `new GameObject` VFX with crisp pooled prefabs elevates both control clarity and sci-fi test-chamber feel.

#### Exact Specs, Curves & Inspector Parameters
- **Mid-Slide Buffered Turn Telegraph (`PendingTurnChevron`)**:
  - Pre-wired child `PendingTurnChevron` (`SpriteRenderer` offset `0.58` units outward, inactive by default) activates **exclusively during genuine mid-slide perpendicular buffering**: `movement.State == MovementState.Sliding && movement.BufferTurnsUntilStop && dir != Vector2.zero && dir != movement.CurrentDirection && dir != -movement.CurrentDirection`.
  - Stationary launches, wall-blocked idle bumps, mid-slide reversals (`dir == -CurrentDirection`), and repeated swipes in the already-buffered direction are **explicitly ignored**.
  - Rotates to `dir`, pops scale `0.5 -> 1.1 -> 0.95` over `0.08s` (`Ease.OutBack`, `.SetUpdate(true)`), and plays soft `SoundConfig.turnBufferClick` (`volume = 0.30f`, `randomizePitch = false`). **No haptic pulse is fired on turn buffering** so `GameHaptics.GlobalMinGapSeconds` (`0.03s`) never suppresses imminent `WallImpact`, `KineticImpact`, or `PadRedirect` haptics `10–25ms` later.
- **`AwaitingFreshInput` Anchor Lock Cue (`AnchorLockRing`)**:
  - While `movement.IsAwaitingFreshInput == true`, pre-wired child `AnchorLockRing` pulses (`alpha 0.35 <-> 0.85`, `scale 0.95 <-> 1.08`, period `0.65s` unscaled loop). Hides immediately on `OnMoveStarted` / `OnDisable`.
